ART DIRECTION
ART DIRECTION (decide these before anything else; the system and pages inherit them)

Design thesis

State the site's design thesis in ONE sentence: what this site does visually that no other contractor site in this trade does.
List THREE specific things this site will do differently (for example: "asymmetric hero with offset type over a full-bleed roof photo", "process timeline drawn as a ridge-line divider", "service list set as a ruled price-guide table"). Reject the first idea; generate three alternatives internally and pick the most distinctive that still converts.
Mood board in words

Name THREE reference moods (never named brands or URLs): for example "industrial print shop", "mid-century architectural digest", "vintage national park poster". Each mood must be describable in 8-12 words and tied to the trade and the owner's brand.
Type pairing rationale

Choose heading and body fonts from the allow-list with a stated reason tied to the trade: "condensed grotesk display + humanist body for a roofer because the condensed face echoes shingle courses and the humanist body reads well at 18px on a phone". Never pick Inter-only. Never use the same family for heading and body.
Define the modular scale ratio: 1.2 for mobile-first, 1.333 for desktop-heavy. All sizes via clamp().
Display headings: line-height 1.0-1.15, letter-spacing -0.02em to -0.04em. Body: 17-19px, line-height 1.55-1.7, measure 55-72ch. Max 5 distinct font sizes per page outside the scale.
Grid and spacing logic

Declare the grid: 12-column at ≥1200px, 8-column at ≥900px, 4-column at ≥640px, single below. Gutter 16-24px. Margin matches gutter.
Spacing scale: 4/8 base. Section padding varies: tight 56-80px, normal 96-120px, loose 128-160px (all via clamp). No two adjacent sections share the same padding value.
Vertical rhythm: every section's top margin is a multiple of the base unit. Proximity groups related elements (8-16px gap); unrelated elements get 32-48px.
Image treatment spec

Hero: at least 60% of the first screen on mobile, or a deliberate type-only hero with a signature device. Aspect ratio 16:9 or 3:2 landscape. object-position keeps the subject visible.
Secondary/work photos: consistent aspect ratio per context (4:5 portrait for cards, 1:1 for gallery, 16:9 for feature bands). Consistent frame treatment from the shape language (radius, border, offset shadow, or none).
Optional tint/duotone via CSS blend modes to unify uneven photos. Captions only from FACTS. Never stretch.
When no photo exists for a slot, design the section without one (typography, numbers from FACTS, inline SVG pattern). Never leave a blank box.
Section-transition style

Define how sections connect: hard edge, hairline rule, diagonal clip-path (CSS only), overlapping element, colour-block shift, or whitespace breath. Use at least two transition types across the site. Never repeat the same transition more than twice in a row.
Texture and pattern

One texture maximum: paper grain, ruled lines, blueprint grid, hatching, halftone dots, or topo lines. Implemented via CSS gradients or inline SVG data images only. No glassmorphism, no blobs, no neon glow.
Iconography style

Custom inline SVG with one stroke width (1.5-2px) and one cap style (round or square). Or typographic marks and numerals. Never emoji. Never identical icon-in-a-tinted-square rows.
Button personality

Primary: solid fill, action colour, white text, min-height 48px, padding 12-16px vertical / 24-32px horizontal, radius from shape language, visible focus ring (2px offset, 3:1 contrast), pressed state (scale 0.97). Secondary: outline or ghost. One primary style, one secondary. Never gradient buttons.
Motion personality

One motion vocabulary: one hero entrance (transform + opacity, under 600ms, ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1), once), OR underline draw, OR accordion grid-template-rows. Always content-visible without JS. Always respects prefers-reduced-motion (no transforms, opacity-only or none).
Signature device

One memorable motif tied to the trade: ridge-line divider (roofing), pipe-bend connector (plumbing), blueprint grid mark (remodeling), airflow curve (HVAC), circuit trace (electrical), turf stripe (landscaping), paint-swatch fan (painting), tape-measure rule (general), stone-course line (masonry). Used in hero, section headings and CTA at consistent scale. Defined as a reusable CSS class or inline SVG.

PAGE COMPOSITION (plan before writing markup)

Blueprint required for every page kind

Ordered sections, each with: purpose, layout family (from the component gallery), density (tight/normal/loose), background/theme (paper/surface/primary/accent/dark), focal element, image slot (key from IMAGES or "none"), copy intent (what the visitor learns or feels), CTA (primary/secondary/none), transition into the next section.
Hard composition rules

No two adjacent sections share the same layout family.
Every long page (home, service, area, about) has at least: one full-bleed moment, one asymmetric section, one dense/data section, one quiet breathing section.
Every long page has its own ONE "signature moment" (marked data-sw-signature) using the concept's signature device; neighbouring pages use different kinds of moment.
Pages of the same kind (the service pages, the area pages) never share one skeleton: different section order, different opening, different signature moment.
Home page hero occupies at least 60% of the first phone screen, or is a deliberate type-only hero with the signature device.
Inner pages (service, area, about, FAQ, contact, blog, 404, thanks) receive the SAME ambition as the home page. About pages are human. Contact is frictionless.
Density map

Alternate loud → quiet → dense → breath down the page. A page with five sections of equal density fails.
Front-load the most important content. Progressive disclosure (details/summary) for secondary detail.
Focal points

One focal point per screen. Test by squinting: is there a clear hierarchy? Cover the logo: could this be any other contractor? If yes, the page is not distinctive enough.
Narrative arc per page kind

Home: promise → proof → services (designed, not a grid) → process → towns → people → questions → ask.
Service: problem → who it's for → step-by-step process → what affects cost → towns → related services → questions → ask.
Area: local angle (from FACTS) → services available → why this business serves this town → neighbouring areas → ask.
About: person behind the business → how they work → proof (rating, photos) → towns → ask.
Contact: form (main event) → phone (large tap-to-call) → what happens next → hours → areas → map.
Gallery: masonry or rhythm of large/small frames → lightbox → captions from FACTS → ask.
FAQ: grouped questions → honest answers → ask.
Blog index: designed cards (different from service cards) → ask.

DESIGN RULEBOOK
DESIGN RULES (this is what separates a site that sells from one that merely exists)

Above the fold, on a phone, with no scrolling, the visitor must see: what the business does and where, one clear promise, a tap-to-call or quote button, and one trust cue (a real rating, a real photo of work). A visitor decides in about three seconds.

Typography

Two families at most, chosen for THIS business (the concept names them). Heading and body must differ in character, not just weight.
Body text 17-19px on phones and desktop, line-height 1.55-1.7, measure 55-75 characters. Never grey-on-white below 4.5:1 contrast (7:1 for small text on photos).
A real type scale (for example 1.25 ratio) with fluid sizes via clamp(). Headings tight (line-height 1.05-1.2), large, and confident; the h1 is the biggest thing on the page.
Numbers (phone, years, ratings) set with tabular figures. Uppercase labels need letter-spacing 0.06-0.12em.
Layout and rhythm

Build on a 4/8px spacing scale. Section padding varies with purpose: do NOT repeat "centered heading, subtitle, three equal cards" down the page. Alternate density, alignment and background so the page has a pulse: a loud section, a quiet one, a dense one.
Use asymmetry on purpose: offset images, overlapping elements, a column that breaks the grid, a full-bleed band, a sticky side panel. Use a strong, visible grid system rather than everything centered.
Photography carries the page. Real photos from the images list, large, with a consistent treatment (aspect ratios, corners, borders or frames from the concept's shape language). Never stretch; use object-fit: cover with sensible object-position. Never use an image as a pure decoration when a photo of the work would prove something.
No stock-photo clichés. If a slot has no photo, design the section so it does not need one (typography, numbers from FACTS, an illustrated shape made with CSS or inline SVG) instead of a blank box.
Cards: vary them. A service list may be a ruled list, an index with big numerals, an image-led strip or a grid, whatever fits the concept. Do not use the same card everywhere.
Icons: draw simple inline SVG that fits the shape language, or use typographic marks and numerals. Never emoji. Never a row of identical icon-in-a-tinted-square features.
Colour

Use the concept's palette as CSS variables only. 60-30-10: a dominant paper or ink, a supporting surface, one action colour reserved for buttons and key links. One bold moment per screen.
Every text/background pair passes WCAG AA (4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large). Check the pairs you create, including text over photos (use a gradient scrim or a solid panel, not luck).
No purple-to-blue gradient hero, no gradient text, no neon glow, no glassmorphism blur panels, no floating blurred blobs, no rainbow accents. Flat colour, real texture (subtle grain via CSS, rules, patterns) and strong contrast read as craft.
Shape and detail

Follow the concept's shape language everywhere: radii, borders, dividers, buttons, image frames. Consistency is what makes a site feel designed.
Shadows are rare and purposeful (one elevation level at most). Prefer borders, offsets and colour blocks.
Buttons: at least 48px tall, clear verb labels ("Call (813) 555-0123", "Get my free estimate"), a visible focus ring, a pressed state. One primary style, one secondary.
Add one signature device from the concept (a divider, a stamp, a numbered process line, a drawn roofline, a measurement ruler motif) and repeat it with restraint so the site is recognisable.
Motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Motion comes only from the effect hooks (EFFECTS CATALOGUE) plus CSS for their states, within the MOTION BUDGET: one intro, page transitions, one or two scroll stories, free micro-interactions. No autoplaying carousels, no scroll-jacking, no layout shift, no content hidden until motion runs.
Mobile first

Design the 390px layout first, then enhance. Tap targets at least 48px, 8px apart. No horizontal scroll at any width from 320px to 1920px. Images never overflow. Navigation collapses to a menu button with a visible phone button beside it.
A sticky bottom bar on phones with the call button and the quote button (the design system provides it).

DESIGN DOCTRINE
DESIGN DOCTRINE (what the best designers do instead)

Composition

One focal point on the first screen; the hero photo or colour field covers at least half of it. Off-centre beats centred.
Hero headline about 5 words; any headline at most 2 lines. Every section has a heading and ONE job.
Neighbouring sections use different compositions; avoid the stock order (hero, features, testimonials, CTA).
The gap between sections is clearly larger than gaps inside a section. Equal outer margins. No blank gap taller than half a screen.
Removing ornament is not enough: fill space with scale, real content, a real photo or a flat colour field.
Under 72% of text centred; never centre a paragraph longer than 3 lines; never justify.
Type

At most 2 families, at most 5 sizes, far apart on a ratio scale. Weight and colour carry hierarchy as well as size.
Never Inter, Roboto, Arial or system-ui as the display face.
Display: tracking -0.02 to -0.05em, line-height 0.85-1.05 (1.15 max). Body 16-20px, line-height 1.45-1.6, 45-75 characters per line, full ink (not grey).
text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on paragraphs; tabular-nums for prices, phones and figures.
All caps only for 3 words or fewer, tracking 0.02-0.06em. Nothing under 13px.
Real characters: curly quotes, … ellipsis, en dash for ranges (8–10am),   between a number and its unit.
Colour and surface

One ground, one ink, one accent (about 60/30/10). The accent marks the action. Colours come from the trade's world.
Neutrals tinted toward the brand hue; avoid pure #000 text on pure #fff.
Separate surfaces with a tone step, not lines or shadows. Shadows only on things that float.
Corners: square or fully round, chosen once; never 12-16px radius on everything. Nested radius is concentric (outer = inner + padding).
Motion

One signature system per site (a motif carried through hero, scroll story, cards and footer). Animate only transform, opacity and clip-path; never "transition: all".
Ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1); ease-in-out cubic-bezier(0.77,0,0.175,1); never ease-in for UI. UI motion under 300ms.
Press feedback scale(0.97); never animate from scale(0). Hover effects inside @media (hover:hover).
prefers-reduced-motion removes movement. Content is never hidden waiting for a reveal.
Craft

Every interactive element has hover, active, focus-visible and disabled states; hit areas at least 44px.
Nav: wordmark, 3-5 links, one action. The footer is a designed close, not a link dump.
Buttons name the action in 1-3 words ("Call now", "Get my quote"); the primary action keeps one name everywhere.
Phone numbers are tel: links; inputs have real labels, the right type/autocomplete and 16px+ text.
Images carry width/height or aspect-ratio; hero image eager, others lazy.
Process

Before writing, name the direction (purpose, tone, the one memorable thing) and fix the tokens first.
After writing, review your own output against AI-SLOP TELLS and the brief; rewrite anything generic before answering.

QUALITY RUBRIC (score each 0-10; publish only when weighted score ≥8 AND every category meets its floor)

First-screen clarity and conversion (phone) (weight 14%, floor 7): Within 3 seconds on a phone, without scrolling: what the business does, where, why them, what to do next. Primary CTA visible. One trust cue.
Visual distinctiveness (cover-the-logo test) (weight 12%, floor 7): With the logo covered, could this be any other contractor site? Signature device present and consistent. Not generic.
Typography and hierarchy (weight 10%, floor 7): Type pairing has a stated rationale. Scale is fluid. Heading/body contrast is structural, not just weight. Measure 55-72ch. Line heights correct.
Layout rhythm and composition (weight 10%, floor 7): No two adjacent sections share layout family. Density alternates. At least one full-bleed, one asymmetric, one dense, one breathing section per long page. A dark or primary section and an image break on every long page. Pages of the same kind do not share one skeleton.
Photography use and art direction (weight 8%, floor 6): Photos are art-directed: consistent aspect ratios, framing, crop to subject. No stretching. Captions from FACTS. Sections without photos are designed to not need them.
Copy specificity and truthfulness (weight 8%, floor 7): Every claim supported by FACTS. No banned phrases. Specific beats general. Voice matches the strategy. No filler.
Mobile usability and responsiveness (weight 7%, floor 8): Tap targets ≥48px, 8px gaps. No horizontal scroll 320-1920px. Sticky bar doesn't cover content. Navigation works. Thumb-zone CTAs.
Accessibility and technical quality (weight 7%, floor 8): WCAG AA contrast. Visible focus. Semantic HTML. Heading order. Alt text. Reduced motion respected. No layout shift. LCP candidate marked.
Motion and interaction (weight 12%, floor 7): Motion has a purpose and stays inside the motion budget: one intro moment, page transitions, one or two scroll stories, varied reveals, ease-out curves, no bounce, no autoplay carousel, no popup on load. Hover/focus feedback on every control. Everything readable with reduced motion.
Wow moment (weight 8%, floor 7): Each page has one moment a visitor would screenshot or show someone, built from the signature device (a pinned story, a before/after slider, a type reveal, a video band). It serves the message and never hides content or the call to action.
Polish (states, details, consistency) (weight 4%, floor 6): Button states complete. Link underlines styled. Selection colour. Radius system consistent. No orphan styles. Print styles exist.
