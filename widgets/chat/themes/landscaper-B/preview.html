We map the zones before a shovel hits soil

Most yards fail because the plan ignores the caliche, the shade line and the watering day. We walk the site first, then build from the sketch.

  1. 01

    Site walk and a pencil sketch

    Slope, sun hours, the heads you already have and the corner the hose never reaches.

  2. 02

    Soil test and a plant list

    Break through caliche where needed, pick natives that match the light and the deer pressure.

  3. 03

    Drip lines, valves labelled

    Every zone mapped, every emitter at a root, the controller set to your address day.

  4. 04

    Hand-off with a zone card

    A laminated card in the garage, so the next person knows which valve runs what.
