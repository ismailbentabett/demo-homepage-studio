01–05 · process

Five sheets between your idea and your skin.

Each sheet is laid over the last one. The drawing under it gets sharper every time, and you see every version.

05 · healed

  1. 01 · idea

    Your screenshot, your words.

    We talk size, place and style and sketch while you watch. Rough is fine: a photo, a pen mark, a handwritten note.

    rough is fine

  2. 02 · drawing

    Redrawn from scratch on vellum.

    Clean lines, sized to your arm, shown to you before anything is booked. We redraw until it looks like yours.

    redraw #3: smaller leaf

  3. 03 · stencil

    Placed on you as a stencil.

    The drawing goes on in violet. You stand at the mirror and we move it until it follows your arm, not the paper.

    move it ¼ in. lower

  4. 04 · lines

    Lines, in the sittings we planned.

    Single needle or soft shading, done at the pace we agreed at the consult. Nothing new gets added on the day.

    single needle, 2 sittings

  5. 05 · healed

    Healed with you, checked at six weeks.

    We look again once it has settled, photograph it and fill any thin spot. That visit is part of the plan.

    6 weeks: photo + notes

Studio rules

Clean you can watch.

Nothing happens behind your back. Every rule below happens at the chair, in front of you.

Rules · at the chair
  1. 1

    Needles and cartridges are single-use, opened in front of you.

  2. 2

    Fresh wrap on the chair, the lamp and the bottles for every client.

  3. 3

    Gloves change every time they touch anything that isn't you.

  4. 4

    Ears are pierced with a needle, never a gun, with titanium jewelry.

Day one to week six, on one line.

What a fresh tattoo does while it heals, and when you come back so we can see it settled.

Day 1–42 · aftercare
  1. Day 1

    The film stays on. Leave it alone.

  2. Day 3

    Film off, a gentle wash, a thin layer of balm.

  3. Day 7

    Flaking starts. Itchy is normal; picking isn't.

  4. Day 14

    Still no pools, no ocean, no long soaks.

  5. Day 30

    The skin looks settled and the shine is gone.

  6. Day 42

    Come in for your six-week check.
