02 · KS

Why soft brick needs soft mortar

Six moments in the life of a St. Louis wall, from river clay to a lime repoint.

  1. 01

    Clay dug from the river yards

    St. Louis red brick starts as local clay, fired at lower heat than modern brick. That makes it soft and porous.

  2. 02

    Mixed lime, not Portland

    Historic mortar was lime and sand. It breathes with the brick and closes its own hairline cracks.

  3. 03

    Laid by hand, course by course

    Soft brick walls were laid in common or running bond, with joints tooled concave to shed water.

  4. 04

    Hard cement patch, 1970s

    A later repair used Portland cement. It is harder than the brick, so the face spalls off in freeze-thaw cycles.

  5. 05

    Rake out, match, repack

    We grind or rake the bad joint to ¾ inch, mix lime putty to match the original sand, and pack it in lifts.

  6. 06

    The wall breathes again

    Lime mortar cures slowly and moves with the brick. The repair lasts because it is softer, not stronger.
