  1. 1

Healing

Salt water and sun, day by day.

Sand, salt water and sun each change your aftercare. Here's the plain version.

  1. Day 1
  2. Day 3
  3. Day 7
  4. Day 14
  5. Day 21 · ocean

A new tattoo

Day 1
Keep the film on as long as we told you. Wash with clean hands, pat dry, thin layer of balm.
Day 3
It feels tight and warm. Loose sleeves, no sand, no pool, no hot tub.
Day 7
Flaking starts. Don't pick it, don't scratch it. Keep it in the shade.
Day 14
Most of the peeling is done. Still no soaking: no ocean, no bath.
Day 21
Closed, matte and no shine? Back in the ocean. Sunscreen on it every beach day after.

Lobe piercing

Day 1
Hands off. Saline spray morning and night, then let it air dry.
Day 3
A little tender is normal. Sleep on the other side.
Day 7
Keep the stud in. No twisting, no swapping yet.
Day 14
Still no ocean or pool. Hair products and sunscreen stay off it.
Day 21
Calm and dry? Ask us at a check-in before the first swim.

Cartilage (helix, conch)

Day 1
Saline twice a day. Don't touch it with hands, hats or headphones.
Day 3
Swelling is normal. The longer bar we fitted gives it room.
Day 7
Sleep on the other side or on a travel pillow.
Day 14
Cartilage takes months, not weeks. Still no ocean.
Day 21
Come back so we can swap to a shorter bar when it's ready.

Nostril

Day 1
Saline outside only. Leave the inside alone.
Day 3
Careful with towels, masks and sweaters.
Day 7
A small bump? Tell us before you try home fixes.
Day 14
No makeup or sunscreen on the spot yet. No ocean.
Day 21
Ask us before the first swim; we'll take a look.
