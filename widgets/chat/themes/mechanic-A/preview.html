  1. 01 You

    Drop it off in the morning

    Leave the keys at the counter and tell us what the car is doing, in your words. The noise, when it happens, what changed.

  2. 02 Us

    We find the cause

    We read the code, then test the part it points to until we know. Diagnostic check $95–$160 Sample pricing, applied to the repair if you go ahead.

  3. 03 Us

    You see the problem

    We text a photo of the part, what it needs, and the price. If something can wait, we say so and leave it alone.

  4. 04

    Your yes

    Nothing moves until you answer

    Say yes and the repair line opens. Say no and you drive away with the diagnosis and the photos. Either way, it is your call.

    Lines 05 and 06 open below this line.

  5. 05 Us

    Repair, then a road test

    We fix what you said yes to, and only that. Then we drive it the way you do, until the noise or the light is gone.

  6. 06 You

    Pick it up, every line ticked

    Keys at the counter, the order in your hand, and the photos still on your phone for the next time someone asks what was done.
