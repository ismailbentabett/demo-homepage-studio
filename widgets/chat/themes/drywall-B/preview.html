GA-214 Levels of Finish

Six levels. We tell you which one you’re paying for.

How we check: a work light on the floor, 8° to the wall.

  1. L0L1L2L3L4L5

    L0–L2 Taped, one coat

    Board up, tape in, a coat over it. Fine behind tile or in an attic. Never in a room you live in.

  2. L0L1L2L3L4L5

    L3 Two coats

    Right under heavy knockdown or skip trowel. The texture hides what is left of the seams.

  3. L0L1L2L3L4L5

    L4 Three coats, sanded

    The normal home finish. Under your ceiling light the seams are gone. Under a work light at 8°, a few still show.

  4. L0L1L2L3L4L5

    L5 A skim over everything

    A thin coat over the whole wall. For west windows at sunset, gloss paint and long hallways lit from the end.
