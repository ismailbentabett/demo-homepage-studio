Which oil does your engine take?

Conventional

The baseline for older engines. Changed every 3,000–5,000 miles. Fine if your manual calls for it and you keep short intervals.

Synthetic blend

A middle ground: some synthetic protection at a lower price. Changed every 5,000–7,500 miles. Common for trucks that see dirt roads.

Full synthetic

Strongest protection at high temps and cold starts. Changed every 7,500–10,000 miles. Most newer cars need it; your manual’s spec wins over any brand.
