Price a service for my house

  1. Termite contract & renewal

    A yearly termite contract with a dated inspection log, so renewals show what was checked.

  2. Formosan termite treatment

    Treatment aimed at Formosan colonies, which can nest in walls and do not need soil contact.

  3. Bait station monitoring

    Numbered stations around the house checked on a schedule, with readings logged every visit.

  4. Mosquito treatment

    Monthly yard treatment from spring to first frost, aimed at shade and standing water.

  5. Fire ants

    Yard mounds treated and rechecked, with a note of where new ones keep popping up.

  6. Roaches & palmetto bugs

    Big outdoor roaches kept outside: entry points found, sealed and treated around the edge.

  7. Rats in the attic

    Roof rats trapped and their way in sealed, from soffit gaps to tree limbs on the roof.

  8. Quarterly pest visits

    Four logged visits a year, and a ticket that says what was used and where.

  9. After-flood termite check

    Wet framing and disturbed bait stations checked after high water, before termites move in.

How a visit reads

Every ticket has the same four lines, in the order the tech does the work.

Visit ticket Sample · May 14
  1. 01

    Arrive

    Walk the foundation with a flashlight and a probe. Mud tubes get marked where they are.

  2. 02

    Read

    Each station cap comes off and the reading goes down next to its number, hits or no hits.

  3. 03

    Treat

    Only where a reading says so, with the product named on the ticket.

  4. 04

    Leave

    A dated ticket with what was used, where, and the re-entry time from the label.
