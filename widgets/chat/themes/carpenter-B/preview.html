30″ window sill

Repair or replace? Probe it first.

Paint hides rot. A screwdriver finds it in ten seconds, and tells you whether the piece can be saved before anyone quotes a new one.

  1. Step 1 Which part is soft?
  2. Step 2 Push a screwdriver into the softest spot

  3. Step 3 · Verdict

    The depth decides it.

    Under ¼″ usually takes a glued-in patch (a dutchman). ¼–1″ means a new piece, milled to match. Through means we check the framing behind it too.
