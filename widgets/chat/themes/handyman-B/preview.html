    Four photos, one firm number

    Send these four shots by text and we reply with a firm price for the list.

    1 · Wide shot

    Stand back so the whole wall or fixture fits in the frame.

    2 · Close-up

    Show the damage or the part up close, in focus.

    3 · Tape measure

    Lay a tape across the area so we can read the size.
