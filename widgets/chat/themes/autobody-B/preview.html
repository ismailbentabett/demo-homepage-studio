Claim path

You pick the shop. Then here is who does what.

Your insurer can suggest a shop. You do not have to use it. Pick what happened.

Someone hit me

  1. You

    Pick the shop. Tell the other driver’s insurer it is us.

  2. You

    Get the claim number, then call or text us 4 photos.

  3. Shop

    We write the first estimate and send the photos to the adjuster.

  4. Insurer

    The adjuster approves it, or comes to look at the car.

  5. Shop

    We take it apart. Hidden damage goes to the adjuster as a supplement. We call them, not you.

  6. Shop

    Repair, paint, color checked in full sun. You get the keys and the sheet.

It was my fault

  1. You

    Pick the shop. Your policy, your choice.

  2. You

    Open a claim with your insurer. Ask about your deductible and a rental.

  3. Shop

    We write the estimate and send the photos to your adjuster.

  4. Insurer

    The adjuster approves it, or comes to look.

  5. Shop

    Teardown. Anything hidden goes in as a supplement. We handle that call.

  6. You

    Pick it up. You pay your deductible; the insurer pays us the rest.

Hail or a tree

  1. You

    Pick the shop. Same rule: your choice.

  2. You

    Open the claim. Take photos in the shade, where dents show.

  3. Shop

    We count and circle every dent on the roof, hood and trunk, on one map.

  4. Insurer

    The adjuster checks the map against the car.

  5. Shop

    Paintless where the paint is whole, body and paint where it is not.

Parking lot, no other car

  1. You

    Pick the shop. No one else is involved yet.

  2. You

    Text us 4 photos of the ding, straight on and at an angle.

  3. Shop

    We tell you if it is paintless and the sample range, before you call anyone.

  4. You

    Decide: pay it yourself, or open a claim. Either way, we do the work.
