3F Season and booking

Book porches in April for June starts

Call in March and you pick your month. By May the summer calendar is spoken for.

Season planner

Spring

Best for porch rebuilds, framing and stair replacement. The ground thaws, and paint cures well from mid-May.

Summer

Best for railings, trim and deck builds. Long days and paint dries fast, so June fills first.

Fall

Best for rot repair, priming and turnover fixes. Paint goes on before mid-October, not after.
