Drip edge, then ice shield at every eave

Metal along the eave sends water into the gutter instead of behind the fascia. Peel-and-stick membrane goes over it at eaves, valleys and pipes, where ice dams and wind-driven rain get in. Both are new on every job.

Layer 07 of 07, then the yard

The finish: air out the top, nails out of the grass

Ridge vent out, soffit air in

Hot attic air leaves through the ridge; cool air comes in at the soffits. Balanced, it stops summer heat from cooking the shingles from underneath. Then the cap goes on and the photo packet is done.

Then the yard. Magnet sweep, twice.

The dumpster leaves before we do. Two passes with the magnet over lawn, beds and driveway, so no nail ends up in a tire or a paw.
