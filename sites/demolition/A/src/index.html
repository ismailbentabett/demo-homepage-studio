One layer at a time, and the house stays clean

Every tear-out runs the same four passes. Scroll the wall: each pass takes a layer off.

  1. 01 Paint02 Plaster

    Plastic first, then the first swing

    Plastic goes up at every doorway with a zipper seam, and an air scrubber pulls from the work room, not your hallway. Floors get ram board taped at the edges. Plaster comes down into bags inside the plastic.

  2. 03 Lath04 Insulation

    Bagged in the room, carried out on a runner

    Lath and old insulation are bagged where they fall. Bags go out along a runner laid on the haul path, so the hallway and stairs keep their paint.

  3. 05 Wiring06 Plumbing

    Shut-offs checked before the first cut

    Supply and drain lines are capped and tagged, and every box gets a tag, so your plumber and electrician know what is live and what is dead.

  4. 07 Studs

    Down to the framing, and we stop if it carries weight

    If an open wall turns out to be holding up the floor above, the work stops and your builder gets the call before anything else comes out. Then the room is swept for the next trade.

Dust plan

How sealed?

Pick how sealed the work zone gets. More people and pets at home, more plastic.

Basic

Plastic at the doorways, floor protection in the work zone, broom-clean daily. For garages, basements and rooms you can close off.

Sealed

Zipper-door seams, taped edges on every sheet, a runner on the haul path. For rooms that share air with the rest of the house.

Air scrubber

A filtered air scrubber pulls from the work zone to outside while we cut. For older houses, or anyone at home with breathing trouble.
