# Strategy — demolition-B (Scale Ticket Demo & Haul)

1. **The one job.** A Toledo homeowner with a deck, shed, garage, pool or tear-out lands here and calls or prices it within 10 seconds because the page shows the paperwork (scope + weigh ticket) before asking for anything.
2. **Homeowner first 5 seconds.** Above the fold: h1 "Deck, Shed & Garage Demolition in {{biz.city}}" on scale-ticket paper; a printed scope-and-ticket sheet clipped to the corner showing loads, sample range and phone; [[call]] button; one proof line "Your written scope is the price".
3. **Contractor first 5 seconds.** Nobody in Toledo sends actual weigh tickets from the transfer station; the signature is the perforated ticket stub that accumulates tonnage as you scroll.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "They'll cut something they shouldn't" → Written Scope section (ticket 0410).
   - "Dust everywhere" → Containment note inside Tear-Out Sizer tool output.
   - "Asbestos / lead?" → House-Year Check tool + plain-language result.
   - "Dumpster cracks my drive" → Debris Load Estimator explains trailer-vs-dumpster honestly.
   - "Bid doubles once walls are open" → Guarantee line on every ticket + footer restatement.
   - "Where does it go?" → Every ticket names the destination (transfer station, concrete recycler, scrap yard).
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: [[call]] in header, hero, mobile bar. Secondary: [[quote]] buttons per service → multi-step form with service prefilled. Tool → form hand-off: Tear-Out Sizer and Debris Load Estimator end with "Book a walk-through" that pre-fills room/structure and size. Phone always visible on 390 via sticky mobile bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's dark stud-bay layer gauge (we are light-led paper, not charcoal darkness).
   - Not C's hillside color-led type mask (we are document-first, not landscape-first).
   - Not a sledgehammer selfie or excavator hero; the hero is a printed scope sheet, not destruction porn.
