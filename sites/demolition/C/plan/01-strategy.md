# Strategy — demolition-C

1. **The one job.** A Roanoke homeowner with a hillside shed, deck or garage lands here and calls or prices the removal within 10 seconds, trusting that the crew knows steep yards and will leave the ground graded back to lawn.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 "GONE" in giant condensed type masking a photo of a leaning shed; kicker "{{biz.tradeLabel}} in {{biz.city}}"; primary button "Price it"; proof line "Sample rating {{proof.rating}} · {{proof.reviewCount}} reviews (Sample) · {{biz.areas|list}}".
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The reclaimed-space ruler: a vertical sq-ft counter that grows as you scroll, showing exactly how much yard each job gave back. Nobody else in Roanoke sells demo as reclaimed land.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "They'll cut something they shouldn't" → Process section (shut-offs, test-first line).
   - "Dust everywhere" → Dust plan picker tool + containment photo.
   - "Asbestos / lead in old house" → House-year check tool + plain-language promise.
   - "Dumpster cracking my drive" → Debris load estimator (trailer vs dumpster, honest note).
   - "Bid doubles once walls open" → Tear-out sizer with sample ranges from facts + walk-through CTA.
   - "Steep yard, can they even get to it?" → Hero + story photos show hillside access; space-back ruler proves outcome.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Price it" → multi-step quote form (#contact). Secondary: [[tel]] in header + sticky mobile bar. Tools hand off via data-service prefill. Phone always ≤ 1 thumb-reach on 390.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's layer-gauge process diagram (we don't peel walls step-by-step).
   - Not B's weigh-ticket paperwork (no carbon-copy stubs or tonnage totals).
   - Not a generic "demo services" list with excavator hero (we are hillside-specific, color-led, place-driven).
