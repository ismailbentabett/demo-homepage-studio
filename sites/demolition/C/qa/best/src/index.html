Before you call

The questions everyone asks

Will you cut into pipes or wires?

We find the shut-offs before we start. Water, gas and electric lines get marked, and we dig by hand near anything that runs close to the structure.

What about dust in the house?

Interior tear-outs go inside plastic with a zippered entry. Outside, we mist when it is dry and sweep the site at the end of each day.

The house is old. Asbestos or lead?

Houses built before 1978 can have lead paint. We test what we will disturb before we start, and handle it safely if anything tests positive.

Will a dumpster crack my driveway?

On steep lots we park a dump trailer on the street and carry debris up to it. If a dumpster is needed, it goes on a flat spot with plywood under the wheels, and we agree where first.

Will the price double once you start?

The sample ranges come from the job type and size, and we walk the lot before setting a number. If something unexpected turns up, we stop and talk it through before going on.
