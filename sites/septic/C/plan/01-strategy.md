# Strategy — septic-C

## 1. The one job of this page
A Lafayette homeowner with an aerobic plant lands here worried about rain, alarms or storm damage and must call, text or price a visit within ten seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Aerobic & Septic · Lafayette, LA" in the kicker.
- A way to act: phone number in the header and a "Price it" button in the hero.
- One reason to believe: the rain almanac strip shows we know Acadiana months and what the plant needs in each.
- Reassurance: sample rating line and open-now status under the h1.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The rain almanac signature is unlike any local competitor: a month-by-month rainfall bar that ties every service to the season, proving we understand the place rather than just listing services.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "Is this going to be a whole new system?" | Hero lead + Services (maintenance and repair before replacement) |
| "Nobody knows where the tank even is / what's in it" | Process story (what we check) |
| "They'll pump it and it backs up again" | Proof reviews + Guarantee token |
| "Is it safe for kids and dogs?" | Chlorinator / spray head service card |
| "The inspection will kill the sale" | Real estate inspections card + tool hand-off |
| "What do I do after a storm?" | After-storm restarts card + backup triage tool |
| "How much will this cost?" | Sample pricing block via estimator |
| "Do you work in my town?" | Area section with ZIP checker |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `tel:` in header, mobile bar, and hero; multi-step quote form at `#contact`.
- Secondary: SMS link in mobile bar; alarm decoder and backup triage tools prefill the form.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's dark process-led depth-stick site (no slab serif, no tank cross-section).
- Not B's light-led numbers-and-report site (no monospace readings, no calculator hero).
- Not a generic septic template with a vacuum truck hero and a "call for quote" wall.
