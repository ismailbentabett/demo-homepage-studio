# Strategy — septic-A

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner in Augusta with a gurgling toilet, a wet yard or a closing deadline lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds because they see a real price range, a clear next step, and proof that we will show them what is in their tank before we pump it.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Septic pumping & repair in Augusta, GA" in the h1.
- A way to act: sticky mobile bar with `tel:` and a "Get a sample price" button that jumps to #contact.
- One reason to believe: a photo of an opened concrete lid in red clay with the depth stick visible, and the line "We measure what's in your tank and show you the reading."
- Sample price anchor: "Pump-out from {{proof.prices.pumping.from}}" visible above the fold at 390px.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The depth-stick signature: a vertical ruler running down the left margin marked in inches like a sludge-judge pole, with each section sitting at a real tank depth. No competitor in Augusta shows the work as a measured cross-section; they show trucks and handshakes.

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `tel:{{biz.phoneE164}}` in header, hero, mobile bar, and every CTA button.
- Secondary: multi-step quote form at #contact, prefilled by each tool (triage → symptom, gauge → tank size, checklist → closing date, ZIP → zip).
- Tertiary: SMS link in mobile bar (facts.biz.sms = true).
- Phone is always ≤ 1 thumb-reach on mobile via the sticky bar.
