Seven steps, measured

Every job follows the same order. Every step gets a reading.

  1. 0″

    Locate the tank

    We probe the yard and mark the lids without tearing up the lawn.

  2. 0″

    Dig and expose

    Lids uncovered cleanly; sod set aside to replace after.

  3. 6″

    Measure scum layer

    Top crust depth read and recorded on your ticket.

  4. 40″

    Measure sludge layer

    Bottom solids depth read; tells us how full the tank really is.

  5. 52″

    Pump to the floor

    Tank emptied completely; no sludge left to resettle.

  6. 48″

    Check baffles and tee

    Inlet and outlet inspected; broken parts flagged before they kill the field.

  7. Grade

    Sod back in place

    Yard put back the way we found it. No bare dirt left behind.
