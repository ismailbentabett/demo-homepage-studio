# fencedeck-C · Torrey Rail · strategy

1. **The one job.** A San Diego homeowner on a slope or canyon-rim lot (deck tired or missing, fence running downhill,
   brush behind the yard) lands from "hillside deck San Diego" on a phone in the evening. Within 8 s they must see
   "they build on slopes like mine" and tap Call ({{biz.phone}}) or "Price my deck/fence" (estimator → form).
   Text ({{biz.phone}} via sms:) is the low-commitment third path.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (above the fold, 390 and 1440).** Kicker "Fence & Deck · {{biz.city}}, hillside and canyon lots";
   h1 (serif, 3 lines on 390) about decks built for the canyon edge; Call button (primary) + "Price it" (secondary);
   proof line: Mon-Fri 7-4:30 · Sat 8-1 · {{proof.rating}} (Sample rating, {{proof.reviewCount}}); the 21:9 strip with the
   bare slope section drawn on it peeking at the bottom edge.

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in San Diego shows the *ground* under a deck. Torrey Rail's page draws the canyon
   slope in section and builds the deck on it as you scroll (footings → posts → joists → boards → rail → lamps).
   That drawing is the signature, and it doubles as the guarantee ("we check every footing with you").

4. **Objections → where answered.**
   - "Can you even build on my slope?" → story 4 PM footings + guarantee band + review Marisol G.
   - "Posts will rot/lean" → story 5 PM posts & beams, footing sheet in services.
   - "Fire zone / will the city reject it?" → Fire-zone checklist tool (7 PM band) + review Craig F.
   - "Salt air rusts everything" → Material-by-exposure tabs (coast / inland / rim) + review Haley J.
   - "Fence on a slope looks like a staircase" → fence estimator draws stepped panels on the grade + review Victor N.
   - "Pool barrier inspection" → services card + review Derek S.
   - "What will it cost?" → sample price sheet from facts.proof.prices + estimator.
   - "Do you come to my area?" → ZIP checker + areas list in the 10 PM footer band.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: tel: in header, hero, sticky mobile bar (bottom, left 50% "Call", right 50% "Price it"),
   footer. Secondary: estimator/checklist → "Send this to {{biz.short}}" prefills form (length, height, material, gates,
   fire-zone answers). Tertiary: sms: link beside every Call. On 390 the phone sits in the bottom bar from first paint
   and in the hero button row at y ≈ 470.

6. **What this direction is NOT.** Not A's taut string line and process filmstrip of a fence job in bare dirt (no
   horizontal line running section to section, no "ft built" counter). Not B's tally marks, grade stamps or daylight
   material mosaic (no counting UI, no stamp labels, no photo grid). Not a light/color-led page: it is a night plate
   with drawn engineering, almost photo-free.
