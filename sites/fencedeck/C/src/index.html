Six hours on the rim

Watch one deck go up, from four o’clock to lights on.

Same slope, same section, one part at a time. This is the order we build in, and the order we explain it in when we walk your yard.

  1. 4 PM
  2. 5 PM
  3. 6 PM

  1. 4:00 PM · Footings

    Piers dug to firm soil, sized for your pitch.

    On a canyon slope the footing is the deck. We dig past the loose fill, bell the bottom, and set every pier for the grade. Then we walk you to each one before anything sits on top.

  2. 5:00 PM · Posts and beams

    Posts stand on steel, never in the dirt.

    Each post sits on a steel base above the concrete, so canyon damp can’t wick into the wood. Tall posts on the downhill side get cross-bracing.

  3. 6:00 PM · Joists and flashing

    The marine layer comes in. The frame is ready.

    Joists 16 in. apart, tape on every top edge, flashing where the frame meets the sill. Evening damp runs off instead of soaking in.

  4. 7:00 PM · Boards

    Boards for a lot that backs onto brush.

    Composite or ignition-resistant boards, with the gaps and the under-deck screening your city asks for in a fire zone.

  5. 8:00 PM · Cable rail

    A rail you look through, not at.

    Stainless cable and fittings pulled tight between posts, because plain steel near the water rusts by the second summer.

  6. 9:00 PM · Lamps on

    Lamps on. Dinner runs past dark.

    Low post-cap lamps wired before the boards close up, aimed down at the deck and away from the neighbour’s window.
