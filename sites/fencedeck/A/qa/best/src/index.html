Post 07 · 48 ft · Questions

Questions we hear at the fence line.

Not on the list? Call [[tel]]. {{biz.owner}} answers most of them himself.

Who finds the property line?

We walk the lot with your plat map and find the corner pins before anything is staked. If a pin is missing, a surveyor sets it first. We don’t guess at a line.

Do I need a permit or HOA sign-off?

Often, yes, for taller fences, pool barriers and raised decks. We check what your city and your HOA ask for before we order material, and we build to those rules.

What about buried gas and power lines?

We call 811 before anyone digs and wait for the paint and flags. Posts move around lines, never the other way.

Wood or vinyl?

Cedar looks warm and takes stain, and it goes grey in the sun without it. Vinyl needs no stain, only a hose. Both get the same posts and the same string.

How do your decks hold up?

We frame at 12 inches on center and tape and flash the ledger where the deck meets the house. We show you the flashing before it’s covered.
