# fencedeck-A · Stringline Fence & Deck · Boise · Strategy

1. **The one job.** A Boise/Meridian new-build owner with a bare dirt yard (or a wind-blown section) lands from "fence company near me",
   sees a per-foot sample range and their town in 5 s, and either calls/texts or types their feet into the estimator and sends the
   prefilled quote inside 60 s. Primary action = call/text; secondary = "Price my fence by the foot".

2. **Homeowner, first 5 seconds (390 and 1440).** Kicker "Fence & Deck · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}}"; h1 (process promise, ≤ 7 words,
   names fence + deck in the sub); the string line drawn taut across under the h1 with a post every "8 ft"; call button
   (`tel:`) + "Price it by the foot" button (#estimator); proof line: ★ 4.9 · 142 sample reviews · "Sample: cedar 6 ft $38-$55 / ft".
   Filmstrip of drawn plates (stake → 811 → dig → post → rails → pickets → gate) starts under the fold line and peeks.

3. **Contractor, first 5 seconds.** The page *is* a fence going up: one taut cedar-orange string runs the full page, every section
   is a post set to it, and the "ft built" counter climbs as you scroll. Nobody in Boise shows how a fence is laid out.
   This is the signature (Mason's String + Post Stations).

4. **Objections → answers.**
   - "Posts will rot / lean in 3 years" → §Post depth section (drawn cross-section: depth below frost line, concrete, 8 ft spacing) + guarantee line (5-yr post reset).
   - "Wrong side of the property line" → §Stake & string step 1 (corner pins, plat map, string before digging) + review Brittany S.
   - "Gas line / digging" → §811 step + review Anita P.
   - "HOA / city rejects it" → New-build service card ("built to your HOA rules") + FAQ, hedged.
   - "Gate sags and drags" → §Gate detail drawing (steel frame, self-closing hinges) + review Jesse W.
   - "Deck boards cup, stain peels" → Deck card (12 in. on center, flashing) + Staining card + review Luke D.
   - "No price until consultation" → sample price table from facts + estimator in the first 2 screens.
   - "Do they cover my town?" → ZIP checker + area list in the footer post.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: call/text (header right, hero, mobile bar, footer). Secondary: estimator → "Send these numbers"
   prefills the quote form (feet, height, material, gates, post count). Tool 2: Fix-or-replace quiz → result prefills service +
   asks for a photo note. ZIP checker sits next to the form. On 390: sticky bottom bar 64px, [Call] [Text] [Price it], phone
   also in the hero as the first button.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a tally/stamp/counting ledger on lumber paper (B, Denver, light-led, slab).
   - Not a dusk/hour clock, dark navy, serif, cinematic photo strip (C, San Diego).
   - Not photo-led: the library has no honest Boise yard photo, so A is drawn: etched-line plates of each build step, not stock.
