## 2 Process
Tag: POST 01 · 0 FT · How it goes up
H2: Seven steps, one string. Nothing gets dug until the line is right.
Steps: 01 Stake the corners / We walk the lot with your plat map, find the corner pins and pull a string between stakes. The string is the fence before there is a fence.
02 Call 811 / We call 811 and wait for the paint and flags. If a gas line runs where the gate post goes, the post moves.
03 Dig below the frost line / Every hole goes below the frost line, so a cold {{biz.city}} winter can't heave a post out of the ground.
04 Set posts in concrete / Posts every 8 feet, plumb to the string, set in concrete. It's written on your quote.
05 Hang the rails / Top, middle and bottom rails, level to the string, so the boards have something straight to hang on.
06 Put the pickets on / Cedar or vinyl, even gaps, tops cut to one line from stake to stake.
07 Hang the gate, walk the line / Steel frame inside, self-closing hinges. Then we walk the fence with you before we pack up.
