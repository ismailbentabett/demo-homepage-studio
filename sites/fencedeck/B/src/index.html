TB·DEN·03Post below frost

{{biz.city}} ground freezes. Posts go below it.

  1. 01

    A post every 8 feet.

    Five posts carry four bays of fence, and every one of them is a line on your quote.

  2. 02

    Dug below the frost line.

    Freezing soil swells and lifts whatever sits in it. A post set deeper than the freeze stays where we put it.

  3. 03

    Steel, or a thick wood post.

    Set in concrete crowned at the top, so rain runs away from the post instead of into it.

  4. 04

    Rails, pickets and a cap.

    The cap sheds rain off the picket tops, so the end grain stays dry through the hail season.
