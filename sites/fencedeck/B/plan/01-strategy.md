# fencedeck-B · Strategy (Tallyboard, Denver, PROOF & TOOLS, light-led)

1. **The one job.** A Denver homeowner (30-65, bungalow or 70s ranch, grey splintery deck or a leaning alley fence) lands
   from "composite deck cost per square foot denver". In ≤ 10 s they see a countable answer and act: on desktop "Count my
   deck" (board counter → quote prefilled), on phone "Call {{biz.phone}}". Text ({{biz.sms}}) is the quiet third.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (above the fold, 390 and 1440).** Kicker: "Decks & fences · {{biz.city}} and 7 towns west and
   south". h1: "Count it before you pay for it." Sub-line: every board, joist and post listed on your quote. Buttons: primary
   "Count my deck" (anchor #counter), secondary "Call {{biz.phone}}". Proof line (fs--1, tabular): "Mon-Fri 7:30-5 · 4.8 from
   203 reviews [Sample] · from $40/linear ft cedar fence [Sample]". Mosaic: lumber-stacks photo + 3 drawn tiles (joist plan
   with tally, hidden clip section, post below frost).

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Denver shows the bill of materials before the sale. Tallyboard's homepage counts
   in front of you: pencil tallies count joists and boards live, and every section carries a lumber grade stamp. The
   signature: **Tally & Grade Stamp** (pencil five-bar tallies that really count + ink-box grade stamps as section labels).

4. **Objections → sections.**
   - "Lump-sum bid hides corners" → #counter (board counter, counts joists/boards) + quote sheet sample (review Rachel B.).
   - "Posts will heave/lean (freeze-thaw)" → #posts drawn section: steel or thick wood post, set below frost.
   - "Hail and UV will wreck it" → #materials compare (hail/UV/upkeep per material, no brands).
   - "Permit / city rejects it" → #permit checklist (decks over 30 in. or attached usually need one; we check your city).
   - "Ledger rot where deck meets house" → #repair band (Omar H. review + ledger flashing drawing).
   - "Wobbly railing / sagging gate" → services ledger rows (railings bolted through frame, heavy hinges).
   - "Price hidden until consultation" → #prices table (5 sample ranges from facts, all Sample-tagged).
   - "Do you come to my town?" → ZIP checker in #area + area list.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: counter → "Put these counts on my quote" → form step 1 prefilled (W, D, board, spacing,
   sq ft, boards, joists). Secondary: tel: in header (≥1100) and sticky mobile bar (390: bottom bar, Call | Text | Count,
   56px, safe-area). Tools 2-3 (compare, permit) each hand off a hidden field (material, permitLikely). ZIP → form zip.

6. **What B is NOT.** Not A's taut string line / process sequence / filmstrip hero / green-and-cedar color field. Not C's
   dusk palette, hour stamps, canyon slope lines or 21:9 cinematic strip. Not a lumber-grain wallpaper or a brand-logo strip
   (brief §4); not a dark site; not a timeline.
