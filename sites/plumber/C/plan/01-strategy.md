# Strategy — plumber-C (Thawhouse Plumbing, Buffalo NY)

1. **The one job.** A Buffalo homeowner lands during or after a freeze, reads the masthead and cover lines in 3 seconds, and either calls/texts or opens the freeze-risk tool; conversion happens before the first scroll.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** Masthead "THE THAW ISSUE" + issue line "Buffalo · Winter {{proof.years}}"; h1 cover line naming frozen pipes and doubles; primary button "Check my freeze risk"; proof line "Sample rating 4.9 · 263 reviews · Cheektowaga to Orchard Park".
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** Nobody else in Buffalo publishes a winter magazine about their houses; the signature is the editorial department system that turns plumbing advice into local storytelling.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "$400 just to show up" → Classifieds department with sample price ranges.
   - "Dig up my yard" → Field Notes: how we locate before we cut.
   - "Don't know where shut-off is" → Freeze Map department + Shut-off finder tool.
   - "Upsell tankless" → Letters: repair-before-replace stories from neighbors.
   - "Happen again next week" → Freeze-risk check tool + Pipe froze? guide.
   - "Will they come in a blizzard?" → Hours/open-now in masthead + Letters describing storm visits.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Check my freeze risk" → freeze-risk tool → pre-filled quote form. Secondary: tel: and sms: in sticky mobile bar and masthead. Tool hand-off: every tool ends with "Book this visit" that carries inputs into the form.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's process rail with brass tags (no pipe-rail metaphor, no numbered step sequence).
   - Not B's dark ledger with measurement rows (no mono data entries, no steel-blue rules).
   - Not a generic service grid with stock wrench photos (editorial layout replaces card grids).
