Pipe Froze? Tear this page out 06

The first ten minutes, step by step

Four steps, in order. Do them while you wait for us.

1 Shut the main off

Find the main shut-off, usually on the basement front wall where the line comes in. Turn it clockwise until it stops. In a double, shut the one for your flat, or both if you are not sure.

2 Open the tap on that line

Open the faucet the frozen line feeds. A trickle means the ice is moving. A dry spout means the block is solid; leave the tap open anyway so the pressure has somewhere to go.

3 Gentle heat only

A hair dryer or a heating pad on low, starting nearest the tap. Never an open flame or a torch: copper carries heat fast and can scorch the wall behind it.

4 When to stop and call

Standing water, a bulge in the pipe, or a line behind drywall you cannot reach: stop there and call. We bring the thaw gear and check for splits before the water goes back on.
