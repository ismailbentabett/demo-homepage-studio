PIPE FROZE? 06

The first 10 minutes, step by step

Find the main shut-off (usually basement front wall) and turn it clockwise until it stops. This keeps pressure off the split while you wait.

Call now

Open the faucet that feeds the frozen line. A trickle tells you the ice is moving; a dry spout means the block is solid.

Use a hair dryer or heating pad on low. Never an open flame or torch — copper conducts heat fast and can scorch the wall.

If you smell gas, see standing water, or the pipe is behind drywall you can’t reach, stop and call. We bring the right thaw gear.
