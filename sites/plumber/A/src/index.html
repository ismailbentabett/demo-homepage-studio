Is my water pressure in the safe range?

Screw a gauge onto an outside spigot, open it all the way, and read the needle. Normal is 40 to 80 psi.

60 psi: inside the normal 40 to 80 range. Nothing to do.

  • Under 40 Weak showers; often a failing regulator or old galvanized pipe.
  • 40 to 80 Normal. Leave it alone.
  • Over 80 Banging pipes, running toilets. A regulator fixes it.
