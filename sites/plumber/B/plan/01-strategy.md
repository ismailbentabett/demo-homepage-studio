# Strategy — plumber-B (Ledgerline Plumbing)

1. **The one job.** A Pittsburgh homeowner with old pipes lands here, sees their problem named in plain words, and calls or texts within 10 seconds to get a written reading before any work starts.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1: "Pittsburgh plumbing, written down." Kicker above: "Ledgerline Plumbing · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.stateName}}". Below h1: proof line "Mon–Sat 7:00–17:30 · Mt. Lebanon to Penn Hills · 4.8 Sample rating". Primary button "Price it", secondary "Text a photo". Phone visible in header and sticky mobile bar.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The page is a ledger: every section is an entry row with a reading, a status and an action. No other plumber in Pittsburgh hands the homeowner the measurement sheet as the design system.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "$400 just to show up" → Sample pricing block + "We write the reading before we quote" in hero.
   - "Dig up my whole yard" → Sewer camera story with footage-by-foot log; repair at the break, not the whole lateral.
   - "Don't know where shut-off is" → Shut-off finder tool + valve-labeling as a service.
   - "Upsell tankless when I need a fix" → Water heater life check tool outputs maintain / plan / replace; repair offered first.
   - "Happens again next week" → Pressure check + backwater valve entries show the preventive reading.
   - "Will they explain what's wrong?" → Every review quotes the written log ("ledger sheet listed every joint").
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact). Secondary: "Text a photo" → sms: link; phone in header/bar → tel:. Tools (pipe ID, pressure check, lateral era) end with a prefill hand-off into the form. Phone always one thumb-reach away on 390 via sticky bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's warm brass-tag process rail (no tags, no copper pipe decoration, no light limestone tone).
   - Not C's editorial magazine cover (no masthead, no departments, no page numbers, no color-led lake-blue).
   - Not a generic blue water-drop template (dark-led iron palette, mono numerals, ledger rows instead of icon cards).
