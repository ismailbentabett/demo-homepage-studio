A Bakersfield homeowner with a sprinkler problem lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds, confident that Lateral Line will show them the broken part, price the fix before digging, and leave the yard clean.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + city: "Sprinkler repair in Bakersfield" in the h1.
- A way to act: phone number and "Price it" button above the fold.
- One reason to believe: "We open the valve box, show you what failed, and price it before we dig."
- Proof line: hours · service area · sample rating with Sample tag.
- Next band peeks in at the bottom of the hero.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The valve-box-lid signature: every section opens with a round lid stamped with its zone number that lifts on scroll. No competitor in Bakersfield organizes their site as a zone-by-zone walkthrough with the actual work shown step by step.
