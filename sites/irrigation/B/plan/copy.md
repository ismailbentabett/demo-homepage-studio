## Process
- H2: Meter test first, then zone by zone
- Step 1: Meter test. We shut everything off and watch the meter. If it spins, there is a leak.
- Step 2: Zone isolate. One zone at a time until the leak is found and marked.
- Step 3: Fix and test. The part is replaced, the zone runs again, and we show you the reading.
