# Strategy — irrigation-B (Catch Cup Irrigation)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Riverside homeowner with a suspicious water bill or a dry lawn patch lands here, sees their yard measured before anyone asks for money, and calls or texts within 10 seconds to book a water audit or price a specific fix.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + city in plain words: "Sprinkler repair & water audits in Riverside"
- Phone number and open-now status above the fold
- A catch-cup grid filling to different levels beside the promise: "See how evenly your lawn is watered before you spend a dollar on fixes"
- One sample price band visible without scrolling so they know a head is not a new system

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The site is the audit report itself: readings per zone, a pressure number, a coverage grid, and every fix priced line by line. No other irrigation site in Riverside shows the measurement before the quote.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section that answers it |
|---|---|
| They'll dig up my whole yard to find one leak | #process (meter test → zone isolate → mark) |
| I'll get charged for a whole new system when it's one valve | #prices (line-item sample prices) |
| I don't know if the guy knows anything | #audit-preview (what an audit measures, what the report looks like) |
| I'll pay and the dry spot will still be there | #reviews (sample reviews tied to the exact service) |
| I'll get fined for watering on the wrong day | #area (ZIP checker + hours/open-now) |
| The bill jumped and I don't know why | #bill-spike tool |
| My controller is ancient and guessing | #controller-age tool |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: call from sticky mobile bar or header phone; form at #contact reached via every "Price it" CTA
- Secondary: text (sms:true in facts); tool outputs hand off to the form with service pre-selected
- Phone is never more than one thumb-reach away on a phone (sticky bar under 900px)

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT direction A (process-led, dark pre-dawn, valve-box lid signature): we are light-led and clinical, not moody
- NOT direction C (local-story clay/freeze, map hero, Texas): we are Inland Empire stucco and tile roofs, audit-report tone
- NOT a landscaping gallery or a golf-course photo: every frame is measurement, gauge, cup, nozzle, drip emitter
