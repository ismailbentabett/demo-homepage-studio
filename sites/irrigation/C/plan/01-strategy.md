# Strategy — irrigation-C (Gumbo Clay Sprinkler Co.)

## 1. The one job of this page
A McKinney homeowner with a sprinkler problem or a seasonal task lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds, confident that this company knows North Texas clay, freeze cycles and local watering rules.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- **Trade + town:** "McKinney sprinkler repair" in the h1, with Allen, Frisco, Prosper and Melissa named just below.
- **A way to act:** phone number and "Price it" button above the fold, plus an open-now line.
- **One reason to believe:** a sample rating line ("4.7 · 91 reviews · Sample") and a soil-season tagline that names the gumbo clay and the freeze.
- **Next band peek:** the top of the season-card section is visible at the bottom of the hero so they know there is more.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature: a wavy clay soil line that runs through every section, cracking wider in summer bands and closing in fall/winter ones, with a frost-blue edge in the freeze section. No other irrigation site in the library uses a soil-line motif; it makes the page look like it was drawn for Collin County, not templated.

## 5. Conversion paths
- **Primary:** `tel:` link in header, hero, mobile bar and footer; "Price it" buttons on services and tools scroll to `#contact`.
- **Secondary:** SMS link (`sms:`) beside the phone; ZIP checker hands off to the form with the ZIP prefilled; season card and freeze checklist hand off with the service pre-selected.
- **Phone is never more than one thumb-reach away** on a phone thanks to the sticky mobile bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- **Not A (process-led):** no valve-box lids, no zone-numbered sections, no pre-dawn dark tone. A tells the job step by step; C tells the year month by month.
- **Not B (audit/tools-led):** no catch cups, no coverage grids, no mono readings. B leads with measurement; C leads with place and weather.
- **Not a generic lawn-care template:** no golf-course green, no water-drop logo, no blue-and-green palette, no "quality you can trust" headline.
