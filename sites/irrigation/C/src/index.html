February

The freeze splits pipes. We replace them fast.

After a hard freeze, backflow devices crack and lateral lines split. We carry the common sizes on the truck and finish most freeze repairs in one visit.

Freeze-ready checklist

  • Backflow wrapped or insulated
  • Main shut-off tagged and working
  • Controller set to off or rain mode
  • Drip zones drained or capped
