# Strategy — mason-A (Soft Joint Masonry)

1. **The one job.** A homeowner with old brick in Cincinnati lands here, sees we understand soft lime mortar, and calls or prices a visit within 10 seconds.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 names "Lime Mortar Repointing · Cincinnati"; a primary "Price it" button and secondary "Call (513) 555-0158" sit above the fold; a proof line reads "21 years on soft brick · Sample rating 4.9". The hero is an offset-framed photo of hands tooling a fresh joint.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The mortar-joint divider system: each section is separated by a drawn joint whose profile advances through the repointing sequence (raked → packed → struck → tooled). No competitor shows the craft as a structural design element.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - Wrong mortar ruins brick → Hero + Process story (lime vs Portland explained).
   - Patch color mismatch → Mortar Match tool + Proof section (test panels).
   - Rebuild vs repair chimney → Repoint-or-Rebuild tool + Services.
   - Money pit fear → Sample prices + Guarantee band.
   - Power-wash damage → Paint Removal service + Photo grade (gentle light).
   - Not local → ZIP checker + Cincinnati neighborhood references.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Price it" → multi-step quote form (#contact). Secondary: sticky mobile bar with tel: + SMS. Tools hand off via prefill (mortar check → service step; chimney quiz → service step; timing → when step). Phone always ≤ 1 thumb reach on mobile via mobilebar.
