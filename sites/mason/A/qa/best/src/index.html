Hard cement on soft brick breaks the brick

Brick laid before the 1930s was set in lime. The mortar has to stay softer than the brick, so the joint wears and the brick does not. Cement is harder than old brick: it traps water, and the face of the brick pops off instead.

A gray patch with crumbled brick around it means someone used cement. We cut it out and repoint in lime.
