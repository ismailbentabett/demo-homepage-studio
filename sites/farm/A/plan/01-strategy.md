# Strategy — farm-A (Sixteen Rows Farm)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Columbia-area parent or cook lands here from a friend's post or a search, sees what is ripe *this* week and that the stand is open today, and calls or texts to reserve a CSA share, a PYO bucket or a beef pickup within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Family Farm · Columbia, SC" in the header.
- Open-now status with today's hours (data-open-now).
- Hero photo of rows at first light with the kicker "Picked at first light. On the stand by ten."
- One reason to believe: the morning-clock timestamp "5:45 Picking" chalked beside the h1.
- Primary CTA: "See what's ripe" → #ripe; secondary: [[tel]].

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The **morning clock** signature: a thin vertical rail on the left whose time advances as you scroll (5:45 → 18:00), stamping each section with the hour it happens on the farm. No competitor shows the work as a clock; every other farm site is a sunset and a basket.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: [[call]] in header + sticky mobile bar; form at #contact (multi-step reserve).
- Secondary: "Text before you drive" CTAs at #ripe and #pyo → tel:{{biz.phoneE164}} with body prefill.
- Tool hand-offs: Share sizer → #contact with service=CSA; Freezer math → #contact with service=Beef; ZIP checker → #contact with zip prefilled.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away via the sticky mobile bar under 900px.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B's crate-tag proof-and-tools grid (light-led, manila tags, overhead flat-lays).
- Not C's Wiregrass almanac map (color-led sand/terracotta, county-road SVG, month dial).
