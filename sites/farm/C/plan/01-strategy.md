# Strategy — farm-C (Sand Road Farm)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Wiregrass parent or cook lands here, sees what is ripe *this* month and whether the stand is open today, and either drives out or reserves a pickup within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Family Farm · Dothan, AL" in the kicker.
- What is in now: the current month's almanac card is highlighted on load with its crop and status.
- A way to act: "Text before you drive" button and the phone in the sticky mobile bar.
- One reason to believe: "23 years on this sand" line with the Sample tag beside it.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The Wiregrass almanac strip: a twelve-month rail that tells the whole year at a glance, with the current month found and lit. Nobody else in Dothan shows the season as a readable calendar tied to their actual fields.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| "We drove 40 minutes and the field was picked out." | Almanac current-month card + text-before-you-drive CTA |
| "Is it actually theirs?" | Field-to-stand photo break + process note in services |
| "CSA box will be all greens I can't cook." | Share sizer tool with sample week |
| "Quarter cow: where does it go, what cuts?" | Freezer math tool with sample range |
| "Is it open today?" | Open-now badge in hero + hours band |
| "What will we spend?" | Bucket math + sample price block |
| "Field trip fit for my class?" | Field trip planner tool |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: multi-step reserve form (`#contact`) reached from every service card, every tool result, and the hero CTA.
- Secondary: `tel:` in header, mobile bar, and open-now band; SMS link in hero and tools.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's morning-clock process timeline (no time stamps, no pre-dawn dark tone).
- Not B's crate-tag proof system (no manila tags, no overhead flat-lays as hero).
- Not a generic farm template with sunset hero and script type; this is place-led, month-led, and color-warm.
