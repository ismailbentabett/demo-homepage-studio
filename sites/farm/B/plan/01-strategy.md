# Strategy — farm-B (Halfbushel Farm)

## 1. The one job of this page
A parent or cook within 40 minutes of Jackson, MS lands here on a phone and decides in 10 seconds whether to drive out today: they see what is ripe right now, whether the field is open, what a bucket costs, and how to reserve a share or ask a question by text.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "U-pick berries, eggs & beef · Jackson, MS" in the kicker above the h1.
- Open-now status with today's hours (`{{hours|today}}`) directly under the h1.
- A way to act: primary CTA "Check the field" scrolls to the picked-out check tool; secondary CTA "Call {{biz.phone}}" is always one thumb-reach away via the sticky mobile bar.
- One reason to believe: a dated crate-tag mosaic showing five crops with their status this week ("in", "coming", "done") and a sample rating line with the Sample tag.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature system: every fact on the page is presented as a string-tied manila crate tag with a punched hole, carrying status, weight, date checked and sample price. No other farm site in the library uses a physical-tag information system; it makes the demo feel like a working stand, not a brochure.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| "We drove 40 minutes and the field was picked out." | `#field-check` (Picked-out check tool) + hero mosaic tags |
| "Is it actually theirs?" | `#proof` (sample reviews with town + service, crate-tag format) |
| "The CSA box will be all greens I can't cook / can't skip." | `#share-sizer` (Share sizer tool with pause-by-text note) |
| "A quarter cow — where do I put it, what cuts?" | `#freezer-math` (Freezer math tool with take-home weight) |
| "Is it open today? Facebook says one thing." | Hero open-now line + `#hours` band + sticky header status |
| "What will a bucket cost?" | `#bucket-math` tool + `#prices` sample block |
| "No prices visible / PDF menu" | `#prices` sample price block with crate tags |
| "Can I text them?" | Sticky mobile bar + contact form step 4 SMS opt-in mention |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Check the field" → Picked-out check → result offers "Reserve a pickup" button → `#contact` form with service prefilled.
- Secondary: "Call [[tel]]" in sticky mobile bar (always visible under 900px); "Text us before you drive" microcopy beside open-now.
- Tool hand-offs: Bucket math, Share sizer and Freezer math each end with a "Reserve" button that scrolls to `#contact` and pre-selects the relevant service via `data-service`.
- Phone is never more than one tap away on mobile via `[data-mobilebar]`.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A (morning clock): no dark pre-dawn palette, no time-rail signature, no process-first narrative. Farm-B is light-led paper-and-tag, answers-first.
- Not C (Wiregrass almanac): no map-led hero, no month-ring signature, no regional story arc. Farm-B is tool-and-proof, not place-and-year.
- Not a generic farm template: no cartoon barn, no script "farm fresh", no sunset hero, no basket-of-perfect-produce stock. Tags are typed in mono, photos are overhead flat-lays, prices are sample-labelled ranges from facts only.
