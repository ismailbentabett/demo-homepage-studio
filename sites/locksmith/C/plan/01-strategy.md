# locksmith-C · Strategy (Many Doors Lock & Key, Tacoma, direction C: local story)

1. **The one job.** Two visitors. Locked-out caller (≈60%): wet porch, one hand, low battery → taps the
   phone in ≤ 5 s. Planned buyer (PCS family, landlord, new owner): counts their doors → gets the sample
   rekey range → sends the quote form prefilled with the count, in ≤ 90 s.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 and 1440).** Kicker "Tacoma locksmith · {{hours|today}}"; h1 "Every door
   in Tacoma has a story. Yours starts with a phone call." (≤ 3 lines at 375); primary `tel:` "Call
   {{biz.phone}}"; secondary "Count my doors" (anchor to the hero counter); proof line: "Range said on the
   phone · text with your tech's name · ID checked at the door · 4.8 (Sample rating)". The hero door wall
   (8 painted doors) is itself the lock counter, so the tool is above the fold.
3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Tacoma has a homepage that *is* their city's front doors: a wall of
   painted Tacoma doors (Stadium Craftsman, Hilltop porch, Proctor bungalow, Lakewood PCS rental, duplex,
   garage entry…) that you tap to count locks and that swing open on the job behind each one. → signature
   "Hinged Door Wall".
4. **Objections → section.**
   - "$29 on the phone, $300 at the door" → Prices band (sample ranges from facts, same words as the call).
   - "Random guy in an unmarked car" → Arrival-text door (sample text with tech first name + vehicle).
   - "He'll drill it and sell me a lock" → Lockout door: "picked open, not drilled" (services blurb).
   - "They'll open anyone's door" → ID-check promise in the hero proof row + lockout door.
   - "My old house hardware gets thrown out" → Old Brass door (glass knobs kept, Claire V. sample review).
   - "Deadbolt sticks every November" → Rain story (swelling door diagram, strike moves) + self-check.
   - "Do you even come to Gig Harbor / Fife?" → ZIP checker + area doors in footer.
   - "Who else has a key to my new house?" → Move-in / PCS checklist.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: `tel:` (header right, hero, sticky mobile bar left half, every door's
   back face, footer). Secondary: `sms:` text (mobile bar right half) + quote form. Tool hand-off: hero
   door counter → "Price these N locks" → form step 1 prefilled (service "Move-in rekeys", locks = N);
   checklist → form with "PCS / move-in" note. On 390: phone button in hero at ~y 420, sticky bottom bar
   (Call | Text) after 1 screen of scroll, 56px tall.
6. **NOT this direction.**
   - Not a night timeline / clock rail (A): daylight, overcast, no timestamps as structure.
   - Not a magazine with folios and footnotes (B): no masthead, no mono-typed footnotes, no red strikes.
   - Not a dark-led or newsprint page: color-led, painted panels in teal / oxblood / mustard / slate.
