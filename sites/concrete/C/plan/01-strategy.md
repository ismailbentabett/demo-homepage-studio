1. **One job.** A homeowner in {{biz.city}} with a heaved, flaking or sunken slab lands here and either calls [[tel]] or taps "Price it" within 10 seconds, confident this company knows Front Range clay and freeze-thaw.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 names the trade + city ("Concrete for swelling clay and a hundred frozen nights · {{biz.city}}"); ZIP checker is embedded in the hero map; one proof line reads "{{proof.years}} winters on this dirt · Sample"; call button sits under the headline.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The hero is a topographic map of the Pikes Peak region with town pins at their real elevations and the contour-line signature system already drawing — nobody else in town sells concrete as a local geology story.
4. **Objections → sections.**
   - "It'll crack in a year" → Winter Damage Reader + Freeze-Thaw Story section.
   - "They'll pour thin / skip gravel" → Base & Mix spec cards (t-deep band) with thickness callouts.
   - "Water runs toward my foundation" → Slope & Drainage note in every service card + Slab Lifting service.
   - "Quote is one number" → Sample price ranges from `{{proof.prices}}` shown via estimator, labelled Sample.
   - "They'll tear up my lawn" → Site Protection promise in Process section.
   - "Will it hold up to road salt?" → Sealing schedule + air-entrained mix explained.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: [[call]] sticky on mobile bar + form at #contact. Secondary: "Price it" buttons prefill service via data-service. Tool → form hand-off: Winter Damage Reader and Lift-or-Replace quiz end with "Have us look at it" that scrolls to #contact with the diagnosis prefilled. Phone is always one thumb-reach away via mobile bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a process ticket (that is concrete-A).
   - Not a dark spec-builder with dimension rails (that is concrete-B).
   - Not a gallery-first decorative showcase; photos serve the geology story, never stand alone as "our work".
