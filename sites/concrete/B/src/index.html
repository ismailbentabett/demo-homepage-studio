Cut a core out of the slab. Every layer has a number.

Scroll down through it. Each layer lights its line on your spec sheet.

  1. 01 · Finish Your pick

    The top you walk on

    Broom for grip, washed pebble, stamped stone, or cool deck by the pool. You pick it below.

  2. 02 · Slab 5–6 in.

    Five inches for cars, six for RV pads

    The thickness is written on the quote. Measure the edge after the pour; it should match.

  3. 03 · Steel #4 @ 16 in.

    Rebar on chairs, not in the dirt

    #4 bar tied in a 16-inch grid and lifted to mid-depth, where it can hold the slab together.

  4. 04 · Base 4 in.

    Road base, compacted in lifts

    Four inches of crushed base, wetted and packed. Hit caliche? You see the rock, and any price change is on paper first.

  5. 05 · Subgrade Slope away

    Native soil, cut to drain

    The dirt is graded so water runs off the slab and away from the house, not under it.
