# Strategy — concrete-B (Fieldtest Concrete Co., Mesa AZ)

## 1. The one job
A Mesa homeowner lands, sees thickness/steel/base as numbers they can check, builds a slab spec in under 60 seconds, and taps "Price it" to send that spec to the quote form.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- H1: giant condensed "6 IN." masking a dawn screed photo; subhead names trade + town ("Mesa concrete driveways, RV pads and pool decks").
- Proof line under h1: "Mon–Sat 5:00–15:00 · Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler · 4.8 ★ (Sample rating)".
- Two buttons: primary "Build your spec" (anchors to estimator), secondary "Call (480) 555-0132".
- No cement truck, no "quality you can trust", no decorative slider.

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
The sticky spec rail on the right (bottom sheet on phones) fills live as the visitor uses tools; every claim on the page is a dimension line with a number. Nobody else in Mesa lets the homeowner assemble a printable spec before calling.

## 4. Objections → section
1. "It'll crack in a year" → Core-sample story (finish / slab / steel / base / subgrade layers labelled with dimensions).
2. "They'll pour thin and skip gravel" → Slab calculator outputs suggested thickness + base depth; spec rail records it.
3. "Quote is one number, then it rises" → Prices block shows ranges from facts.proof.prices with Sample tag; spec hand-off lists every line item.
4. "Water runs toward the foundation" → Slope/drainage callout in the core sample + pad sizer notes approach grade.
5. "RV pad won't fit my rig" → RV & boat pad sizer takes vehicle length + gate width, outputs pad size and 6-in. rebar spec.
6. "Summer heat ruins fresh concrete" → Pour season planner shows dawn start + retarder + wet-cure for July; cure clock gives walk/drive/28-day dates.
7. "I don't know which finish to pick" → Finish compare tabs (broom / exposed aggregate / stamped / cool deck) with grip, upkeep and sample $/sq ft.
8. "Will they tear up my lawn?" → Service-area section names sprinkler protection and clean edge; reviews mention caliche and price-change-on-paper.

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Build your spec" → estimator → spec rail → quote form (prefilled with size, thickness, finish, pad options).
- Secondary: sticky mobile bar with phone + SMS; "Call" button always visible.
- Tool → form hand-off: every tool ends with "Add to my spec"; the spec rail opens full-screen as the quote form.
- Phone placement: header right on desktop; sticky bottom bar on ≤ 640 px with tel: and sms:.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
1. NOT a process ticket (that is concrete-A's carbon-copy pour ticket with numbered line items and a 28-day curve).
2. NOT a local topo story (that is concrete-C's contour map with elevation markers and winter-damage reader).
3. NOT a gallery of finished slabs or a decorative-concrete slider; the hero is a measurement, not a finish.
