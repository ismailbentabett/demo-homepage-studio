# Strategy — concrete-A (Long Cure Concrete Co., Salt Lake City)

## 1. The one job
A Salt Lake homeowner with a failing driveway or planned patio lands, sees the slab spec written out like a ticket, and clicks "Price it" or calls within 8 seconds because the invisible half (base, steel, joints, cure) is already visible.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- H1 kicker: "Concrete · Salt Lake City"
- H1: "A slab is decided in the dirt, not the truck."
- Hero is a pour ticket (document object) listing EXCAVATE · BASE · STEEL · MIX · JOINTS · CURE with a broom-finish photo clipped to it and a SAMPLE TICKET stamp.
- Two buttons: "Price it" (quote form) and "Call (801) 555-0131".
- Proof line under h1: "Mon–Sat 7:00–5:30 · Millcreek to Draper · 4.9 ★ (Sample rating)".

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in SLC shows the base, steel and cure schedule as the hero; everyone shows a mixer truck or a shiny stamped patio. This page makes the ticket — the paper that proves patience — the wow moment.

## 4. Objections → sections
1. "It'll crack in a year" → 06 JOINTS line item + saw-cut photo + story section explaining where cracks go on purpose.
2. "They'll pour thin / skip gravel" → 02 BASE + 03 STEEL line items with thickness callouts and rebar-on-chairs photo.
3. "They'll tear up my lawn" → 01 DIG line item + crew-behind photo + note on hand edging near grass.
4. "Quote is one number" → Slab calculator tool outputs sq ft, yards, sample range from facts.proof.prices; pre-fills quote form.
5. "Water runs toward foundation" → 02 BASE slope callout + walkway photo showing positive grade.
6. "Salt ruined the last one" → 08 CURE line item + winter damage reader tool + sealing service card.
7. "Is this sunken slab fixable?" → Crack reader + Lift-or-replace quiz tools.

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact); every service card has its own [[quote svc]] link.
- Secondary: sticky mobile bar with Call + Price it; phone in header at ≥ 900px.
- Tool → form: slab calculator, crack reader, cure clock each end with "Send me these numbers" that scrolls to #contact with data-service prefilled.
- ZIP checker sits in the service-area band; "yes" offers the form with ZIP prefilled.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B's dark-led spec rail with giant condensed numerals and desert-dawn photos (we are light-led, document-first).
- Not C's color-led topo map with elevation markers and Front Range granite palette (we stay valley-flat, paper-toned).
- Not a gallery of finished slabs or a mixer-truck hero; the ticket IS the hero, and finished work appears only as proof, never as the first impression.
