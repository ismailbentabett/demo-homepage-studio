# Strategy — consulting-A

## 1. The one job of this page
An owner-run business operator in Northampton lands here after closing their shop, sees the six-session engagement laid out as six Thursday evenings at their own table, and calls or books the first session within 30 seconds because the scope, price range and first-step outcome are already visible.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Small Business Consulting · Northampton, MA" in the kicker above the h1.
- The promise: "Six Thursdays, after you close." as the h1.
- A way to act: `[[call]]` button and phone in the header + mobile bar.
- One reason to believe: a proof line under the h1 with sample rating, years and the guarantee, all labelled Sample.
- Next band peek: the top of the Session 1 card is visible at the bottom of the hero viewport.

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature ledger system: every section is framed as a session slip with "Thursday N of 6", a margin column of "You bring / You leave with", and a homework tear-off. No other consulting site in the Pioneer Valley shows the engagement as a physical, bounded process.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "They'll use words I don't understand" | Hero + every session slip uses owner words only; voice statement in About. |
| "It'll cost more than the problem" | Sample pricing block with fixed-fee ranges + estimator tool. |
| "They've never run anything" | About section with Ruth M.'s background in owner-operated shops. |
| "I don't have time" | Hero states "after you close"; sessions are evening; planner shows weekly commitment. |
| "What do I walk away with?" | Each session slip lists the tangible deliverable. |
| "What if it isn't useful?" | Guarantee line under the hero proof strip. |
| "Do they serve my town?" | ZIP checker in the Area section. |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `[[call]]` in header, hero, mobile bar, and end of every session slip.
- Secondary: Where-to-start quiz → pre-filled form; Engagement planner → pre-filled form; ZIP checker yes → form with ZIP.
- Tertiary: SMS link in footer (facts.biz.sms = true).
Phone is always one thumb-reach away via the sticky mobile bar under 900px.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT a numbers-first worksheet site (that is Direction B).
- NOT a local-story map of the Miami Valley (that is Direction C).
- NOT a founder-bio-heavy credibility site; credibility comes from the process artifact, not credentials.
