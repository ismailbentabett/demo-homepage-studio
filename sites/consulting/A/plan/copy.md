## Sessions 1-2
- Section kicker: The engagement

- Session 1 heading: Thursday 1 of 6 — Where the money goes
- Session 1 You bring: Last three months of bank statements, your current price list, payroll dates.
- Session 1 You leave with: A twelve-week cash calendar with the tight weeks marked.
- Session 1 homework: Circle the two payments you could move if next month looks like this one.
- Session 2 heading: Thursday 2 of 6 — What a job really costs
- Session 2 You bring: Three recent jobs, invoices, material receipts, hours logged.
- Session 2 You leave with: A true cost per job and a price you can say out loud.

- H2: Thursdays 3 and 4 — People and process

- Session 3 homework: Ask one person to own a task they have been doing without being asked.
- Session 4 heading: Thursday 4 of 6 — The jobs only you know
- Session 4 You bring: The thing that stops when you take a week off.

- H2: Thursdays 5 and 6 — Growth and the plan
