The engagement

What happens on Thursdays 1 and 2

Thursday 1 of 6 — Where the money goes

You bring
Last three months of bank statements, your current price list, payroll dates.
You leave with
A twelve-week cash calendar with the tight weeks marked.
Homework

Circle the two payments you could move if next month looks like this one.

Price it

Thursday 2 of 6 — What a job really costs

You bring
Three recent jobs, invoices, material receipts, hours logged.
You leave with
A true cost per job and a price you can say out loud.
Homework

Post the new price sheet where your team sees it before Friday.

Price it

Thursdays 3 and 4 — People and process

Thursday 3 of 6 — Who does what

You bring
Your current org chart (even if it is just names on a napkin).
You leave with
Written roles and the description of the next hire.
Homework

Ask one person to own a task they have been doing without being asked.

Price it

Thursday 4 of 6 — The jobs only you know

You bring
The thing that stops when you take a week off.
You leave with
Steps someone else can follow, with photos.
Homework

Watch someone else do it from the binder, not from memory.

Price it

Thursdays 5 and 6 — Growth and the plan

Thursday 5 of 6 — What to stop paying for

You bring
Last year's marketing spend, referral sources, lost leads.
You leave with
One page: who you want more of, where they look, what to cut.
Homework

Cancel one channel that has not earned its keep in six months.

Price it

Thursday 6 of 6 — The written plan

You bring
The five pages from the last five weeks.
You leave with
A bound plan with numbers, dates and the next hire described.
Homework

Hand it to someone who needs to believe it.

Price it
