# Strategy — consulting-B (Plain Figures Business Advisors)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Frederick small-business owner lands here, runs the cash runway check in 30 seconds, sees a real number about their own business, and calls to talk through what it means — all within 10 seconds of deciding to stay.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- **Trade + town:** "Small Business Consulting · Frederick, MD" in the kicker above the h1.
- **The tool:** The cash runway check is the hero — three inputs, a bar that fills, a plain sentence result.
- **A way to act:** Phone number beside the tool with hours and "Open now" status.
- **One reason to believe:** "Sample rating 4.8 · 118 reviews" line under the h1, labelled Sample.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The worksheet grid signature: faint squared paper behind every section with cell references in the margin (B4, C7). Every claim has a "how we figure it" footnote. No other consulting site in Frederick looks like a working spreadsheet brought to life.

## 5. Conversion paths
- **Primary:** Call from hero phone, mobile bar, or form thank-you.
- **Secondary:** Tool → "Walk me through my runway" button pre-fills the form service field.
- **Tertiary:** Text/SMS link in mobile bar.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- **Not A (Six Thursdays):** No evening/lamp mood, no session ledger, no literary serif. Direction B is daylight, mono-accent type, and tools-first.
- **Not C (Dayton makers):** No map, no river lines, no condensed industrial type. Direction B is Frederick-specific but not place-led; it is proof-and-tools-led.
- **Not generic consulting:** No handshake photos, no "unlock potential", no glass towers, no lightbulbs. The hero is a calculator, not a metaphor.
