# Strategy — consulting-C

## Buyer
Owner of a small maker shop in the Miami Valley (machine shop, print shop, HVAC firm, family manufacturer). Age 55–65. Second-generation or founder thinking about what comes next: grow, hand it to a son/daughter/manager, or sell. They do not buy "strategy"; they buy a calmer Monday, a payroll that clears, and a plan for the day they step back.

## Job
Planned engagement around succession, exit readiness, growth planning, operations walk-throughs, cash flow calendars and owner coaching. The urgent 20% is cash or a key-person gap; the planned 80% is "what happens when I stop".

## Objections to answer on the page
1. "A consultant will use words I don't understand and leave me with a binder." → Plain shop-floor language, every deliverable named.
2. "It'll cost more than the problem it fixes." → Sample price ranges visible above the fold and repeated in the estimator.
3. "They've never run anything." → Operations walk-through described as walking the floor with you; reviews from owners in Kettering, Miamisburg, Beavercreek.
4. "I don't have time." → First meeting outcome stated; monthly cadence; stop any month.
5. "Who takes over when I'm done?" → Succession timeline tool and dedicated section.
