Questions owners ask

What happens in the first meeting?

We walk your floor with you, write down where the work waits, and name the one thing to fix first. You leave with a dated next step, not a binder.

How do we stop if it is not working?

Stop after any month with no penalty. Keep every page we wrote together.

Do you tell me to fire people?

No. We write who does what so the shop runs on a day you are out. If a role needs to change, you decide, at your pace.

What do I need to bring?

Your last twelve months of numbers, a list of who does what today, and an hour on your calendar. We bring the worksheets.
