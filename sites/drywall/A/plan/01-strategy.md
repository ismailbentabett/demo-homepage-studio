# drywall-A · Strategy (Knife & Hawk, Harrisburg PA)

1. **The one job.** A Harrisburg-area homeowner with ONE wall problem (doorknob hole, leak stain, nail pops)
   lands from "drywall repair near me" and, within 10 s, either taps the phone or opens the patch sizer and
   leaves with a Sample price band + visit count carried into the quote form. Primary: call. Secondary: "Price my patch".

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 + 1440).** Kicker "Drywall repair · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}}"; h1 "Patches that
   disappear under paint."; giant masked word "Three coats." (aria-hidden) filled with raw mud texture; call button
   ({{biz.phone}}) + "Price my patch" button; proof line: "Mon–Fri 7:30–4:30 · West Shore to Hershey · 4.9 Sample rating
   · photo after every coat".

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** The headline itself gets skimmed smooth: a knife blade passes across the hero and the
   rough-mud letters go flat, nearly vanishing into primer white (only a raking-light shadow stays). "The patch
   disappears" is shown, not said. Nobody in Harrisburg has a page whose surface is finished as you scroll →
   signature "Feathered Knife Pass".

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "I'll still see the patch when the sun hits it" → hero wipe + §Coats story (coat 3 + raking light check).
   - "What will a fist-size hole cost?" → patch sizer + price ledger (facts.prices, Sample).
   - "Why three visits for one hole?" → §Coats story (mud dries between coats) + sizer output "visits".
   - "Dust all over the house" → §Sand & prime (plastic at doorways, review Priya S.).
   - "My texture won't match" → texture matcher (test board first; review Tom H.).
   - "Do I wait until spring for these cracks?" → crack & nail-pop timer.
   - "Small job ignored / price changes" → guarantee band + review Alana W. ("price was what the text said").
   - "Do they come to Camp Hill?" → ZIP checker in the footer-adjacent "primed" section.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary tel: in header (desktop, tabular phone), hero, mobile bar left half. Secondary
   "Price my patch" → #sizer. Tools hand off: sizer → form prefill {service, size, surface, visits}; texture
   matcher → form {texture}; timer → form {timing: now | spring list}. Form = "final coat" on primed white (#contact).
   390: sticky bottom bar 64px: [Call] [Text] [Price it], phone also inline in hero above the fold.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a dark inspection room with a moving beam and GA-214 L0–L5 ruler (that is B).
   - Not era cross-sections, arches or brick-red color bands (that is C).
   - Not a numbered left-margin coat stripe (painter-A owns that): our progress lives in the surface itself and
     in knife strokes, never in a margin rail.
