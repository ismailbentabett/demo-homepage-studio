## Coats
- Kicker: How a patch disappears · h2: Four visits' worth of steps, in the order the mud allows.
- Step 1 Visit 1 · Cut it square and back it: Damaged board is cut back to a clean square. A backer strip goes in behind so the new piece can't sink.
- Step 2 Visit 1 · Tape every seam: New board screwed in, paper tape bedded in the first coat of mud on all four seams.
- Step 3 Visits 2–3 · Three coats, dry between: Each coat runs wider than the last so the edge fades out. Mud has to dry, so a fist-size hole is three short visits, not one long one. (video caption: A knife laying a coat of mud across the wall.)
- Step 4 Last visit · Sand under a side light, then prime: Plastic at the doorway first. Every coat checked with a work light held flat to the wall, then primed.
