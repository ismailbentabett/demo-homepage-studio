## #eras
- kicker: Three walls, three repairs
- h2: Every house here was built with the wall of its decade.
- I c. 1890: Horsehair plaster on wood lath. Wet plaster pushed through the gaps in the lath and curled behind it. Those curls, the keys, hold the wall up. When a crack runs and the wall sounds hollow, the keys have let go. Verdict: Keep · pin the loose plaster back to the lath, tape, skim.
- II c. 1950: Gypsum plaster on rock lath. Sheets of gypsum board took the place of wood strips, with two thin coats over them. Cracks run along the sheet edges, straight and tidy. Verdict: Pin · reset the edges, tape the joints, skim.
- III c. 2005: Board, paper tape and compound. Screwed to the studs, taped at the seams, finished with three coats. Nail pops and seam cracks show up after the first dry winter. Verdict: Replace only what failed · reset screws, re-tape, refinish.
