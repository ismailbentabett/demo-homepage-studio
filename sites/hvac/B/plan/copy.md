## 2 Story
- Kicker: Three sample readings
- h2: Same thermostat. Very different air.
- Step 1: "72 on the wall, 64 in the air" — The thermostat is happy. The dew point is 64, so sheets feel clammy and the closet smells musty. Turning it down makes it colder, not drier.
- Step 2: "A bigger unit made it worse" — An oversized system hits the set point in minutes and shuts off before the coil can pull water out. Cold blasts, damp rooms, a higher bill.
- Step 3: "Dehumidifier on the return" — Water comes out at the duct, not at the thermostat. Dew point 51: inside the band at the same 72.
