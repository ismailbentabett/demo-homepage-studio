# Strategy — hvac-C (Shadeline Heating & Cooling)

1. **The one job.** A Phoenix homeowner whose AC is struggling at 4 p.m. lands here, sees the shade-line story that mirrors their day, and calls or texts within 10 seconds because the page proves we work on the same schedule they do.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** Above the fold: giant condensed "115°" type mask with rooftop package unit photo showing through; kicker "{{biz.city}} {{biz.tradeLabel}}" in mono; primary button "Call now" linking to `tel:{{biz.phoneE164}}`; secondary "Text us" button; proof line "22 years · {{biz.areas[0]}}–{{biz.areas[2]}} · 4.9 Sample rating".
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The diagonal shade line splitting every section is something no Phoenix competitor has: it turns a generic HVAC page into a local clock that only someone who lives here would design. That becomes the signature.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "They'll sell me a whole new system" → Repair-or-replace sorter + sample price ranges in services.
   - "Nobody can come today" → Hours/open-now chip in hero + early-morning story section.
   - "I don't understand SEER/tons" → Size sanity check tool + plain-language service blurbs.
   - "Some guy in my attic will break something" → Crew/process photos with booties/drop cloth + review quotes naming care.
   - "It's 112 and I'm panicking" → Hot-day plan printable + monsoon checklist.
   - "Is this a real local company?" → ZIP checker + {{biz.city}} neighborhoods named in story.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: `tel:` button in sticky mobile bar + hero + footer. Secondary: SMS link (`sms:{{biz.phoneE164}}`) in hero and bar. Tool → form: symptom sorter and repair-or-replace end with pre-filled quote form. Phone always visible in bottom-right sticky bar on ≤ 768px.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - NOT a service-ticket timeline (that's hvac-A's clock rail).
   - NOT a psychrometric chart / dew-point tool-first hero (that's hvac-B).
   - NOT dark-led desert charcoal or light-led Gulf teal; this is color-led adobe terracotta with a sun/shade diagonal.
