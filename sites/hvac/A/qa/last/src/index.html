While you wait

Six things that help until the truck arrives.

  1. Close blinds on west-facing windows.
  2. Check the breaker and the outdoor disconnect.
  3. Set the fan to ON to move air (won’t cool, but helps).
  4. If ice is visible, turn cooling OFF and leave fan ON to thaw.
  5. Keep pets hydrated and away from the outdoor unit.
  6. Text us your address so dispatch can route the nearest tech.
