Plain answers before you sign anything.

Do I need my electric bill to book?

No. We start with the roof. A year of bills helps later, when we size the system, and you can bring them or not.

My foam roof is due for a recoat. Now or later?

We tell you at the roof check. Roof work goes first, then the panels. If panels are already up, we take them down, store them and put them back.

How do you keep a flat roof from leaking?

Mounts are sealed into the membrane or foam, ballast is used where the roof can carry it, and every penetration is photographed before it is covered.

What happens after the install?

City inspection, utility approval, then monitoring is switched on. After that we come back for inverter codes, wind and hail checks, critter guard and resets.

Will a battery run my well pump?

Often, yes, with the right battery and a start that the inverter can handle. Use the planner above, then we check the pump at the panel.
