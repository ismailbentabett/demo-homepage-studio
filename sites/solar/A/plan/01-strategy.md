## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in Sacramento solar shows the *roof*. Every competitor shows a bill and a sun. This page draws the rafters under the
shingles and makes the visitor watch the crew find each rafter before anything is bolted. That is the signature: the
snapped chalk line, the rafter ticks and the flashed mount, bolted section by section.
