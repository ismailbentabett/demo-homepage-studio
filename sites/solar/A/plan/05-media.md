## Keys
### hero → `rooftops-aerial` (Hero, right 6 cols; phone: strip under the buttons)
- Crop: `object-position: 62% 40%` (right-hand row of hip roofs with arrays sits in the roof plane; the street runs out of
  frame left). Clip: 5/12 roof-plane polygon `clip-path: polygon(0 100%, 100% 58%, 100% 0, 0 0)` on ≥1024, eave line is the
  rafter row. Phone: 16:9, same polygon flatter (`0 100%, 100% 72%, 100% 0, 0 0`).
- Treatment: site grade `saturate(.82) contrast(1.06) sepia(.06) hue-rotate(-4deg)`; overlay SVG on top: thin graphite
  outline traced over ONE roof (the house with the large array, right column) + mono tag "ROOF 01 · CHECKED FIRST" (decor,
  aria-hidden). Eager, preload, width/height set.
- Caption (mono, under the strip): "Hip roofs from above: arrays laid out around vents, ridges and hips."
- Alt: "Top-down view of a suburban street, several hip roofs carrying dark solar arrays."
- Weakness the critic will see: Canberra suburb, top-down, small houses; no Sacramento tree canopy. Mitigation: it reads as a
  site-survey view, which fits "roof first"; keep it at half-width, never full-bleed.

### detail-clamps → `hero-B3` (Mount detail section, beside the exploded mount drawing)
- Crop: frame 4:5, `object-position: 18% 78%`: only the lower-left rail ends, mid clamps and conduit run over the old tile;
  the town, palms and chimneys must be cropped out (they say Spain). On phones 1:1, same position.
- Treatment: site grade + `contrast(1.1)`; a drawn callout line from the visible clamp to the SVG drawing's clamp label.
- Caption: "Rail ends and mid clamps on an older roof: every clamp lands on a rail, every rail on a mount."
- Alt: "Close view of solar panel rail ends and clamps resting above weathered roof tiles."
- Note: tile, not comp shingle. Caption never claims it's a Sacramento job or a Rafter Line install.
