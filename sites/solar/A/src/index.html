MOUNT 01 / 09

Install in order.

  1. 01

    Walk the roof.

    We measure pitch, check decking soft spots, count vents and chimneys, and photograph every face.

  2. 02

    Shade walk.

    A sun-path tool reads your trees across the year. We move the array to the face that earns its keep.

  3. 03

    Panel layout.

    You see the exact placement on paper before anything is ordered: panels, conduit run, inverter spot.

  4. 04

    Permit and utility.

    We file the city permit and the interconnection application. You sign nothing we haven't explained.

  5. 05

    Rafters found.

    On install day we snap a chalk line over each rafter, mark the bolt point, and drill only into solid wood.

    [[img install-crew | lazy | class="step__img"]]
  6. 06

    Flashing set.

    Each mount gets a flashing plate under the shingle course above it. Water has a path off the roof, not into it.

  7. 07

    Rails and panels.

    Rails bolt to the mounts, panels clamp to the rails. Conduit runs clean to the inverter and panel.

  8. 08

    Inspection.

    The city inspector checks our work. The utility swaps your meter and approves interconnection.

  9. 09

    Monitoring on.

    Your system app shows what each day produces. We leave with the site clean and your questions answered.

What holds it to your roof.

Seven parts, one job: keep water out and panels on. Every mount is flashed under the shingle course above it, bolted into a rafter, and sealed.

01 Rafter
Solid wood the lag bites into. We find each one before we drill.
02 Lag bolt
Stainless, sized for the load. Torqued to spec, not guesswork.
03 Flashing
Aluminum plate under the shingle above. Water sheds over it, not around it.
04 L-foot
The bracket that carries the rail. Adjustable for pitch.
05 Rail
Anodized aluminum. Panels clamp to it; conduit clips to it.
06 Clamp
Holds the panel frame without piercing it. Mid-clamps and end-clamps.
07 Panel
Bolted to the rail, wired to the inverter, producing.
