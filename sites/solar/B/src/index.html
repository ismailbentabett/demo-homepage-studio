05 Inverter light reader

What is that light on the inverter telling you?

Pick the light you see. It tells you what it usually means and whether it can wait for a weekday call.

Green, steady: making power.

The inverter is on the grid and exporting. If the app still shows low numbers, a string may be down; that is a production check.

Can it wait? Yes.

Green, blinking: starting up or waiting.

Normal at dawn and dusk while it checks the grid. Blinking at midday for hours means it can’t connect: often a tripped breaker.

Can it wait? A day, yes.

Orange or yellow: a warning.

Often heat derating around 2 pm in July, or a ground fault reading. Write down the code on the screen before you call.

Can it wait? Call this week.

Red: a fault, it has stopped.

The system is making nothing. Don’t open the box. Photo the screen code and call; we read it with you on the phone.

Can it wait? No, call.

No light: no power to it.

At night that is normal. In daylight check the breaker and the DC switch beside it; then call if it stays dark.

Can it wait? Not past a sunny day.
