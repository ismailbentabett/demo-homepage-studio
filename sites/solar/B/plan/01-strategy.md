# solar-B · Plain Watt Solar · Tucson · strategy (fast)

1. **One job.** A Tucson owner (50-70, high July bill, already pitched by a phone/door seller) lands, sees that every
   number here names its source, and either **calls/texts** or **runs the 12-month size range** and hands it to the
   quote form. Target: understands the promise in 5 s, taps call or the tool in 10 s.

2. **Homeowner, first 5 s (390 + 1440).** Kicker: "Solar installer · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}}". h1: "Every number
   has a source." (h1 also carries trade+town via kicker, visually one block). Under it: the size-range tool input
   (annual kWh) with its result line + footnote ring ¹ "Source: your bill + sample production band". Buttons: Call
   {{biz.phone}} (primary) · "Size it from my bill" (secondary, scrolls to tool). Proof line: hours today · areas ·
   {{proof.rating}} (Sample rating, {{proof.reviewCount}}) · licence SAMPLE.

3. **Contractor, first 5 s.** Nobody in Tucson shows *where a number comes from*. The **Source Trace** signature:
   copper numbered rings on every figure, a leader line that draws to its source, a "Sources" strip closing each
   section. Plus "second opinion on a quote" as a front-door service: the anti-door-knocker company.

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "Pushy pitch, they'll lock me in" → Second-opinion section + Questions-to-ask checklist (tool).
   - "Their numbers were made up" → Hero tool + "How we size" story (bill → roof measure → band → range), each traced.
   - "Holes in my roof will leak" (tile/foam) → Tile-roof story step + Arturo review + service cards.
   - "Company gone in two years" → After-install list (inspection, utility approval, monitoring, production check)
     + {{proof.years}} years + licence SAMPLE.
   - "I don't understand what I'm signing" → Guarantee line ({{proof.guarantee}}) + checklist printable.
   - "Inverter dies in July heat" → Inverter light reader + Frank review.
   - "What does it cost" → Sample price table, each row with source ring "sample; real numbers from a site visit".

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: call (header, hero, mobile bar, footer). Secondary: text ({{biz.sms}}) in the
   mobile bar. Tools → form: size range prefills `service=Solar install` + `kwh`/`range`; light reader prefills
   `service=Inverters` + `light`; checklist prefills `service=Quote review` + ticked count. ZIP check above the form.
   390: sticky bottom bar (Call · Text · Size it), 56px, thumb zone; header phone icon too.

6. **What this is NOT.** Not A's roof-pitch diagonals/rail grid/process-in-order story (we are proof & tools, not a
   build sequence). Not C's sun disc, parapet steps or color-field warmth (we are dark-led, documentary, no sun icon).
   Not a sunny-sky photo hero at all: our right half is a graded panel-cell macro carrying drawn markers, not a
   rooftop vista either sibling would use.
