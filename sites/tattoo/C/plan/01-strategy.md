# tattoo-C · Strategy (Crane Light · Long Beach · LOCAL STORY)

1. **The one job.** A 19-45 Long Beach local (often on a phone, often on the same day, often near the water) lands
   wanting flash today or a cover-up looked at. Action: **text a photo / call to hold a flash sheet** within 8 s;
   secondary: book a custom/cover-up consult via the form. Walk-in = the hero action, not "Book now".

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (above the fold, 390 and 1440).** Kicker ribbon "{{biz.tradeLabel}} · {{biz.city}}";
   h1 "Flash off the wall, a block from the water." (≤ 8 words); proof line: today's hours ({{hours|today}}) ·
   flash {{proof.prices.0.from}}–{{proof.prices.0.to}} fixed on the sheet · 4.9 (Sample rating); one hygiene line
   "Needles opened in front of you, titanium in every piercing."; buttons **Call {{biz.phone}}** (primary red) +
   **Text us a photo** (sms:, secondary). Mosaic tiles carry the price + hours so the facts are visual, not paragraphs.

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Long Beach has a site that *looks like a flash sheet*: every price and
   answer is lettered on a traditional tattoo banner ribbon that unfurls as you read. That ribbon = the signature.
   Second hook: aftercare written for salt water and sun (a tide-line healing timeline), not a PDF.

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "What will it cost?" → Flash rail (fixed price per sheet) + price board (proof.prices, Sample).
   - "Is it clean / are the needles new?" → hero hygiene line + "Clean bench" strip (process steps, no invented logs).
   - "Can my old tattoo be covered?" → Cover-up check tool (3 honest answers) + Rudy's review.
   - "Will it stay bright in the sun / at the beach?" → Tide-line healing timeline (swim/sun days).
   - "Piercing gun? what metal?" → Piercing strip: needle, titanium, marked in the mirror (services 7-9).
   - "Will they rush me / make me feel dumb?" → reviews as banner notes + voice (plain, warm).
   - "Can I just walk in? when?" → hours table + open-now in header + ZIP/area checker ("how far from the water").
   - "Touch-up if it fades?" → guarantee line on a ribbon (facts.proof.guarantee) in the price board.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: tel: (header right, hero, sticky bar). Secondary: sms: "Text us a photo" (cover-ups,
   flash holds). Tools → form: flash board "Hold this sheet" → form step 1 prefilled `service=Flash walk-ins`,
   `design=No. NN`; cover-up check → form prefilled `service=Cover-ups`, `answer=...`; healing timeline → "Book a
   touch-up check". 390 screen: sticky bottom bar 64px, two halves: Call (red) | Text (cream), always visible after hero.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a process story of drawing layers / tracing sheets / vellum (A's lane).
   - Not a clinical log, steel trays, autoclave gauges, mono readouts, dark graphite (B's lane).
   - Not black-page-skull-blackletter, not an Instagram grid, not "Book now" with a 20-field form.
