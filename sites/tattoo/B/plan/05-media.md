# Media plan · tattoo-B (Cycle Log)

One photo, everything else drawn. The concept is a log book: drawings (SVG) are the honest medium for the
session sheet, needle pack, autoclave gauge, ear map and indicator tape. No stock photo of a face, an artist or
fresh work is honest for this direction, so none is used.

## Hero
- Drawn (no photo): the session sheet clipped to a dark stainless tray + a sealed needle-pack line drawing.
- `hero` slot → **hero-B-2** is reserved for this site (uniqueness + OG image), not placed in the hero.

## Slots
- **tray → hero-B-2** (approved). Gloved hand dipping a film-wrapped rotary machine into ink caps on a barrier-covered
  tray; single-use cartridges in their sleeve at left. Used once, as the full-bleed `.break` between the cycle story
  and the prices ledger. Crop 21:9 desktop / 4:5 phone. `object-position: 46% 52%` (glove + caps on the left third,
  the stickered drawer at the far left falls outside the crop on desktop; phone crop centres the glove).
  Grade: the site grade `saturate(.78) contrast(1.08) brightness(.96) hue-rotate(-4deg)`.
  Caption (describes the frame): "Gloved hand, machine in barrier film, single-use ink caps on a covered tray."
  Small machine lettering and drawer stickers are not readable at the crop size; a scrim covers the lower band.

## Rejected
- hero-B (prehistoric bone needle, museum object): off-topic, not a tattoo studio.
- hero-B-3 (bead design tray): jewellery craft, not piercing jewellery; draft.
- hero-A*, hero-C: sibling directions (A/C) and off-direction frames.
