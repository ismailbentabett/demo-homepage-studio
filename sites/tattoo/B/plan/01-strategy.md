4. **Objections → answer.**
   - "Is it clean, are the needles new?" → §Cycle (autoclave scroll story + hygiene log checklist).
   - "What will it cost / why a deposit?" → §Prices ledger + estimator with deposit math (`proof.prices`).
   - "Will it blur / too small?" → estimator rule "fine line under ~2 cm not advised" + Fine line service row.
   - "Can you cover this?" → Cover-ups row: three honest outcomes, "Text us a photo".
   - "Piercing gun? What metal?" → §Ear map (needle only, implant-grade titanium named, free downsize).
   - "Will they draw MY idea?" → §Services: Custom + Consults (written plan, sample price band).
   - "Will I feel stupid asking?" → FAQ in plain words + review entries (Tamika asking for the log).
   - "Is the touch-up extra?" → guarantee line (90-day touch-up, free downsize) in proof + footer stamp.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: hero [Book a consult] → #contact multi-step form. Secondary: [Text us] sms:, tel:
   in header. Tools: estimator "Book with these answers" → form prefill (placement, size, style, band);
   ear map "Hold a walk-in / book this piercing" → form prefill (piercing). ZIP check → "you're inside our area"
   → form. On 390: sticky bottom bar [Text] [Call] [Book], 56px, phone always one thumb away; header shows Book only.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's warm vellum, serif headlines, stacked tracing sheets or sticky-photo hero (no process-of-drawing story).
   - Not C's color-led harbor flash wall, condensed signage type, pinned sheets or photo-mosaic hero.
   - Not the competitor black page: no skulls, no blackletter, no red; dark here is clinical steel, not goth.
