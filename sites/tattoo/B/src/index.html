Entry 02 · Autoclave

The autoclave cycle

Your needles come sealed. You watch us open them.

Grips, tubes and tools that are used again go through one full cycle every time. Needles and ink caps never do: they are single use and go in the sharps bin.

  1. 01 · Load

    Bagged and strip-marked

    Grips, tubes and tools go into sealed pouches, each one with an indicator strip inside.

  2. 02 · Heat

    Up to 134°C under pressure

    The strip only changes colour if the chamber really gets there. Pale strip, cycle fails.

  3. 03 · Hold

    Held for the full time

    A short cycle is logged as a fail and run again. Nothing comes out early.

  4. 04 · Dry

    Dried in the chamber

    Pouches dry before the door opens, so nothing wet or torn reaches your tray.

  5. 05 · Log

    Written in the binder

    Cycle number, time and strip result. Your pouch carries the same number. Ask to see the page.

Questions people ask at the counter

Ask anything. Nobody here thinks it's a silly question.

Why do you take a deposit before I've seen a drawing?

It holds your date and pays for the drawing time. It comes off your final session, so it is not an extra charge. Sample range: {{proof.prices.5.from}}–{{proof.prices.5.to}}.

Will fine line blur in a few years?

Very small, very fine lines can spread as skin ages. If your idea is under about 2 cm we'll say so, and show you a size that still reads clean later.

Can you cover my old tattoo?

Text us a clear photo. You'll get one of three honest answers: it covers in one go, it needs a few laser sessions first, or a rework will look better than a cover.

Do you pierce ears with a gun?

Never. Every piercing is done with a single-use needle and implant-grade titanium or solid gold, and you come back for a free downsize once the swelling settles.

What if a spot heals patchy?

{{proof.guarantee}} Come in once it has healed and we fill it in.
