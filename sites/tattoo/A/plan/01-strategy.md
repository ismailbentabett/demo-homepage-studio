# tattoo-A · Strategy (Vellum & Needle · Brooklyn · axis: PROCESS)

1. **The one job.** A Brooklyn first-timer or cover-up client (19-45, phone in hand, a saved screenshot in the camera
   roll) lands from "custom tattoo artist brooklyn". In 10 s they must believe "they'll draw MY idea" and tap
   **Book a consult** (primary) or **Text a photo** (sms:, secondary). Phone call is tertiary (studio is appointment-first).

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (above the fold, 390 and 1440).** Kicker: "Custom & fine line tattoos · {{biz.city}}".
   h1 (Instrument Serif): "Drawn for you." (first of 3 sticky statements, visible without scroll). One line: styles
   done (custom, fine line, black & grey, cover-ups, lobe piercing). Hygiene line: "Single-use needles, opened in
   front of you." Buttons: Book a consult · Text us a photo. Proof line: hours today · 4.9 (Sample rating, 212).
   The healed fine-line forearm photo sits under a tracing sheet stamped "01 idea".

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Brooklyn shows the drawing *before* the skin: here the page itself is a
   stack of tracing sheets, and the sketch under them gets sharper each sheet you scroll (idea → drawing → stencil
   → lines → healed at 6 weeks). That's the signature: the Tracing Sheet Stack with register marks.

4. **Objections → answer section.**
   - "Will you draw MY idea or a Pinterest copy?" → Process stack (sheets 01-05) + Maya L. review (handwriting redrawn 3×).
   - "Will fine line blur in 5 years?" → Sheet 05 healed + style notes ("under 2 cm not advised") + 6-week check service.
   - "Is it clean / new needles?" → Hygiene strip in hero + Studio rules sheet (single-use, opened in front of you).
   - "What does it cost, why a deposit?" → Estimator (sample prices) + Deposit ledger tool.
   - "Can you cover my old one?" → Cover-up card + Darius W. review (tracing paper over photo).
   - "Will they rush me / make me feel stupid?" → Voice + Gwen P. review + consult described as 20 unhurried minutes.
   - "Piercing with a gun?" → Lobe piercing card: single-use needle, titanium, Shauna B. review.
   - "Do they come near me / am I near them?" → ZIP check (areas list) in the booking sheet.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Book a consult" → 3-step form (idea · placement & size · when). Secondary:
   "Text us a photo" (sms:{{biz.phone}}). Tools hand off: estimator → form prefilled with placement/size/style;
   healing timeline → "Book your 6-week check" prefill service; deposit ledger → "Book a consult". 390: sticky bottom
   bar 64px, two buttons: Book (primary, 60%) + Text (40%); call lives in header icon + footer. Bar appears after hero.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a dark clinical log book with mono readings and stamped checks (B's lane).
   - Not color-saturated flash sheets pinned with push pins, condensed sign lettering, walk-in energy (C's lane).
   - Not a black page/blackletter/skull, not an Instagram-grid portfolio, not an artist face hero (niche clichés).
