# Strategy — cannabis-A (Night Jar Cannabis Co.)

## 1. The one job of this page
An adult 21+ in Flagstaff or a visitor on the way to the canyon lands here, sees a calm dark-led shop that explains every step from door to sealed bag, and either pre-orders for pickup or calls within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Flagstaff cannabis dispensary" line above the h1.
- Hours today with open-now status.
- Two buttons: Pre-order and Call.
- One honest line: "We open the jar at the counter so you can see and smell before it is weighed."
- A filmstrip of five frames showing the visit path (jar → scale → seal → pickup).

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature system: **lamp stops**. Every section is a numbered pool of amber light on a deep night field, like lanterns along a dark-sky trail. No other dispensary site in town treats a visit as a lit path; they all do neon green menus. This looks like a place that respects the product and the person walking in.

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: Pre-order form (#contact) — appears in hero, mobile bar, and end of every tool.
- Secondary: [[tel]] in header, mobile bar, and next to hours.
- Tool hand-offs: first-visit walk-through → pre-order prefilled with "First-time guidance"; format explainer → pre-order prefilled with chosen format; visitor checklist → call CTA; ZIP checker yes → pre-order with ZIP; estimator → pre-order with size hint.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B's lab-report / batch-tag document (light-led, mono numbers, white field). We are dark-led and narrative.
- Not C's fog-dial coastal story (color-led teal→orange, shape-masked disc). We are pine-night and amber lamps, high desert not coast.
- Not a menu iframe or a wall of brand logos. We show the visit, not the catalog.
