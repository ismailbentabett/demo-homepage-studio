Your first visit, step by step

Nobody rushes you. Here is exactly what happens from the door to the sealed bag.

  1. 01 · ID at the door

    A valid government-issued ID showing you are 21+. Out-of-state IDs are accepted.

  2. 02 · The case

    Browse the glass cases. Jars are opened at the counter so you can see and smell before anything is weighed.

    [[frame first-visit-hands frame--inline]]

  3. 03 · Questions

    Tell the budtender what you are looking for. They will ask about how you prefer to use it and how strong you want it.

  4. 04 · The scale

    Your flower is weighed on a digital scale where you can read it. The price per gram is printed on the tag.

  5. 05 · Sealed bag

    Everything goes into a child-resistant sealed bag with your receipt. Tax is added at the register.

How each format works

Start low, go slow. Every package shows milligrams per serving in plain numbers.

Flower

Inhaled. Effects are felt within minutes. Sold by the gram; an eighth is 3.5 g.

Edibles

Eaten. Effects take up to two hours. Each piece is labelled with its milligrams.

Vapes

Inhaled from a cartridge or pen. Effects are felt within minutes. Lab sheet on request.

Tinctures

Drops under the tongue. Effects take 15–45 minutes. Milligrams per dropper written out.
