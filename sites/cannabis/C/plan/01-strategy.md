# Strategy — cannabis-C (Poppy & Fog)

## 1. The one job of this page
An adult 21+ in Santa Cruz or the mountain towns lands here, confirms they belong (right town, right age, calm shop), and either pre-orders before driving down Highway 17 or calls to ask a first-visit question — within 10 seconds of the page loading.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
Above the fold they must see:
- A 21+ confirmation line (plain text, no modal gate blocking content).
- The shop name and city: "Poppy & Fog · Santa Cruz".
- Open-now status or next opening time from facts hours.
- Two actions: Pre-order and Call, both thumb-reachable on mobile.
- One honest line about what a first visit feels like ("Quiet mornings, busy afternoons — come in when the fog lifts").
- The hero photo: coastal fog over California shoreline masked in a round disc, signaling the local weather rhythm.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature **fog dial** — a round disc at the edge of each section that turns from morning fog teal to afternoon poppy orange as you scroll, reading the shop's day from the facts hours. No competitor in Santa Cruz has a page that moves with the marine layer; it makes the site feel like a neighbor's note, not a dispensary template.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| "I don't know what to ask for" | First-visit walk-through (tool + step cards) |
| "Edibles hit too hard last time" | Format explainer / start-low card (serving size, timing, no effect claims) |
| "Is this tested?" | Lab sheet mention in product blurbs + "ask for the sheet" line |
| "What's the real price after tax?" | Sample price block with "before state and local tax" note + out-the-door estimate tool |
| "Can I use my out-of-state ID?" | Visitor checklist tool |
| "Will it feel sketchy walking in?" | Hero tone + store photos (bright, organized, glass cases) + reviews from locals |
| "Where do I park / how does curbside work?" | Pickup planner tool + curbside service blurb |

## 5. Conversion paths
- **Primary:** Pre-order button → multi-step form (service → details → when → contact). Appears in hero, sticky mobile bar, and after every tool.
- **Secondary:** Call button in header, mobile bar, and contact section. Phone token only.
- **Tertiary:** Text/SMS link (facts.sms = true) in mobile bar.
- Every service card carries `[[quote <short>]]` that prefills the form.
- ZIP checker answers "is this our pickup area" and hands off to the form with ZIP prefilled.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- **Not A (lamp stops / dark-led):** No dark pine-night palette, no amber lamplight pools, no numbered lantern steps. Direction C is color-led and warms down the page.
- **Not B (batch tags / light-led):** No lab-white field, no monospace numbers as the primary voice, no document-object hero. Direction C uses condensed sans headlines and a shape-masked photo hero.
- **Not generic dispensary:** No neon green, no leaf logo, no smoke clouds, no stoner puns, no third-party menu iframe, no brand logo wall. The fog dial and local place names are the identity.
