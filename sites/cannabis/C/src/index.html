Your first visit, step by step

Come in on a quiet weekday morning and we talk you through the case. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. 01

    ID at the door

    A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. Out-of-state IDs are fine.

  2. 02

    The counter

    Glass cases with jars opened right in front of you. Price tags are posted and do not change at the register.

  3. 03

    Questions we will ask

    What have you used before? What are you looking for today? We start everyone with the smallest serving on the pack.

  4. 04

    Paying

    Cash or debit. Tax is added at the register; sample ranges are posted so there are no surprises.

  5. 05

    Sealed bag

    Everything leaves in a sealed, child-resistant bag. Keep it out of reach while driving.

Common questions

Can I buy with an out-of-state ID?

Yes. Any valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older is accepted. Keep purchases sealed and out of reach while driving; cannabis is not allowed on federal land.

Do you take debit cards?

We accept cash and debit. Credit cards are not currently accepted due to banking restrictions. An ATM is available on site.

What should I bring on my first visit?

A valid ID and a form of payment. If you pre-ordered, bring the confirmation email or text. Nothing else is required.

How long do edibles take to notice?

Edibles can take up to two hours. We start everyone with the smallest serving printed on the pack and write the timing down. Inhaled formats are noticed within minutes.

Can I see the lab sheet before buying?

Yes. The certificate of analysis for every product is available at the counter. Ask for the sheet of any jar or cartridge.
