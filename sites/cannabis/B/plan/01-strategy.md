# Strategy — cannabis-B (Batch & Date Cannabis)

## 1. The one job of this page
An adult 21+ in Kalamazoo lands here to decide whether to drive over or pre-order now; they must see test dates, strength and price per gram above the fold and be able to start a pre-order or call within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- A plain "You must be 21+ to enter" line (no modal, no product imagery above it).
- The trade and town: "Cannabis dispensary in Kalamazoo, MI".
- Open-now status and today's closing time.
- Two buttons: Pre-order and Call.
- One honest line: "Every jar shows its test date, THC % and price per gram on the tag."

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The hero is a shelf tag / lab-sheet card with real fields (batch code, test date, THC %, top terpenes, sample price per gram) that looks like something no other local shop puts on their homepage. It signals: this store runs on numbers, not vibes.
