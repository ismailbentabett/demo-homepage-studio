# Strategy — tile-A (Pitch to Drain Tile Co.)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Miami homeowner who suspects their shower is leaking or hates their old tile must trust that we build showers in layers that keep water out, see a price range, confirm we serve their ZIP, and call or text a photo within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + city: "Shower & tile installation in Miami" in the h1.
- Proof line under the h1: hours today · service area · Sample rating.
- One reason to believe: the hero shows wet work (water on tile, membrane, trowel) not a spa.
- Way to act: sticky mobile bar with Call and Price it; desktop header CTA.
- Next band peeks in at the bottom of the viewport so they know there is more.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The layer-stack ruler running down the left margin. It is a vertical cross-section of a shower wall (stud → backer → membrane → thinset → tile) that fills as you scroll. No competitor shows the build-up; they show finished bathrooms. This signature says "we know what goes behind the tile."

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| Will it leak again? | hero (layers thesis), process (flood test), guarantee strip |
| Are the walls flat / big tiles flush? | process (backer + slope), large-format card |
| What does it cost? | estimator tool, sample price block |
| Do you come to my town? | ZIP checker in area section |
| Will you disappear mid-job? | process timeline (named days), reviews with detail |
| Dust and mess? | process demo step, review mentioning containment |
| Is the grout going to turn black? | regrout/seal card, review mentioning humidity |
| Can I trust the price won't double? | estimator ranges labelled Sample, form hand-off |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `tel:` from sticky mobile bar and header; multi-step quote form at `#contact`.
- Secondary: ZIP checker → prefill form; estimator → prefill form; service cards → prefill form.
- Every CTA above `#contact` points to `#contact` or `tel:`. Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B (Oakland, light, numbers-first, inspection sheet). We do not use gauge readouts or lippage sliders as the hero.
- Not C (Chicago, warm serif, hex mosaic map). We do not use vintage nostalgia or neighborhood maps.
- Not a gallery of finished bathrooms. The finished tile is the reward at the end of the process, not the opening image.
