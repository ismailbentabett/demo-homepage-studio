THE FIVE LAYERS

What sits behind your tile

Every shower we build follows these five layers in order. The flood test takes 24 hours. We don't set tile until it passes.

01

Studs

Plumb and flat. Day 1.

02

Backer board and slope

Slope to the drain, no low spots. Day 2.

03

Liquid membrane + flood test

Orange coat, then water stands 24 hours. Days 3–4.

04

Thinset, full coverage

Notched trowel, back-butter big tiles, leveling clips. Day 5.

05

Tile, grout, seal

Straight lines, tight grout, sealed against humidity. Days 6–7.
