# Strategy — tile-B (Feeler Gauge Tile Works)

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner in Oakland with an older house lands here because a floor is uneven, a shower leaks, or they want big tiles that don't lip. Within 10 seconds they must see: the trade, the town, a way to price their room, and proof that we measure before we set. The action is "Price it" (form) or call; text-a-photo is secondary.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Tile Installation in Oakland" in the h1.
- A reason to believe: a mono readout under the h1 ("Flatness ≤ 1/16" over 10 ft · Sample rating 4.8").
- A way to act: primary CTA "Price my room" anchored to #contact; phone visible in header and mobile bar.
- Visual proof: a large-format porcelain floor in raking light with spec tags ("lippage ≤ 1/32", "95% coverage") in a tight mosaic.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature: every claim carries a gauge readout (target / measured / tool) like a punch-list ticket. The whole page sits on a 1/16"-gap tile grid where grout lines are the gutters. No other tile site in Oakland looks like an inspection sheet.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| Big tiles will have edges sticking up | hero (lippage tag), flatness story, services (large-format) |
| Is the floor flat enough? | flatness story (raking-light scroll), estimator (floor prep line item) |
| Will the shower leak again? | services (waterproofing + flood test), reviews (Kenji W.) |
| What will it cost? | estimator (sample ranges from facts), price block |
| Do they come to my ZIP? | area section with ZIP checker |
| Are the cuts going to be ugly? | services (backsplash layout, pattern center line), reviews (Rosalind P.) |
| Dust and mess? | process notes in services, review (Mateo S. mortar coverage) |
| How long will it take? | open-now + hours, form step "when" |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price it" buttons → #contact (multi-step form with service prefill via data-service).
- Secondary: tel: in header, mobile bar, and beside form microcopy.
- Tool hand-off: estimator ends with "Send these numbers" → form pre-filled with room size and service; ZIP checker "yes" offers the form with ZIP.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT tile-A's dark process cutaway with layer-stack ruler (we are light-led, proof-first, not process-first).
- NOT tile-C's warm hex-mosaic local story (we are cool, exact, number-driven; no nostalgia).
- NOT a gallery of finished bathrooms with no measurements; every photo carries a spec tag or appears inside the estimator/story context.
