What sits under your tile

A shower that leaks in two years was built wrong from the stud out. Ours are built in layers you can check.

  • Backer board screwed to studs, no flex.
  • Slope to drain so water never pools behind the tile.
  • Waterproof membrane rolled on and flood-tested for 24 hours before any tile goes down.
  • Thinset and tile set with full coverage, edges flush.
  • Grout sealed so January steam stays on the surface.

Every layer is visible before we close the wall. Ask for photos.
