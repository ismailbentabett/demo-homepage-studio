My water is cloudy. Is it safe to swim?

Cloudy water usually means low chlorine, a tired filter or high calcium. Hold off until it is tested. One visit finds which reading is out and starts bringing it back.

Do I really need a new salt cell?

Often not. We clean the cell and measure its output first. You only get a replacement price when a clean cell still reads low.

My pump is loud. Does it need replacing?

Not always. Bearings, seals and capacitors fail on their own. We name the part, write down the cost and labor, and tell you if the whole pump can wait.

Can you work around my rental guests?

Yes. Visits are timed to check-ins, and every turnover gets a dated photo and readings you can show a guest or a platform.

What does the weekly report show?

All seven readings, each on a scale next to its ideal range, what we added, the filter pressure, and a photo of the pool that day.
