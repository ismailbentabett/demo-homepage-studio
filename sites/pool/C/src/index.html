11November · ResurfacingJFMAMJJASOND

Pick the right month to pour

Pebble and plaster cure best when nights stay above 50° and days stay under 90°. Summer pours crack and chalk.

October–April: pour

Nights above 50°, days under 90°. Pebble gets the slow cure it needs. Ask for a November date when you call.

May–September: wait

Decks over 110° flash-cure the mix and leave hairline cracks and a chalky finish. A summer pour is the one we talk people out of.
