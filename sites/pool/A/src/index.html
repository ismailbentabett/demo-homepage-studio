08:00 Visit log

Thirty minutes at your pool, line by line

  1. 08:02

    Skim and brush

    Leaves, pollen and storm debris come off the surface first. Then the walls, steps and waterline tile get brushed so the chlorine can reach them.

    [[frame process-1 log-thumb log-thumb--skim | data-stamp="08:02" data-reveal="clip" | alt="A leaf net lifted dripping out of a blue backyard pool beside the house wall" eager]]

  2. 08:09

    Test the water

    pH, free chlorine, alkalinity and stabilizer read on site, from water drawn at elbow depth, away from the return jets.

  3. 08:15

    Balance it

    Only what the 08:09 reading calls for goes in: chlorine, acid or stabilizer. Each one is written on your report with the amount, next to the after photo.

    [[frame process-3 log-thumb log-thumb--after | data-stamp="08:15 after" data-reveal="clip" | alt="After balancing: clear turquoise water around a steel pool handrail and a stone edge" eager]]

  4. 08:22

    Baskets and pressure

    Skimmer and pump baskets emptied. Filter pressure read on the gauge. If it is high, the cartridge gets cleaned or the sand gets backwashed.
