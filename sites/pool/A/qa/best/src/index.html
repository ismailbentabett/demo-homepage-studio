Thirty minutes at your pool, line by line

  1. 08:02

    Skim and brush

    Leaves, pollen and storm debris come off the surface first. Then the walls, steps and waterline tile get brushed so the chlorine can reach them.

    [[frame process-1 log-thumb | data-stamp="08:02" | alt="Pool brush working along a wall below the waterline"]]

  2. 08:09

    Test the water

    pH, free chlorine, alkalinity and stabilizer read on site, from water drawn at elbow depth, away from the return jets.

    [[frame process-2 log-thumb | data-stamp="08:09" | alt="Test vials of pool water with chlorine and pH drops added"]]

  3. 08:15

    Balance it

    Only what the readings call for goes in: chlorine, acid or stabilizer. Each one is written on your report with the amount.

    [[frame process-3 log-thumb | data-stamp="08:15" | alt="Chlorine tablets in a floater on clear pool water"]]

  4. 08:22

    Baskets and pressure

    Skimmer and pump baskets emptied. Filter pressure read on the gauge. If it is high, the cartridge gets cleaned or the sand gets backwashed.
