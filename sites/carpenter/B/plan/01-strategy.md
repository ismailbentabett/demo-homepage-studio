# Strategy — carpenter-B (Story Pole Carpentry)

1. **The one job.** An Albany homeowner with an old-house repair or built-in idea lands, sees sample prices and a measurement tool, and calls or fills the quote form within 10 seconds because nothing is guessed.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 "Measured before it is priced" + kicker "Albany trim, built-ins & stair carpentry"; primary CTA [[call]]; secondary CTA [[quote]]; one proof line "{{proof.reviewCount}} sample reviews · {{proof.years}} yrs in {{biz.city}}" with Sample tag.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** A vertical story-pole ruler runs the left margin with real rowhouse heights marked; every section snaps to a mark. Nobody else in Albany shows tolerances and measurements as the design language.
4. **Objections → sections.**
   - "They'll rip out old trim" → Repair-or-replace check tool + rot story section.
   - "Deposit taken, no show" → Hours/open-now + schedule band + sample guarantee.
   - "Paint over rot" → Rot repair service card with probe-and-measure copy.
   - "New piece won't match" → Molding profile picker tool + casing/crown cards.
   - "Is the porch/stair safe?" → Stair fix finder + rail/baluster cards.
   - "Can I afford this?" → Sample price ranges on every service card via estimator hook.
   - "Will it fit my weird house?" → Built-in planner tool + custom pieces card.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: [[call]] in header, hero, mobile bar, footer. Secondary: [[quote]] scrolls to #contact with data-service prefill from any service card or tool output. Tool → form hand-off: each tool ends with a CTA that pre-fills service + ZIP. Phone always ≤ 1 thumb-reach on 390 via sticky mobile bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's process filmstrip / cut-list numbering (we do not show cuts or joints).
   - Not C's triple-decker floor bands / dark porch-light mood (we are light-led, drafting-vellum calm).
   - Not a gallery-first portfolio site; tools and measurements lead, photos support.
