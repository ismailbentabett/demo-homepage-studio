# Strategy — nursery-B (Sun Hours Nursery)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Polk County homeowner lands here with a bare yard or a dead plant and must know within 5 seconds that this nursery sells plants sized and priced for their exact spot, delivers and plants them, and is open right now; they must tap call or text before scrolling past the fold.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Plant Nursery · Lakeland, FL" in the header and hero kicker.
- Open status: live "Open now · Closes 5 pm" or next-hours pill beside the phone.
- Tool-first hero: the "Will it live in my yard?" check visible above the fold with four big tap choices (sun hours, soil, watering, wind).
- One reason to believe: "Trees and shrubs we plant are replaced once if they fail in the first 12 months" as a short guarantee line under the lead.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The sun-hours dial signature: a drawn sun-path arc over a house roofline that sets the hero input, repeats as mini dials on every service card, drives the price-table pot-size rings, and shows open hours as a lit arc. No other site in the library uses a daylight dial; it makes the sales demo look like a tool nobody else has.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "I'll spend $300 and half die by August" | Hero tool (matches plants to sun/soil), Guarantee strip, Proof reviews |
| "The tag says full sun but our sun is different" | Hero tool (sun-hours input), Services (each blurb names sun need) |
| "That tree will lift the driveway in ten years" | Trees service card (mature height on every tag), Photo check tool |
| "Is it in stock or am I driving for nothing?" | Hours / Open-now strip, Services list, Delivery section |
| "I'll look stupid asking a basic question" | Photo check tool ("Text us a photo"), Voice throughout, Reviews quoting real questions |
| "Sand drains instantly, nothing lives" | Natives service card, Soil & pots section, Review from Lake Wales |
| "Delivery is too expensive / unclear" | Sample price block, Delivery service card, Estimator hand-off |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `tel:` button in sticky mobile bar and header; form at `#contact` reached from every CTA.
- Secondary: SMS link (`sms:`) in Photo check section and mobile bar; ZIP checker → pre-filled form hand-off.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bottom bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A (Hardening Bench): no dark greenhouse mood, no week-counter rail, no bench-card rows, no mono-accent type.
- Not C (Fog Season): no warm orchard palette, no serif-led type, no almanac ribbon, no seasonal color bands.
- Not a florist or wedding site: no bouquet heroes, no script fonts, no pastel palettes; this is a working nursery with data.
