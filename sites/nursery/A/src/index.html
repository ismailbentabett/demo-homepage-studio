THE HARDENING PROCESS

Six weeks from cutting to ready

  1. 01

    Week 0–2: Rooting under mist

    Cuttings sit in the propagation house until roots fill the plug. No sun stress yet.

  2. 02

    Week 3–4: First bench time

    Pots move to the open bench. Wind, cool nights and real sun start the hardening.

  3. 03

    Week 5–6: Ready tag

    Roots checked, foliage hardened. Only then does it get a price tag and a bench card.

PLANTING CALENDAR · {{biz.city}}

When to plant what

Fall (Oct–Nov): Natives and shrubs

Rain returns and soil stays warm. A strong window for manzanita, ceanothus and coffeeberry.

Winter (Jan–Feb): Bare-root fruit trees

Apples, pears and stone fruit arrive bare-root. Soak, prune and plant while dormant.

Spring (Mar–May): Perennials and veg starts

Salvias, yarrow, tomatoes and peppers hardened off outdoors so they do not sulk after planting.
