## process
- kicker: THE HARDENING PROCESS
- h2: Six weeks from cutting to ready
- step1 title: Week 0–2: Rooting under mist
- step1 body: Cuttings sit in the propagation house until roots fill the plug. No sun stress yet.
- step2 title: Week 3–4: First bench time
- step2 body: Pots move to the open bench. Wind, cool nights and real sun start the hardening.
- step3 title: Week 5–6: Ready tag
- step3 body: Roots checked, foliage hardened. Only then does it get a price tag and a bench card.
