# Concept — nursery-A

## Thesis
Nothing leaves the bench until it has lived outside here.

## Signature
The bench card. Every section is a row on a numbered growing bench with its own card: "Row N · Week X · Stage · Last night's low". Services are bench rows; reviews are grower notes clipped to the row end; prices are the ready rack. A running week counter in the margin advances as you scroll.

## Hero
Horizontal filmstrip under the headline: seed tray → rooted cutting → 4-inch pot → gallon on open bench → root check → tagged ready. Slow left drift, pauses on hover/focus, static with reduced motion. Headline sits on deep soil-black with warm scrim; proof line under h1 shows hours · area · sample rating.

## Motion
- Intro: filmstrip frames fade up like grow lights clicking on.
- Scroll story: thin bench rail down left margin; week counter ticks forward at each section; plant icon grows from seedling to gallon.
- Pinned only ≥ 640px; stacked cards with clip reveal on phone.

## Palette
- deep: #1A1814 (soil-black)
- paper: #E8E4DC (chalky mist)
- accent: #D4872E (grow-lamp amber)
- ink: #2C2822 (wet slate)
- rule: #9C9488 (bench rail)

## Photo mood
Low-key, close, dawn or overcast light in greenhouse or under shade cloth. Mist, condensation, seed trays, roots, hands potting. Dark backgrounds, one warm source, shallow focus. No sunny retail displays.
