# Strategy — nursery-A (Hardening Bench Nursery)

## Buyer
Homeowners in Sonoma County (Santa Rosa, Sebastopol, Windsor, Rohnert Park, Healdsburg, Petaluma, Kenwood) who have lost plants to clay, deer, or box-store shock and want stock that has already lived through local weather before purchase.

## Objection
"I'll spend hundreds on plants and half die by August because they were trucked in yesterday."

## Path
Show the hardening-off process as the product: every plant spends weeks on open benches in real sun, wind, and cool nights before it is tagged ready. The site follows one flat from seed tray to sale, with bench cards marking each stage.

## Differentiator
Process-led nursery site where the proof is time-on-bench, not a smile or a slogan. No other site in the library uses a bench-card signature or a dark-led propagation-house tone.

## Tools
- Weeks-to-ready tracker (bench timeline per service)
- Planting calendar for Santa Rosa (fall native window, bare-root window)
- ZIP checker
- Multi-step quote form

## Tone
Dark-led: deep soil-black and wet-slate base, misted glass gray, warm grow-lamp accent, chalky white notes.

## Type
Mono-accent: monospace for week numbers, bench rows, sizes, sample prices; humanist sans for headlines and body.
