# Strategy — nursery-C (Fog Season Nursery)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Modesto homeowner lands here in any season and, within 8 seconds, sees what to plant *this month* in their valley yard, trusts that Fog Season knows the local chill hours and heat, and either calls/texts or starts a delivery quote.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Plant Nursery · Modesto" in the kicker above the h1.
- Season signal: the almanac ribbon shows the current month highlighted; the hero mosaic reads fog / bloom / summer shade / harvest at a glance.
- Way to act: sticky mobile bar with Call and Text; desktop header has phone + "Get a planting list".
- Reason to believe: "Bare-root trees that do not leaf out by May are replaced one time" visible in the hero lead.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody else in Stanislaus County runs a site organized as the valley's garden year with an almanac ribbon tracking months across every section. It reads like a seed catalog written for this zip code, not a generic garden center template.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "I'll spend $300 and half die by August" | Guarantee strip under hero + proof reviews naming survival through July/August |
| "I don't know what grows here" | Planting calendar tool (hero-adjacent) + services organized by season |
| "That tree will lift my driveway" | Mature-size check tool in shade trees section |
| "Is it in stock or am I driving for nothing?" | Hours/open-now strip + "Call/text to confirm availability" on every CTA |
| "I'll look stupid asking a basic question" | Reviews quote real questions answered plainly; voice never talks down |
| "Delivery? Planting? Or just drop-off?" | Delivery & planting section with sample price range + estimator |
| "When do I prune / plant / buy bare-root?" | Almanac ribbon answers timing per section; pruning lessons tied to winter |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: multi-step quote form at `#contact` (planting list / delivery / planting). Every CTA above points to it or `tel:`.
- Secondary: text line ("Send a photo of the sick plant") linked to `sms:`; appears in help/advice section and mobile bar.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away via sticky mobile bar on phones.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A (Hardening Bench): no dark greenhouse aesthetic, no bench-card system, no week counter. This site is warm, color-led, seasonal.
- Not B (Sun Hours): no tool-first hero, no sun-hours dial, no bright Florida light. This site leads with the valley's story, not a calculator.
- Not a florist/wedding site: no bouquet heroes, no glossy cut-flower imagery. Photos are orchard rows, backyard beds, real nursery benches.
