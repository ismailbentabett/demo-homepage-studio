04/30Car stuck inside

Before you pull the red cord, look up.

Tap what you see above the door. We’ll tell you if it’s safe to lift by hand, or if it’s a phone call.

[[i stop]] A gap in the spring: stop. Don’t lift it.

With the spring broken, the door carries its full weight, often more than two people can hold. Leave it closed, keep kids and pets clear, and call. We’ll tell you how to get the car out.

[[call btn--burnt]]

[[i stop]] A loose cable: stop. Don’t lift it.

A cable off its drum means one side of the door is hanging on nothing. Lifting it by hand can drop it or jam it in the track. Leave it down and call.

[[call btn--burnt]]

[[i stop]] A crooked door: stop. Don’t lift it.

A door off its track can come out of the opening if it moves. Don’t pull the cord and don’t run the opener. Call, and we’ll brace it before anyone touches it.

[[call btn--burnt]]

[[i check]] Opener hums: you can lift it by hand.

  1. 1Close the door all the way if it moves at all.
  2. 2Pull the red cord down and toward the back of the garage.
  3. 3Lift with two people, hands flat on the panels, never in the seams.
  4. 4If it feels heavy or starts to drop, let it down slowly and call.

[[i check]] Nothing wrong you can see: try it by hand.

  1. 1Unplug the opener so it can’t start mid-lift.
  2. 2Pull the red cord down and toward the back of the garage.
  3. 3Lift with two people. It should feel light and stay put halfway.
  4. 4If it feels heavy or drops, the spring is tired. Let it down and call.

08/30The repair, turn by turn

How a spring gets changed, in five moves.

  1. 30 → 00

    Let the tension off

    A steel bar goes in the winding cone and the old spring is let down a quarter turn at a time. Never by loosening a bolt and hoping.

  2. 00

    Swap the pair

    Both springs come off, even if only one broke: the other has done the same number of cycles. The new pair is sized to your door’s weight on site.

    {{proof.prices.0.from}}–{{proof.prices.0.to}} {{proof.prices.0.note}} [[sample pricing]]

  3. 00 → 30

    Wind thirty quarter turns

    Two bars, one quarter turn each, counted out loud. A two-car door usually wants about thirty. Watch the painted line twist into a spiral.

  4. 30 · balanced

    Let go halfway

    The opener is pulled off and the door is lifted by hand to waist height, then let go. A balanced door stays put. If it drifts, we adjust and try again.

  5. ✓ reverse test

    Put a box under it

    The door comes down on a real obstacle and has to go back up. Then we show you the broken coil, so you know what you paid for.
