# 01 Strategy: garagedoor-A (Brightrail Door Co., Dallas) — axis PROCESS

1. **The one job.** A Dallas-area homeowner whose spring just banged (car inside, work at 8) lands on a
   phone, sees "spring snapped → we lift it today", a sample spring range and the call button, and taps
   call or text in < 8 s. Planned buyers (opener, hail panels) price it with the estimator or the form.
2. **Homeowner, first 5 s (390 and 1440):** kicker "Garage door repair · {{biz.city}} & 7 suburbs";
   h1 "Spring snapped? Car inside? We lift it today."; primary `tel:` Call {{biz.phone}}; secondary
   "Price my repair" (#quote); proof line: "Mon–Fri 7–6 · Sat 8–2 · Torsion spring pair $260–$420
   (Sample) · 4.9 ★ (Sample, 214)". The 21:9 spring strip under it, top of next band peeking.
3. **Contractor, first 5 s:** the page *is* the repair. A drawn torsion spring in the left margin winds
   as you scroll and counts quarter turns 0 → 30; every section lands on a turn mark. Nobody in DFW
   shows the winding count. That is the signature.
4. **Objections → section**
   - "Is it safe to touch?" → Safe-release guide (stop panel = call) right after hero.
   - "$300 spring, $4,000 door upsell" → Process story step "balance test" + price sheet: repair ranges
     beside new door range, Hannah B. review ("nobody tried to sell me a new door").
   - "Trip fee, come back with the part" → Process step 1 + services: "parts on the truck" phrased as
     process ("we carry spring pairs sized on site") — no fee claims (not in facts).
   - "Car stuck, work at 8" → hero h1 + Luis M. review + hours/emergency line.
   - "Do you serve my suburb?" → ZIP checker + area list in coverage band.
   - "Will it fall on my kid/dog?" → Story step 5 safety reverse test + sensor service.
   - "How long do springs last?" → Spring age estimator.
5. **Conversion paths:** primary call (header, hero, stop panel, mobile bar); secondary text (`sms:`);
   tools: estimator result "Book a spring check" → prefills form step 1 = spring + estimate note;
   safe-release stop panel → call; ZIP ok → form ZIP prefilled. On 390 the phone sits in the sticky
   bottom bar (Call | Text | Price) from first paint, plus hero button in first screen.
6. **NOT:** no spec-plate/mono readings as the main voice, no symptom-finder hero (B); no weather/
   thermometer rail, no video hero, no blue field (C); no light-paper lead, no galvanized gray look.
