Heard a bang from the garage?

[[i stop]] Don’t touch the spring.

A torsion spring holds the whole weight of the door. Leave it to us, and do these four things while you wait.

  1. 01

    Keep hands off the spring and the cables, even if one is hanging loose.

  2. 02

    Pull the red release cord only if the door is down and sitting level.

  3. 03

    Unplug the opener so the motor stops straining against a dead door.

  4. 04

    Text us a photo of the gap in the coil. We’ll know which spring to bring.
