The spring’s life, in cycles

Every open is one cycle. A standard spring is rated for about 10,000.

Torsion springCC-02

Cycles10,000

Rated (std.)10,000

Broken · call · don’t lift it

Drawing · one torsion spring on its shaft, winding cone at left · wire, length and inside diameter get measured before we quote

  1. 0 cycles

    Wound to your door’s weight.

    A new spring is sized to the door, not to whatever happens to be on the truck. Too light and the opener does the lifting; too heavy and the door flies up.

  2. 5,000 cycles

    Four trips a day is about 1,460 cycles a year.

    Out to work, home, out for groceries, home. A standard spring is halfway through its life in under four years. You won’t hear it coming.

  3. 8,000 cycles

    Measured before we touch it.

    Wire size, length, inside diameter. All three go on the quote next to the cycle rating, so you can see what heavier wire buys you.
