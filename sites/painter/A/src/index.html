02–04 Most of the job happens before the color

Paint fails at the layer under it. Here is what goes on, and comes off, before any color.

  1. 02 Washdry: 1 day

    Wash

    Siding, brick and porches washed gently to lift mildew and grime, then a full day to dry. Paint over dirt is how a four-year job peels in two.

  2. 03 Scrapedust: wet, contained

    Scrape & sand

    {{services.0.blurb}}

  3. 04 Primedry: 2–4 h

    Spot-prime, then two coats

    Primer on bare wood and stains so the top coat bonds and hides what was underneath. Then two full coats, never one thick one.

Dry time Primer needs 2–4 h, and we never coat over damp wood. A wet morning moves the day; it never shortens the dry.
