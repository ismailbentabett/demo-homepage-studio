# Strategy — painter-A (Cutline Painting Co.)

## 1. The one job
Homeowner with peeling paint or a move-in deadline lands, sees the prep-first promise, and calls or texts within 10 seconds to price the job.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- H1: "Richmond house painting that starts with the prep" (trade + city + outcome).
- Proof line under h1: "Mon–Fri 7:30–5 · Henrico, Glen Allen, Midlothian · 4.9 (Sample rating)".
- Primary button: "Price it" → #contact. Secondary: "Call (804) 555-0119".
- Sticky photo of a crisp cut line stays fixed while three statements scroll past: "Wash. Scrape. Prime." / "Then two coats." / "Then we leave."

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in Richmond shows the boring part as the product. The page is the coat schedule itself: every section is a numbered coat with a dry-time stamp and a film-build stripe that thickens as you scroll.

## 4. Objections → sections
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| They'll paint over peeling stuff | 00 Wash + 01 Scrape (process story) |
| Paint on my floors / shrubs | 00 Protect (photo + list) |
| Old house lead worry | Old-house paint check tool + 01 Scrape note |
| One coat stretched as two | 04 Coat one + 05 Coat two (dry-time stamps) |
| Quote doubles when rot found | Wood repair service card + estimator range |
| Don't know how long it takes | Coat schedule tool (hero-adjacent) |
| Price per room unclear | Room quote builder + sample price list |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact), prefilled by service/ZIP/tool.
- Secondary: sticky mobile bar with Call + Text; visible in header at desktop.
- Tool → form hand-off: coat schedule ends with "Price this schedule"; old-house check ends with "Get a lead-safe number"; ZIP yes → form with ZIP prefilled.
- Phone always one thumb-reach away on mobile via sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT B's color-led tint-ticket lab (no swatch strips, no drawdown bars, no receipt-printer motion).
- NOT C's dark-led Charleston facade walk (no haint blue, no street row, no historic plaques).
- NOT a generic "quality you can trust" painter site with swatch fans and smiling rollers.
