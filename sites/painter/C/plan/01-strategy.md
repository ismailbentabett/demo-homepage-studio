# Strategy — painter-C (Haint & Harbor)

## 1. The one job
Charleston homeowner with a historic or coastal house lands, sees their street's palette and salt-air timing reflected, and calls or texts within 10 seconds to price an exterior, porch ceiling or shutter job.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- H1: "Historic House Painting in Charleston" (trade + town).
- Kicker above: "Haint & Harbor Paint Co."
- Proof line under h1: "Mon–Fri 7–4:30 · Peninsula & Sea Islands · 4.9 ★ (Sample rating)".
- Two buttons: "Price it" (primary, anchors to #contact), "Call (843) 555-0121" (secondary, tel:).
- Hero: horizontal filmstrip of pastel facades + shutters + haint-blue porch ceiling drifting slowly; pauses on hover.

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
The page is a walk along a row of Charleston facades: each section is a house front drawn in SVG, painted in its historic color, with a small plaque naming the street feeling, the salt/sun enemy, and the service that fits. Nobody else in town shows painting as a *street story*.

## 4. Objections → sections
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "They'll paint over peeling stuff" | Prep story (house #2): scrape/prime/wood repair shown inside that facade |
| "Salt air eats paint fast" | Coastal timing story (house #4): humidity/dew notes + weather window tool |
| "Wrong color for the street" | Historic color explorer tool inside house #1 + palette swatches |
| "Porch ceiling mildew returns" | Haint blue story (house #3): wash/mildewcide primer/two coats |
| "Quote doubles when rot is found" | Sample pricing block with from/to ranges labelled Sample |
| "Lead paint in old house" | Old-house note in prep story, plain words, no credential claim |
| "Will they protect my floors/shrubs" | Interior story (house #5): drop cloths, careful cut lines |
| "Don't know if they serve my island" | Service area + ZIP checker at the end of the row |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact); appears in hero, mobile bar, and at end of row.
- Secondary: "Call" / "Text" buttons with tel:/sms: links; phone always in header and sticky mobile bar.
- Tool hand-off: historic color explorer and weather window each end with "Price this job" that pre-fills the form service field via data-service.
- Phone placement: header right, mobile bar bottom (sticky under 900px), and inline in proof line.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT Direction A's coat-schedule/process timeline (no numbered steps, no dry-time stamps, no light-led primer white field).
- NOT Direction B's tint-ticket/lab-document system (no printed labels, no color-led saturated bands, no calculator/sheen-picker tools).
- NOT a generic painter template: no smiling painter hero, no fan-deck swatches, no "quality you can trust", no brand logos, no before/after sliders.
