# Strategy — painter-B (Drawdown Painting Co.)

1. **The one job.** A Raleigh-Cary-Apex homeowner lands, sees a sample price range and gallon count for their room in under 8 seconds, and clicks "Price it" or calls to book a visit.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 names trade + town ("Raleigh painters who show the numbers first"); a tint-ticket hero displays coverage, sheen and a sample range; primary CTA is "Price it", secondary is "Call (919) 555-0120"; proof line: "4.8 · 236 reviews (Sample rating)".
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** Nobody else in Raleigh prints a tint ticket with gallons, sheen and a sample range before the call; the page itself is the drawdown lab that answers the cost question upfront.
4. **Objections mapped.** "Quote will double" → Room quote builder + sample prices section. "Paint over peeling" → Prep scope listed on every service ticket. "Floors and shrubs" → Protection step in process tickets. "One coat called two" → Coat count and dry time on every ticket. "Wrong sheen" → Sheen picker tool. "Lead in old paint" → Old-house paint check tool. "When can I use the room" → Dry-time output in tools and service blurbs.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact) with service prefilled. Secondary: sticky mobile bar with Call + Price it; ZIP checker yes-result hands off ZIP to form. Tools (calculator, sheen picker, quote builder) each end with a "Price it" CTA that pre-selects the service.
6. **What this direction is NOT.** Not A's process-coat-schedule narrative (no stamped dry-time stripes, no prep-first storytelling). Not C's Charleston facade filmstrip (no historic color explorer, no dark-led palette, no horizontal scroll). This site is a numerate lab, not a story or a street.
