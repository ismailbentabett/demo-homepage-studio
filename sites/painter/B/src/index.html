DD-03 Prep

Four layers go on before the color does.

  1. 01 Wash

    Pollen, mildew and chalky old paint rinsed off at low pressure, then a full day to dry.

  2. 02 Scrape & patch

    Loose paint scraped back to sound wood, edges feathered with 80-grit, nail pops filled.

  3. 03 Prime

    Bare wood, patches and stains sealed with a bonding or stain-blocking primer.

  4. 04 Cut in, then two coats

    Edges brushed by hand along trim and ceilings, then two rolled finish coats.
