Insurance, step by step

Your insurer can recommend a shop. You still choose one.

Pick what happened. These are the steps in order, and who acts at each one.

Someone hit me

  1. You

    Get the other driver’s insurance and open a claim with their company. Write down the claim number.

  2. You

    Tell the adjuster which shop you picked. Their list is a suggestion.

  3. {{biz.short}}

    We photograph the damage and send the photos and our estimate to the adjuster.

  4. Insurer

    The adjuster reviews it and approves the first estimate.

  5. {{biz.short}}

    Once the panel is off, hidden damage shows. We send the supplement and handle the back-and-forth.

  6. You

    Pick it up and check the paint match with us outside, in daylight.

It was my fault

  1. You

    Ask us for a price first, so you can compare it with your deductible before you claim.

  2. You

    Claiming? Open it with your own insurer and name the shop you chose.

  3. {{biz.short}}

    We send photos and the estimate to your adjuster.

  4. Insurer

    They approve the estimate. Your deductible is the part you pay us.

  5. {{biz.short}}

    Anything found after teardown goes to them as a supplement, sent by us.

  6. You

    Daylight check of the match, then the keys.

Storm limb or debris

  1. You

    Photograph the car or boat where it was hit, with the limb or debris still in the frame if it’s safe.

  2. You

    Open the claim with your insurer and tell them the shop you chose.

  3. {{biz.short}}

    We check whether the paint cracked. If it didn’t, the dent can often come out paintless.

  4. Insurer

    The adjuster approves the estimate we send.

  5. {{biz.short}}

    Dent out, scratch leveled and polished, and any supplement sent for you.

Parking lot, no other car

  1. You

    Text us photos: one wide, one close, one at an angle.

  2. {{biz.short}}

    We reply with a range, so you can decide whether a claim is worth it.

  3. You

    Paying yourself? Book a look. Claiming? Open it and name us as your shop.

  4. {{biz.short}}

    Door dings and cart dents pushed out; scratches filled, leveled and polished in.
