   "Send photos" (form) within 8 s. Restoration buyers: price the hull/coating first, then text.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 and 1440).** Kicker: "{{biz.tradeLabel}} · {{biz.city}}". h1: "Salt and sun
   take the shine off the car and the boat. We bring both back." Sub: paint correction · ceramic coating · gel
   coat · collision. Buttons: Call {{biz.phone}} (primary) + "Send photos" (secondary → form). Proof line:
   hours today · {{proof.rating}} Sample rating · "Your insurer can't pick the shop for you." (plain, brief §1 fear 1).

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** One shop page that treats the car in the driveway and the boat at the canal dock
   as the same job (salt + UV on a finish), split by a hull's boot stripe. Nobody in Fort Lauderdale shows cars
   and hulls on one waterline, with a hull-length price tool. → signature "Boot Stripe".

4. **Objections → sections.**
   - "Insurance will make me use their shop" → claim path tool (you choose the shop, who does what, days).
   - "App estimate low, who pays the rest" → claim path step "supplement after teardown, we send it".
   - "Will the paint match" → services row "Paint match" + collision note (blend into next panel, noon-light check from review).
   - "Will you burn through my clear / scratch my boat" → process story: test spot first (fact in services blurb + review).
   - "What does my hull cost" → hull size calculator (per-foot sample range).
   - "Is coating worth it here / how long" → restoration planner + guarantee (2-year fix-it, fact).
   - "Do you come to the dock?" → review mentions dock work; we only say "at your dock" where facts show it (review quote, not a claim).
   - "Do you serve me" → ZIP checker in the season/area band.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: call (tel:). Secondary: "Send photos" → 4-step quote form (service → car/boat
   → photos/notes → contact). Tools hand off: hull calc → form prefilled service=Gel coat + length; planner →
   service=Ceramic coating/Paint correction; claim path → service=Collision + situation; ZIP yes → form with ZIP.
   SMS link beside call. 390: sticky bottom bar (Call | Text | Send photos), phone also in header right.

6. **What this is NOT.**
   - Not a shop-at-night process walk with station numbers and tape labels (A's lane): no dark booth, no stations.
   - Not a test-sheet of gauge readings / mils / mono-everything (B's lane): numbers only where facts give them.
