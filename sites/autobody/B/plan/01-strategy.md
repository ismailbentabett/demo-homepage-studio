# autobody-B · Basin Light Body & Paint · El Paso · Strategy

## 1. The one job
An El Paso driver with a peeling roof, a hail-dented hood or a claim number lands on a phone at lunch.
In ≤ 10 s they know: this shop measures before it sells, it works with their insurer and *they* pick the shop,
and the call / "text photos" button is under their thumb. Action: call or text photos (70%), price it via sorter → form (30%).

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds (above the fold, 390 and 1440)
- Kicker (mono): "Auto body & paint · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}}". h1: "Peeling, chalky or just dull? Tap your paint."
- The sun-damage sorter IS the hero: 4 drawn paint tiles (dull / chalky / peeling / bare) → fix + sample reading + sample range.
- Buttons: Call {{biz.phone}} (primary) · Text us photos (secondary, sms:).
- Proof line: "Mon–Fri 7–5 · Sat 8–1 · 4.9 from 168 reviews (Sample) · Your insurer, your choice of shop".

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in El Paso shows a paint-depth number before the phone call. Every section carries a probe readout
(mils, dent count, days, sample $) — the shop looks like it measures everything. This is the signature.

## 4. Objections → sections
1. "Does insurance make me use their shop?" → hero proof line + Claim path tool ("You choose the shop" is step 0).
2. "App said $1,400, will it become $4,000?" → Claim path step "Teardown → supplement, we call the adjuster, not you".
3. "Will the paint match?" → Daylight match band (facts.proof.guarantee, spray-out card + blend + full-sun check).
4. "Will you buff through my clear?" → Story "One hood, four readings" (gauge before polishing, test spot first).
5. "Do I need paint or just polish?" → Hero sorter + Efrain review ("just a polish").
6. "Is this dent paintless?" → Dent check tool.
7. "What does it cost?" → Sample price sheet (facts.proof.prices only, all "Sample").
8. "Do you come to my side of town?" → ZIP check + areas list.
