# autobody-A · Strategy (Booth Nine Collision, Detroit)

1. **The one job.** A Detroit driver, rear-ended or slid into a curb in salt season, on a phone in a lot with a claim
   number and a low app estimate, must **call or send photos** within 10 s, convinced the claim will not be a black box.
   Secondary buyer (salt-rusted rockers, mismatched door) prices it via the dent check / quote form.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 + 1440).** Kicker in tape: "Detroit collision repair · Station 1 of 9".
   h1 "Nine stations. You hear from us at every one." Under it one line: "We work with every insurer, and the
   shop is your choice, not theirs." Buttons: **Call {{biz.phone}}** (primary, tape yellow) + **Send photos**
   (secondary → #quote step 1 = photos). Proof line: hours today · Detroit + 7 towns · 4.8 (Sample, 214).
   Below: the filmstrip of the 9 stations peeking into the fold.

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Every section is labelled in masking tape written in marker, the way his own
   techs label panels on the floor: "STN 4 · Measure · Day 2 · You get a text". Nobody's site in Detroit shows
   the shop floor's own labelling system as the navigation. That tape is the signature.

4. **Objections → where answered.**
   - "Will insurance make me use their shop?" → hero sub-line + Claim path tool (#claim), step 1 "You choose".
   - "App said $1,400, will it become $4,000?" → Station 3 "The supplement call" in the story + claim tool step.
   - "Will the paint match?" → Station 7 (booth) + Hiba's review + test-card note in services.
   - "How long without my car?" → stations story: a text at each station; Marek's "station 6, day 4" review.
     No invented day counts.
   - "Is the frame bent?" → Station 4 measure, printout in your folder (facts service blurb).
   - "Will the rust come back?" → salt-rust band (Detroit winter), Gerald's review, guarantee.
   - "Who stands behind it?" → guarantee band: covered as long as you own the car + licence + insured.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: call (header right, hero, sticky mobile bar left half, footer). Secondary: send
   photos → quote form (#quote, step 1 photos, step 2 what happened, step 3 contact). Text (sms) in mobile bar
   right half. Tools hand off: claim path → form prefill `situation`; dent check → form prefill `service` +
   `size`; ZIP check → form `zip`. On 390 the sticky bar (Call | Text photos) sits at the bottom thumb zone,
   appears after the hero buttons leave the viewport.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not B's lab sheet: no gauge readings, no mono numbers, no light/noon test-sheet look.
   - Not C's coast: no boats, no seasons calendar, no serif, no teal; restoration is a station, not the theme.
   - Not a shiny-car gallery: no hero car, no before/after slider, no carrier-logo strip.
