# autobody-A · Media plan

**This build uses no photos.** The autobody media library (`niches/autobody/media/manifest.json`) was still
downloading when the build ran, so `src/media.json` has no slots and nothing is hot-linked or faked.

What stands in, all drawn as inline SVG from one sprite (shapes only, no text in the drawings):
- **Door skin** (front door, side view) in five finishes: dented old paint, bare steel with featheredge + filler,
  guide-coat primer, flat basecoat red, clear gloss.
- **Nine station overlays**: check-in viewfinder, teardown bolts and tape, hidden bent bar on a phone, measuring
  points, filler and sanding rings, sanding block, masking paper and tape in the booth, polisher pad, wash drops.
- **Rocker cross-section** for the salt break: rot, cut line, new steel, weld bead, seam sealer.

Photos come in the fix round: hero filmstrip frames (masking, sanding, primer, booth, polisher) from the approved
library, cool booth grade `saturate(.78) contrast(1.08) brightness(.96) hue-rotate(-4deg)`; the drawn door panel
stays as the stations story's subject. OG image: plain deep colour until a hero photo exists.
