The nine stations, in order

STN 1-9 · The floor

Your car moves through nine stations. Each one ends with a text.

No hold music, no chasing the adjuster. When a station is done, the tape comes off and your phone buzzes.

1/9

Check-in

  1. STN 1 · Check-inDone · photo set sent

    We photograph the car with you standing there.

    Every panel, the damage up close and the odometer. The same photo set goes to your insurer.

    You hear: a text with the photos and your work order number.

  2. STN 2 · TeardownDone · what we found

    Bumper, lamps and trim come off.

    Now we see what a photo estimate from an app can't: clips, brackets, the bar behind the cover.

    You hear: a text when it's apart, with what we found.

  3. STN 3 · The supplementDone · adjuster called

    Station 3 is the phone call nobody likes. We make it, not you.

    If we find a bent bar behind the bumper, you get a photo before anyone gets a bill, and we take the extra to the adjuster.

    You hear: the photo, and what the insurer said.

  4. STN 4 · MeasureDone · printout sent

    Is the frame bent? We measure, we don't guess.

    We measure the car against its factory points on a frame bench, pull it back where it needs it, then measure again. The printout comes home with you.

    You hear: the measuring printout, by text.

  5. STN 5 · Metal and plasticDone · metal finished

    Dents pulled, rot cut out, covers repaired.

    A cracked bumper cover gets repaired when a repair will hold, and replaced when it won't. Bare steel gets a thin skim of filler and is sanded flush.

    You hear: a text when the metal work is done.

  6. STN 6 · Prime and blockDone · ready for paint

    Primer, guide coat, then sanding by hand.

    A dusting of black guide coat shows every low spot. We block it until none is left under the booth lights.

    You hear: that it's going into the booth.

  7. STN 7 · The boothDone · colour on

    Will the paint match? We check before it goes on.

    Mixed to your car, sprayed on a test card, held against your car, then blended into the next panel so there's no line to see.

    You hear: a photo of the test card beside your car.

  8. STN 8 · PolishDone · gloss checked

    Same gloss as the panel next to it.

    Clear coat cut and polished, then looked at again outside, because booth light is kinder than a parking lot.

    You hear: a time to pick it up.

  9. STN 9 · Wash · Keys backDone · come get it

    Washed, then walked around with you, in daylight.

    You see the repair outside before you sign anything. The measuring printout and the photos go home in your folder.

    You hear: "It's ready." Then your keys.

Station 3, before it starts · the claim

Your insurer pays for the repair. Where it's done is your choice.

Pick what happened. You'll see who does what next: you, your insurer, or us.

Do you have a claim number yet?

Not yet: call us before you call them. We'll tell you what to ask for, and which shop to name.

Got one: read it to us on the phone. We send the photos to the adjuster from there.

Someone hit me

  1. You

    Get the other driver's name, insurer and policy number. Take photos of both cars where they stopped.

  2. Insurer

    Their insurer (or yours) opens the claim and gives you a claim number.

  3. You

    Tell them the car goes to {{biz.short}}. Their preferred list is a suggestion, not a rule.

  4. {{biz.short}}

    Check-in photos and a written estimate go to the adjuster. You get a copy.

  5. {{biz.short}}

    Anything hidden turns up at teardown: we photograph it and call the adjuster for the supplement.

  6. Insurer

    Approves the repair and the supplement. You see every photo they see.

It was my fault

  1. You

    Call your own insurer and ask whether you have collision cover, and what your deductible is.

  2. Insurer

    Opens the claim and may send you an app for a photo estimate. That number is a first guess.

  3. You

    Name {{biz.short}} as your shop. It's your car and your policy.

  4. {{biz.short}}

    We tear it down, photograph what the app missed, and send the supplement to the adjuster.

  5. You

    Pay your deductible at pickup. The insurer pays us the rest.

Deer or pothole

  1. You

    Photos of the car and the spot. With a deer, a police report number helps.

  2. Insurer

    Tells you which part of your policy it falls under. Deer hits and potholes are often handled differently.

  3. You

    Choose the shop. Grille, lamps and radiator support are common after a deer, so ask for a teardown, not a guess.

  4. {{biz.short}}

    We check the cooling parts and the sensors behind the bumper before we price it.

  5. {{biz.short}}

    Supplement photos to the adjuster for anything found behind the cover.

Parking lot, no other car

  1. You

    Send us photos before you call anyone. A ding or a scrape can cost less than your deductible.

  2. {{biz.short}}

    We text back a range, and tell you plainly if a claim is worth filing.

  3. {{biz.short}}

  4. You
