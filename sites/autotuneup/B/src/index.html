Conventional, blend or full synthetic

Your owner’s manual sets the spec. We match it, write the viscosity on your receipt, and stick the next change on the windshield by mileage, not by date.

Ranges vary by make and driving. Your spec wins.

Tread depth and wipers, checked in your driveway

Run a quarter across three grooves. If the tread meets Washington’s head, you’re above 4/32. Lift each blade: if the rubber is cracked or streaks after one pass, swap it before the next storm.
