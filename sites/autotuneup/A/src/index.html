Which oil does your car need?

The one your owner’s manual names. Here is how the three types differ, in plain words.

Type Conventional Synthetic blend Full synthetic
How often Shortest; your manual sets it In between Longest the manual allows
Cold mornings Thicker at start-up Flows better Flows fastest on an inversion morning
Older engines Fine when it’s the spec A common middle choice Holds up longer under heat
When we pour it The manual says conventional The manual allows either The manual calls for synthetic

We read the spec off your manual or the oil cap. No upsell to a better bottle.
