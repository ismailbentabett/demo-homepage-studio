Is it safe to drive?

Three quick checks that tell you what to do next.

Tap the light you see on your dash. We'll tell you what it usually means and whether to drive in or stop.

  • Check engine (steady)

    Usually a sensor or emissions part. Safe to drive in; book this week.

    Diagnose it
  • Check engine (flashing)

    Active misfire. Stop driving and call — this damages the catalytic converter fast.

  • Brake / ABS

    Brake fluid low, worn pads, or an ABS sensor. Drive gently and book today.

  • Temperature

    Overheating risk. Pull over if the needle climbs. Call before restarting.
