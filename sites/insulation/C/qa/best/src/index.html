How an ice dam happens

The ice at your gutter was made in your attic.

  1. 1 Warm air leaks up through ceiling lights, hatches and wall tops.
  2. 2 It warms the roof deck and melts the snow from underneath.
  3. 3 The water runs down to the cold eave and refreezes into a ridge.

Seal the leaks and the snow sits evenly, like your neighbour’s roof.

Asked at the attic hatch

The questions people ask before we climb up.

Is the gravel-looking stuff in my attic asbestos?

It may be vermiculite, which can contain asbestos. Leave it alone, keep kids out, and get a lab test. We stop work, explain the test and the options, and never blow insulation over it until you know.

Will you cover my vents and cause mould?

No. Baffles go in at every eave first so the soffits keep breathing, and bath fans get ducted outside, not into the attic.

Will someone’s foot come through my ceiling?

We walk on boards laid across the joists, not on your drywall. Drop cloths run from the door to the hatch, and we vacuum on the way out.

Can you do this in the middle of winter?

Yes. Blown-in and air sealing work fine in January. Spray foam needs warm surfaces, so we heat the space first or plan it for a milder week.

Will dense-pack holes show on my clapboard?

Where we can, we drill behind a lifted course of siding. Where we can’t, the holes get plugged and painted to match.
