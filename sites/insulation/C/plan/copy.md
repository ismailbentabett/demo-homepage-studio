## FAQ
- h2: The questions people ask from the attic hatch.
- Q1 Is the gravel-looking stuff in my attic asbestos? A: It may be vermiculite, which can contain asbestos. Leave it alone, keep kids out, and get a lab test. We stop work, explain the test and the options, and never blow insulation over it until you know.
- Q2 Will you cover my vents and cause mould? A: No. Baffles go in at every eave first so the soffits keep breathing, and bath fans get ducted outside, not into the attic.
- Q3 Will someone's foot come through my ceiling? A: We walk on boards, not drywall, lay drop cloths from the door to the hatch, and vacuum when we leave.
- Q4 Can you do this in the middle of winter? A: Yes. Blown-in and air sealing work in January. Spray foam needs warm surfaces, so we heat the space or plan it for a milder week.
- Q5 Will dense-pack holes show on my clapboard? A: We drill behind a lifted course of siding where we can, then plug and paint the rest to match.
