How the job runs02

No mud pit left behind. Five steps, in order.

01

Locate

We call 811 and wait for the paint marks before any machine touches dirt.

02

Stake & laser

Grade stakes set the cut and fill; a rotating laser checks every pass.

[[frame process-1 center|class="step-frame"]]
03

Cut & haul

Topsoil stripped and stockpiled or hauled; rock broken if ledge shows up.

04

Compact in lifts

Fill placed 6–8 inches at a time and packed with a plate compactor.

05

Finish grade & seed

Final rake pass at 5% away from the house, then straw and seed on bare soil.
