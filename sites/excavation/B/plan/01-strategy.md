# Strategy — excavation-B (Rod Reading Excavation)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Fayetteville homeowner with a sloped, rocky lot lands here needing a driveway, pad or drainage fix; they must trust that we measure before we dig and call or price-it within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Excavation & driveways in Fayetteville" in the h1.
- A way to act: sticky mobile bar with Call and Price-it, plus an in-hero dirt calculator.
- One reason to believe: a laser-red level line crossing the hero with a live reading, and "Your final bill stays within 10% of the written number" beneath the h1.
- Service area proof: ZIP checker visible at the top of the scroll.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature rod-reading system: a thin laser-red beam crosses every section, each header is a grade-rod reading in monospace, and the dirt calculator actually returns cubic yards and truckloads. No other local excavator site shows numbers this cleanly.
