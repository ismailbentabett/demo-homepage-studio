# Strategy — excavation-A (Batter Board Excavating)

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner in Springfield, MO with a wet yard, rocky lot or new build pad lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds because they see their exact problem named, a price range that matches their fear, and proof that the crew finishes what it starts.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- **Trade + town:** "Excavation & backhoe work in Springfield, MO" in the h1.
- **Way to act:** sticky mobile bar with `tel:` and a "Price my dig" button that jumps to `#contact`.
- **Reason to believe:** a grade-stake signature showing the current step ("C 2.4 · foundation cut") and a line: "Every dig staked, cut, checked and closed."
- **Their problem named:** kicker reads "Wet yard · Rocky ground · New pad" so they self-select before the fold ends.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The diagonal-split hero seam is drawn at the exact 1:1 batter slope of a real foundation cut; the grade-stake signature repeats as a margin rail with surveyor marks (C/F/0.0). No other excavation site in the library uses a process-axis signature tied to real cut/fill notation.
