# siding-B · Fieldtest Siding Co. · Strategy

1. **The one job.** A Hartford-area owner (colonial, cape, Victorian two-family, 60s split-level) lands asking
   "which siding for my house, and what will it cost?". Within 8 s they see a sample $/sq ft range and a
   way to get their own number: measure (squares calculator) → compare → book a measure visit. Call is
   always one tap; the measure-visit quote is the main action.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (above the fold).** Kicker `REPORT No. 01 · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}} · SIDING`;
   h1 "Which siding for a Hartford house? We tested five." (city via token); cover strip of 5 material
   labels each with a mono sample range ($6–12 … $14–24 /sq ft, Sample); buttons "Measure my house"
   (primary → #squares) + "Call {{biz.phone}}"; proof line: Mon–Fri 7:30–5 · 8 towns · 4.9 (88) Sample.
3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Hartford publishes a scored, side-by-side test of every siding
   with the price per square foot. The five-cell score bar on every section is the signature: the site
   looks like an independent report, not a brochure.
4. **Objections → sections.**
   - "Vinyl will look cheap and wavy" → #compare (Look criterion) + review James T. (straight courses).
   - "No idea what it costs" → hero cover strip + #prices price sheet (Sample).
   - "How much siding do I need?" → #squares calculator (squares + waste + sample range).
   - "They'll cover rotten wood" → #method "Under the old siding" (sheathing priced before it starts; per-sheet line).
   - "I only want two walls done" → #elevation picker → quote form prefill.
   - "Which one for an old house / lead paint?" → #compare Cedar row + #faq pre-1978 line.
   - "Will they leave a mess / take weeks?" → #method steps + #faq.
   - "Insurance hassle" → #faq: we write scope + photos, you talk to the insurer.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: squares calculator → "Book a measure visit with these numbers" → #quote
   form step 1 prefilled (squares, material). Secondary: elevation picker → walls list prefilled into form.
   Tertiary: header + sticky mobile bar (Call · Text · Quote) at bottom of 390 screen, 56px, always visible;
   ZIP checker in #area hands its ZIP to the form.
6. **What this is NOT.** Not A's dark wall-building process story (no dusk photos, no layer stack, no
   courses metaphor). Not C's colour-led era pattern book (no serif, no ornament, no nostalgia, no
   type mask). Not a manufacturer brochure: no brand logos, no "lifetime" type, no storm urgency.
