## 2 · Compare (p. 02)
- Header row: TEST 01-05 · FOUR MATERIALS · SAMPLE READINGS
- h2: The results, side by side.

- Criteria: Impact & hail · Cold & moisture · Upkeep · Look up close · Price per sq ft (5 = lowest)
- Notes per material:
  - Vinyl: Lowest price and no paint, ever. Dents in hail and can wave on a long wall in sun.
  - Fiber cement: Takes knocks and holds paint. Heavy, so it needs a crew that handles long boards well.
  - Cedar clapboard: The real thing for capes and Victorians. Needs paint every 7 to 10 years.
  - Engineered wood: Wood look, lighter than fiber cement, long factory-painted lengths for ranches.

## 5 · Method (p. 04)
- Header row: TEST 07 · THE JOB · 4 PAGES
- h2: What happens under the old siding.
- Pages:
  1. Measure every wall. We measure each wall, gable and opening, then give you squares and four prices side by side.
  2. Tear-off and sheathing check. Old siding comes off one wall at a time. If the sheathing under it is soft, you see the per-sheet price before we replace it.
  3. Wrap and flashing. New house wrap, taped seams, and flashing bent around every window, door and deck ledger.
  4. Courses and trim. Starter strip level, courses straight, corners and trim tight. The yard is magnet-swept each night.
