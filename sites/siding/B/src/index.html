Test 07The job4 pages

What happens under the old siding.

Four pages, in the order the work happens. Nothing new goes on until the wall behind it has been checked.

  1. Page 1 of 4Measure

    Measure every wall.

    Each wall, gable and opening gets measured. You get the squares and four material prices side by side, on paper.

    1/4

  2. Page 2 of 4Tear-off & sheathing

    Tear-off, then a sheathing check.

    Old siding comes off one wall at a time. If the sheathing under it is soft, you see it and the per-sheet price before we replace it.

    2/4

  3. Page 3 of 4Wrap & flashing

    Wrap and flashing.

    New house wrap with taped seams, and flashing bent around every window, door and deck ledger before a board goes up.

    3/4

  4. Page 4 of 4Courses & trim

    Courses and trim.

    Starter strip level, courses straight, corners and trim tight. Nails are swept with a magnet at the end of each day.

    4/4

Five questions owners ask

Will you work with my insurance claim?

We write the scope with photos of each wall. You talk to your insurer; we answer the adjuster's questions about the work.

What if the wood under my siding is rotten?

You see it, and the per-sheet price, before anything is replaced. No new siding goes over soft wood.

How long is the house open?

One wall at a time. A wall that comes off in the morning is wrapped and flashed before the crew leaves.

My house was built before 1978.

Painted wood gets lead-safe prep: plastic on the ground, wet scraping and HEPA cleanup.

Who cleans up?

Old siding is hauled off as it comes down, and the yard is swept with a magnet at the end of each day.
