# siding-A · Strategy (Coursework Siding Co., Minneapolis)

1. **The one job.** A Minneapolis-area owner of a 1950s-90s rambler/bungalow with wavy, dented or soft siding lands
   from "siding replacement minneapolis" / "hail damage siding repair". In ≤ 10 s they must see that Coursework opens
   the wall and checks it before re-siding, see a sample price range, and either **call** or **price it** (squares
   calculator → quote form). Text is the quiet third path (facts: sms true).

2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds (above the fold, 390 + 1440).** Kicker "Siding · {{biz.city}} & 7 nearby towns";
   h1 "Built in courses. Checked in layers."; one line: vinyl, insulated vinyl, fiber cement, hail repair; buttons
   **Call {{biz.phone}}** + **Price my walls** (anchor #squares); proof line: hours today · {{proof.rating}} (Sample)
   · Sample re-side from {{proof.prices.0.from}}. The drawn wall section beside the photo already shows a soft sheet
   flagged under the wrap: the sheathing answer is visible before reading.

3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The page *is a wall being built*: every section is a 7-inch lap course with a
   drip-shadow under it and a nail line at its top that the next course covers as you scroll. Nobody in the Twin
   Cities shows the sheathing, wrap and flashing; this site is made of them. That is the signature.

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "They'll cover rotten wood" → #layers story, layer 2 "Sheathing checked" + price-per-sheet note + review Ingrid N.
   - "It'll look cheap and wavy" → course & reveal visualizer (#reveal) + insulated-vinyl review (Beth A., "straight in January").
   - "Insurance won't cover it / cash for the rest" → #storm checklist: owner talks to insurer, we write scope + photos; review Craig O.
   - "Nails and old siding in the yard" → #layers step 1 tear-off line "swept for nails twice a day" (review Gary P.) + course list.
   - "It'll take weeks in plastic" → #layers: wrap is covered the same week; review Gary P. (nine working days, told each evening).
   - "No prices" → #prices course board, 5 Sample ranges from facts, rotten sheathing priced after tear-off.
   - "Pre-1978 paint" → one plain line in #layers step 1 (lead paint on old trim changes handling; no credential claimed).

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: tel button (hero, header ≥ 900, mobile bar). Secondary: "Price my walls" → squares
   calculator → "Send these numbers" prefills the quote form (#contact) with squares + material + sides. Tertiary:
   sms link in mobile bar and contact. ZIP checker in the areas course hands the ZIP to the form. On 390: fixed
   bottom bar (Call · Text · Price it), 64px, appears after hero buttons leave view; hero call button is the first
   button, in view at 390×844 without scroll.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a lab/test report (B): no score bars out of 5, no mono readings table as the core, no clipboard sheets.
   - Not a pattern book of eras (C): no serif era plates, no fishscale/shingle swatches as backgrounds, no painted-lady color.
   - Not a light editorial cover: dark winter-evening field, one ember accent, drawings carry the material color.
