# Strategy — landclearing-C

## 1. The one job of this page
A Lynchburg-area landowner who just inherited or closed on overgrown pasture or a sloped wooded lot lands here, understands in under five seconds that we reclaim old farms without tearing up the red clay, and calls or prices the job before scrolling past the fold.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in plain words: "Land clearing in Lynchburg" above the h1.
- The promise tied to place: "We put old farms back the way they were, one fence row at a time."
- A way to act: `[[call]]` button and a ZIP checker immediately visible.
- One reason to believe: a photo of a reclaimed Piedmont hillside with broomsedge and a split-rail fence, plus a proof line "Mon–Fri 7–5 · Forest, Bedford, Amherst · 4.7 (Sample rating)".

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature: a drawn split-rail fence timeline that runs through every section, each rail representing a season (leaf-off, spring rain, summer resprout check, fall grass). No other land-clearing site in the region ties the work to the Piedmont year; it reads as local knowledge, not a template.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| They'll bulldoze everything and I'll lose the big oaks | hero + process (mulch, not doze) |
| They quote by the job then charge more when it's thick | estimator tool + sample price block |
| They'll leave piles and burn holes | debris answer tool + process |
| Ruts and torn-up ground on my slope | ground reader tool + process photo |
| Will it all grow back by next summer? | regrowth calendar + guarantee token |
| Are you even in my town? | ZIP checker + area list |
| Can I trust these people? | sample reviews + years + license + family-owned |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `[[call]]` in header, hero, mobile bar, and end of every major section.
- Secondary: estimator → prefill form; ZIP yes → prefill form; service card "Price it" → prefill form.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky mobile bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT A (process-led with flagging ribbon): no orange tape, no step-by-step machine close-ups as the spine.
- NOT B (proof/tools-led with survey sheet): no density legend, no monospaced acreage grid as the dominant voice.
- NOT a bare-dirt moonscape gallery: every photo shows living land, haze, rolling hills, or finished grass.
