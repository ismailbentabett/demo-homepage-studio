# Concept — landclearing-A

## Seed
The job told as one walk across an East Texas lot: walk and flag, mark the pad, mulch the yaupon, grind stumps, grade, walk again. Orange flagging ribbon is the thread tying every section.

## Signature system
Flag ribbon motif. Each process step carries an SVG tag tied to a trunk; a ribbon line unspools between sections. Keeper-tree count in margin never drops. The ribbon appears in hero, process, services, estimator, and footer.

## Hero
Full-bleed photo (hero-A slot) with dense left-aligned scrim. Headline left-aligned, one strip of orange ribbon drawn across the corner. Proof line under h1: hours · Tyler area · Sample rating.

## Motion
- Intro: ribbon ties around first word of headline (≤ 1s).
- Scroll story (desktop ≥ 640px): plan-view lot drawing where understory dots clear step-by-step while flagged trees stay lit. Pinned sequence, height = calc(var(--n) * 55svh + 100svh).
- Phone: stacked cards with clip reveal, no pin.
