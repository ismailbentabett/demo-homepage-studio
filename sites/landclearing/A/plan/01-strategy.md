# Strategy — landclearing-A

## Buyer
Owners of 1–40 acres in East Texas (Tyler area). New-land buyers needing a house pad and driveway; inherited-land owners reclaiming overgrown pasture; horse/cattle owners clearing fence lines. Fear: losing keeper trees, hidden costs, ruts, debris piles. Trust comes from walking the land with someone who flags what stays before any machine arrives.

## Objection path
1. "They'll bulldoze everything." → Process story: walk, flag, mulch around keepers.
2. "What does it cost?" → Acreage estimator with sample ranges per density class.
3. "Will it grow back?" → Regrowth note + follow-up option.
4. "Is my ground too wet/steep?" → Ground reader tool.
5. "Can they fit through my gate?" → Access check.

## Differentiators (vs clichés)
- No yellow excavator hero; no bare-dirt moonscape.
- Orange flagging ribbon as the visual thread (process, not equipment).
- Price transparency via estimator, not a blind form.
- Photos show understory, pines, red soil — local ground.

## Tools selected
- Keep-or-clear picker (direction A signature interaction)
- Acreage estimator (sample pricing from facts.proof.prices)
- ZIP checker
- Multi-step lead form

## Tone axis
Dark-led: deep pine-shade green-black with flagging-tape orange accent. Condensed grotesk display + humanist sans body.
