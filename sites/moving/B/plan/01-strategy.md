# Strategy — moving-B

## 1. The one job of this page

A Fort Collins homeowner or renter lands here with a move date or a junk pile in mind and must get a sample number (hours or truck fraction) within 8 seconds, then call or text to hold that date.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds

Above the fold they see:
- The trade and town in the kicker: "Fort Collins movers & junk haul-away"
- A headline that names the promise: "The number before the truck rolls"
- The move sizer tool itself (three taps: home size → stairs → boxes), already showing a sample range
- The phone number and an open-now line beside it
- A small "Sample pricing" tag so they know the number is illustrative

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds

Nobody in Fort Collins has a page where the visitor builds their own load ledger before the call. The signature is the running load meter: a slim truck outline in the sticky header that fills as the visitor uses any tool on the page, and every section adds a line to a visible ledger. The contractor sees their own estimating process made public and honest.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered

| Fear | Section that answers it |
|---|---|
| "They'll quote three hours and it takes seven" | Hero tool + ledger story (shows how hours are built) |
| "They'll hold my stuff hostage for more money" | Prices section with sample ranges + guarantee token |
| "Someone will gouge the hallway or drop the dresser" | Process break photo + wrap/pad detail |
| "The junk guys will dump it in a ditch" | Where-it-goes section with donation/recycling/landfill order |
| "I'll be standing in an empty apartment at 9 p.m." | Hours/open-now + reviews naming specific towns and times |
| "How much of a truck is my pile?" | Junk-fill gauge tool |
| "Do you serve my street?" | ZIP checker |

## 5. Conversion paths

- **Primary:** Call button in header, mobile bar, and at the end of every tool output. Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile.
- **Secondary:** Tool → form hand-off. Every tool ends with "Hold this number" which scrolls to #contact with the tool's answers prefilled via data-service.
- **Tertiary:** Text-a-photo CTA in the junk section (sms: link using biz.phoneE164).

## 6. What this direction is NOT

- **Not A's inventory-tag system.** A uses numbered kraft tags and a filmstrip hero; B uses a monospaced load ledger and a tool-first hero.
- **Not A's light-led warm palette.** A is kraft paper + tag orange; B is dark-led truck-box charcoal + load-strap yellow.
- **Not A's process narrative.** A tells the journey of one household's things; B puts the estimating math on the page as a running number the visitor builds.
