Common questions

How early should I book?

Two to three weeks for local moves in peak season (June–August). Junk pickups can often be scheduled within a few days.

Do I need to empty drawers?

Light clothing can stay. Remove heavy items, books and anything that could shift or damage the drawer during transit.
