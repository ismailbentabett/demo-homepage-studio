# Strategy — moving-A (Tag & Lift Moving, Durham NC)

## 1. The one job of this page

A Durham renter or first-time buyer landing here must call or price their move within 10 seconds, confident that every piece gets tagged to its new room and nothing disappears in transit.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds

Above the fold they see: the trade ("Moving & Junk Removal"), the town (Durham), a way to act (phone + "Price it" button), and one reason to believe (the inventory-tag signature showing "No. 001 · Living Room → Living Room · wrapped, 2 pads"). The filmstrip hero shows real move-day frames (tag, wrap, ramp, truck, placed) so the process is visible before any copy.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds

The inventory tag system. Every section is a numbered tag with a room code and condition line, exactly like a household inventory sheet. The tag number in the sticky header counts up as you scroll. No competitor in Durham shows the move as a tracked chain of custody from old room to new.

## 5. Conversion paths

- **Primary:** Call [[tel]] or "Price it" [[quote]] → #contact form (multi-step: service → job details → when → contact).
- **Secondary:** Text (sms: true in facts), tool output → form hand-off with prefill.
- Phone is in the header, the mobile bar, and beside every CTA. Never more than one thumb-reach away on a phone.

## 6. What this direction is NOT

- **Not B (Fort Collins, dark-led, tool-first hero).** We do not put the estimator in the hero; our hero is the filmstrip of move-day frames. We are light-led, not dark-led.
- **Not C (Boulder, color-led, map hero).** We do not use a map or route lines. Our signature is the inventory tag, not a route.
- **Not a generic mover template.** No smiling couple holding a box, no truck lineup, no "stress-free" headline, no nine-field quote form with no number.
