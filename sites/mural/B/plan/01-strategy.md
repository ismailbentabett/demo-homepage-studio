# Strategy — mural-B (Wall Proof Mural Studio)

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner or shop owner in Washington, DC lands here with a wall in mind and must, within ten seconds, see a to-scale proof concept, a square-foot price range and a dated schedule path, then call or start the quote form to hold dates.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + city: "Murals & Custom Art · Washington, DC" in the kicker above the h1.
- The promise: "See the wall before you pay for it."
- A way to act: the wall-size estimator is visible above the fold beside the headline, with width/height inputs and a sample range readout.
- One reason to believe: a proof line under the h1 reading "Sample rating 4.8 · {{proof.years}} years · {{biz.city}}" with the Sample tag.
- Next band peeks: the top of the process proof strip shows below the hero so they know there is a path.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature system: every section is laid out like a printer's proof sheet with crop marks at the corners, a registration target and a color bar holding that section's swatches. No other mural site in the library uses print-production language as its spine. On a sales call the contractor can say "every claim has a number and a proof next to it."

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section that answers it |
|---|---|
| What if I hate it once it's on the wall? | #hero (estimator shows range), #process (three-proof story: sketch → color → approved), #guarantee |
| I have no idea what this costs | #hero (wall-size tool), #prices (sample price block with estimator link) |
| Will it fade or peel? | #finish (outdoor finish check tool + clear coat explanation) |
| Will it look like clip art / Instagram wings? | #style (style picker with six original directions), #gallery (close brushwork frames) |
| How long will someone be in my house / blocking my storefront? | #schedule (timeline counter working back from a date), #reviews (night-paint and dated-schedule notes) |
| Is this a real business or a demo? | Footer disclaimer, Sample labels everywhere, phone/hours/open-now visible |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `[[call]]` in header, mobile bar and hero; `#contact` multi-step form at page end.
- Secondary: every service card carries `[[quote <svc>]]`; the estimator result block ends with "Send me this range" which scrolls to `#contact` with the size prefilled; the timeline counter ends with "Hold my dates" → `#contact`.
- Phone is never more than one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A (Pounce Wheel): we do not use the chalk grid as the signature, we do not use sun-bleached stucco coral, we do not use a condensed sign-painter display face.
- Not C (Trap Rock): we do not use a drawn map, we do not use trap-rock brown-black or harbor night blue, we do not use an old-style serif.
- Not a gallery-first artist portfolio: the tool is the hero, not a finished mural photo; prices are shown as sample ranges through the estimator, not hidden behind "contact for pricing."
