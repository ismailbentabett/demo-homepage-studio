## Process (t-chalk)
- Kicker: THE- H2: Seven steps from blank wall to finished mural
- Lead: You approve each one before we move on. No paint until the sketch is right.
- Step 1 (A1): Measure & sketch — We measure the wall, talk it through and hand you a scaled color sketch to approve.
- Step 2 (B2): Scale & transfer — The sketch gets gridded at full size so every line lands where it should.
- Step 3 (C3): Chalk the grid — Faint chalk lines on your wall show exactly where every shape begins.
- Step 4 (D4): Block in color — Flat color goes down first so you can see the composition at full scale.
- Step 5 (E5): Detail & shade — Brushwork, shadows and highlights bring the scene to life.
- Step 6 (F6): Clear coat — A UV-resistant top coat seals exterior walls against sun and rain.
- Step 7 (G7): Walk-through — We walk the wall with you and touch up anything that isn't right.
- Caption (process-sketch): Scaled pencil sketch on paper before any wall work begins
- Caption (process-grid): Chalk grid transferred to stucco for accurate placement
- Caption (process-projector): Projector used for complex outlines on large walls
