# Concept — mural-A

## Big idea
The wall is drawn before it is painted. Every section is a grid square on the chalk layout; the homeowner sees exactly where they approve before paint touches stucco.

## Signature
The chalk grid. A faint 1-foot grid underlies the page; each section carries a coordinate (A1, B2…). As a section enters view its square "fills" with a flat color block. Coordinates repeat in the process frames and the estimator's sq-ft readout.

## Tone
Color-led: sun-bleached stucco coral (#E8C4B0) as field, jacaranda violet (#6B4C9A) and palm-shadow green (#3A5F4F) as partners, chalk white (#FDFBF7) type and lines. Warm, hard LA afternoon light.

## Type
Condensed display: Barlow Condensed 700 for step titles and headlines (sign-painter feel). Body: DM Sans 400/500 for readability. Mono accent: DM Mono 400 for coordinates, dimensions, sample tags.

## Hero
Sticky photo with scrolling text. Tall photo of a hand blocking in color on a chalk-gridded wall stays pinned (desktop); three lines scroll past: "Sketched on paper." "Gridded on your wall." "Then painted, square by square." First line visible without scroll. Mobile: stacked, no pin.

## Motion
Intro: dotted pounce line traces the h1 outline then fills (≤ 1s). Scroll story: pinned wall diagram goes blank → grid → outline → blocked color → finished detail as seven steps pass (desktop only, ≥ 640px). Phone: stacked cards with clip reveal.

## Tools
Grid preview, Wall size price range, Timeline counter, ZIP checker, multi-step quote form (wall → size → style → date → contact).
