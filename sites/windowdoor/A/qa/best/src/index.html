The schedule, in six openings

From the first tape measure to the passed inspection.

Your schedulestatus

  1. W1Measure[[i check]]
  2. W2Order[[i check]]
  3. W3Remove[[i check]]
  4. D1Set[[i check]]
  5. W4Seal[[i check]]
  6. W5Inspect[[i check]]

  1. W1Measure[[i check]]

    Three widths, three heights, the smallest wins.

    Width at the top, middle and bottom. Height left, center and right. Old Florida walls are rarely square, so we order to the smallest number and the unit sits true.

  2. W2Order[[i check]]

    A ship week when we order. A call when it ships.

    Impact units are made to your sizes, so there is a wait. You get the week the factory gives us, in writing, and a phone call the day it ships. No guessing.

  3. W3Remove[[i check]]

    One opening at a time, closed by evening.

    The old unit comes out only when the new one is on site. We clean the rough opening back to block or frame and look at it with you before anything goes in.

  4. D1Set[[i check]]

    A sill pan first, sloped to drain out.

    The pan catches what sideways rain gets past the glass and sends it outside. The new window or slider sits on it, shimmed plumb, square and level.

  5. W4Seal[[i check]]

    Sealed at the head and jambs. The sill left to weep.

    Sealant goes where water should never get in, and the sill keeps its drain path. Then trim, stucco touch-up and the old unit hauled away.

[[i sun]] Glass by wall direction

Which way does the wall face?

North wall

Little direct sun. Clear low-E glass is usually enough; spend the money on the impact rating and the frame.

East wall

Hot mornings, cooler afternoons. A mid-range solar control glass keeps breakfast rooms comfortable without darkening them.

South wall

Sun most of the day. A lower SHGC helps a lot here; a roof overhang helps too, so tell us how deep yours is.

West wall

The 3 pm room. The lowest SHGC you can live with, often on this wall only. The other walls can stay as they are.

The label on the glass lists the U-factor and the SHGC. Lower SHGC blocks more sun heat. We bring the labels to the measure visit.
