## FAQ
H2: Questions people ask before we come out.
Q1 Do you repair old wood windows, or only replace them? → We do both, and we price both. If the sash is sound, restoring it usually costs less and keeps the old glass.
Q2 Do I need approval in a historic district? → You may. Districts often review window changes. We check what yours asks for before work and prepare the window details for review.
Q3 Who handles the permit? → We prepare the permit for replacement work and are there for the inspection.
Q4 How long do impact windows take? → Often about 6 to 14 weeks from order. The manufacturer and the permit decide most of that, so we give you a date once both are known.
Q5 Can you fix just one window? → Yes. One stuck sash or one rotten sill is a normal job for us.
