Questions people ask before we come out.

Do you repair old wood windows, or only replace them?

We do both, and we price both. If the sash is sound, restoring it usually costs less and keeps the old glass.

Do I need approval in a historic district?

You may. Districts often review window changes. We check what yours asks for before work and prepare the window details for review.

Who handles the permit?

We prepare the permit for replacement work and are there for the inspection.

How long do impact windows take?

Often about 6 to 14 weeks from order. The manufacturer and the permit decide most of that, so we give you a date once both are known.

Can you fix just one window?

Yes. One stuck sash or one rotten sill is a normal job for us.
