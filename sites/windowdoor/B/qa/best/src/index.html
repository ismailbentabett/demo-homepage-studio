01 The label, translated

Five numbers. Plain words under each one.

Tap any row to peel it. This is a sample label; yours will have its own numbers, and we show you which ones your wall needs.

Impact window · product labelSample label

  1. The window held this pressure in the test, pushing in and pulling out. Your wall height and exposure set the number you need.

  2. A 9-lb 2x4 fired at 50 feet per second, then 9,000 pressure cycles. The glass may crack; the opening stays closed.

  3. How fast heat moves through the window. Lower holds your cool air in.

  4. How much sun heat gets in. Lower blocks more, which is what a west wall in July wants.

  5. How much daylight comes through. Higher is brighter, and a very low SHGC can cost you some of it.

What to ask for

  1. DP Ask for the DP printed for each opening, not one number for the whole house.
  2. Missile Ask for the impact rating on the label and the product approval behind it.
  3. U Ask for the U-factor at your window size, not the lowest one in the brochure.
  4. SHGC Ask for the SHGC on the west and south walls first.
  5. VT Ask whether the low SHGC you picked costs you daylight.
