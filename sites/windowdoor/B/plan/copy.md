Kicker: 01 · The label, translated
H2: Five numbers. Plain words under each one.
Lead: Peel any row. This is a sample label; yours will have its own numbers, and we show which ones your wall needs.

The glass may crack; the opening stays closed." · U-FACTOR 0.45 → "How fast heat moves through. Lower holds your cool air in." · SHGC 0.24 → "How much sun heat gets in.
