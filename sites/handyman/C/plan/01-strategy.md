# Strategy — handyman-C

## 1. The one job of this page
A Louisville homeowner with a list of small fixes lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds because they see their neighborhood named, their house type understood, and a clear price for a visit.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + city in the h1: "Small fixes for old Louisville houses."
- A photo of a shotgun or camelback porch (their house type).
- Phone number visible in the header.
- One primary action: "Price a visit" button.
- Proof line: "Serving Old Louisville, Germantown, Highlands and Crescent Hill" + sample rating.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The street-sign marker system: each section is a neighborhood stop with a hanging blade sign. No other handyman site in Louisville organizes by neighborhood and house type. It says "we know your street."

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "Nobody calls back for a small job." | Hero lead + services: half-day and full-day visits named with hours. |
| "They'll say 8–5 and show up at 4." | Process section: arrival window, walk-the-list, agreed order. |
| "I'll pay a trip charge and they'll do one thing." | Pricing section: half-day and full-day prices, what fits in each. |
| "They'll try something they shouldn't." | Honest limits callout: panel work, gas lines, structural go to licensed trades. |
| "My old house has quirks they won't understand." | Neighborhood stops: each names the house type and its specific fixes. |
| "Derby is coming and my porch isn't ready." | Derby-ready checklist tool at the end of the walk. |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price a visit" → #contact form (multi-step, pre-filled by service buttons).
- Secondary: phone in header and mobile bar (always one thumb-reach away on phone).
- Tool hand-off: Derby checklist → hour band → form pre-filled with "Porch repair" + notes.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT a time-card process story (that is direction A, Punchclock).
- NOT a pegboard tool wall with hour counts (that is direction B, Pegboard).
- NOT generic "we do everything" handyman copy. This is neighborhood-specific, house-type-specific, and ends at Derby.
