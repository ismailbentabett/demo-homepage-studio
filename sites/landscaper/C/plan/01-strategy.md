# Strategy — landscaper-C

1. **One job.** A Savannah homeowner lands, sees shade-garden proof for their city, and calls or texts within 8 seconds to plan a courtyard, shade bed or marsh-edge yard.
2. **Homeowner first 5s.** h1 names Savannah + live oaks + shade gardens; primary CTA "Call to plan" and secondary "Text a photo"; one proof line: "14 years in Savannah · Sample 4.9 (64 reviews)". Arch-masked hero photo of moss-draped oaks over a brick path sets place instantly.
3. **Contractor first 5s.** Every section reads as an embossed botanical plant marker (common name, Latin italic, where it thrives in Savannah, light/bloom). No other local landscaper presents their work as a curated garden index tied to specific streets and microclimates.
4. **Objections → sections.** "Plants die by August" → Plant Marker system shows light/water needs per species. "Don't know cost" → Price ranges via estimator tool with Sample tag. "Wrong plants for my lot" → Plant Picker by Light tool + marsh-edge/shade/courtyard service sections. "Crew will damage roots/irrigation" → Process story (hand-work around oak roots, drip zones). "No sense of local climate" → Season Planner + Savannah-specific photos. "Form asks budget first" → ZIP checker → quote form prefill, no budget field.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: sticky mobile bar with tel: + sms: always visible; desktop header CTA. Secondary: Plant Picker and Season Planner end with "Book this window" → quote form pre-filled with service + month. Tertiary: estimator range → "Get this price" → form pre-filled with project type.
6. **Not this direction.** NOT A's soil-profile depth gauge (process axis, Charlotte clay). NOT B's irrigation-controller zones (proof/tools axis, Austin water). NOT generic estate-lawn drone shots or "outdoor oasis" copy. This site is a place-specific botanical index, dark-led, serif, arch-framed.
