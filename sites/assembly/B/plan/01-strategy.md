# assembly-B · Strategy (Piece Count Assembly, Jersey City)

## 1. The one job
A Jersey City renter/owner with boxes in the hall (or a 65" TV against a concrete wall) lands on a phone.
In **30 s** they tap their pieces, see a sample price range and a sample time band, and either **call** or hit
"Book this list" (form prefilled). Secondary: text a photo of the wall/box label (sms:).

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in Hudson County shows the price per piece AND what goes into a poured-concrete tower wall on the first
screen. The signature is the **stencilled carton stack**: every answer is stencilled on a carton, and the stack
on the right edge grows with the pieces you tap — the owner can see the job being counted.

## 4. Objections → section
1. "Strip the particleboard / drawers won't close" → §Pieces (per-piece cards: "squared, cam locks set by hand, drawers run before we leave") + guarantee.
2. "TV falls / drill into a pipe" → §Wall checker (drywall / plaster / brick / concrete cross-sections + anchor).
3. "Show up without tools / not at all" → §How a visit goes (tools listed: hammer drill, level, stud finder, anchors) + review Ezra W. (freight elevator, on time).
4. "Costs more than the furniture" → hero counter + §Packing list (facts.proof.prices, all Sample).
5. "Building wants a COI + elevator booking" → §Building checklist tool (what we send, what you book).
6. "Leftover boxes clog the trash room" → §Haul-away band + footer flattened stack.
7. "Walk-up, 4th floor" → review Vikram R. + checklist "walk-up" option.

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: tel: in header (desktop shows the number), hero, mobile bar (left, 50% width, yellow).
- Secondary: "Book this list" → #contact form, items + wall + building prefilled (prefill.js / data-service).
- Tools → form: counter → items field; wall checker → "TV mount on concrete" service; building checklist → note field.
- SMS: "Text a photo of the wall" (sms: with body) under the wall checker and in the mobile bar (right half).
- 390 screen: phone in the sticky bottom bar at all times + hero button above the fold.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not a numbered manual with exploded views and hardware-bag tallies (A's PROCESS axis).
- Not a warm daylight floor plan / room-by-room house story (C's LOCAL axis).
- Not a printed receipt strip (cleaning-B owns that): our object is corrugated cartons and stencil, on tower-night dark.
