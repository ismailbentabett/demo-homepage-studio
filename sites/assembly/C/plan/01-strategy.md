# assembly-C — Strategy (Post & Beam Assembly Co., San Jose)

## 1. The one job
A San Jose homeowner (Eichler/ranch/townhouse, often a new parent or a 60+ owner) lands with a box in the hall or a
bookcase that rattled in the last shake. Within 8 s they must know: this crew builds AND anchors, it knows beam-and-glass
walls, and the number is right there. Action: **call** (primary) or **text a photo of the wall/box** (secondary).

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds (above the fold, 390 and 1440)
- Kicker: `{{biz.tradeLabel}} & earthquake anchoring · {{biz.city}}` (trade + town).
- h1 (Fraunces): "Built, mounted and strapped to the beam." — the Eichler line, not "quality".
- Call button `{{biz.phone}}` (redwood/white) + "Text a photo of the wall" (sms:, secondary).
- Proof line: hours today · areas · {{proof.rating}} (Sample rating) · "Anchoring from {{proof.prices.0.from}} a piece (sample)".
- Photo across the low-pitch roofline seam: sunlit beam-and-glass room, mounted TV / tall bookcase.

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds — the thing nobody in San Jose has
A homepage drawn as a **mid-century floor plan around an open atrium**: every section is a room on the plan, a corner
mini-plan lights the room you're in, and every tall thing on every room carries a drawn **strap tick**. The anchoring
promise is visible on every screen. Competitors show a stock couple on a sofa. → the signature.

## 4. Objections → section that answers
1. "They'll strip the particleboard, drawers won't close" → Kids' room / Flat-pack room: "square, doors + drawers adjusted" + review (Marguerite).
2. "TV will fall / they'll hit a pipe" → Living room: wall checker incl. glass-and-beam + Minh's Eichler review.
3. "Will it tip in a quake?" → Anchoring story (bookcase leans, strap snaps it upright) + tip-over check tool.
4. "No tools / no-show" → Visit section: "drill, level, stud finder, straps come with us"; hours + open-now line.
5. "Costs more than the furniture" → Sample price rail (facts.proof.prices) in plan "schedule" table, Sample-tagged.
6. "Is the playset ok on my slope?" → Yard room: playset sizing tool + Bao's Willow Glen review.
7. "If the strap loosens?" → Guarantee (2-year reset, from facts) in the anchoring section and footer.
8. "Do they come to my town?" → ZIP check in the site-plan (service map) section.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's numbered manual steps with exploded-view margins (no step numbers, no "bag C" tallies).
- Not B's dark tool-first hero or running receipt (no receipt, no item-tile board as hero, no yellow-on-black).
- Not a generic "handyman" grid: rooms of one house, one town, one fault line.
