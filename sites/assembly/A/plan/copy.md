## 2 Steps
- kicker: How a visit runs
- h2: Six steps, in the same order, every visit.
- lead: The manual in the box skips the parts that matter. Ours doesn't.
- 01 Count the parts on a blanket. — We open every box and count the parts on a blanket before a single screw goes in. A missing bag shows up now, not halfway up a wardrobe. · BAG 01: Boxes opened all · Parts counted every bag · Floor covered yes
- 02 Square the frame. — Before the back panel goes on, we measure corner to corner both ways. When the two numbers match, the drawers will run straight. · BAG 02: Diagonals equal · Back panel after
- 03 Set the cams by hand. — Cam locks get turned until they bite, not until they spin. The last turn into particleboard is by hand, so the holes hold. · BAG 03: Cam locks hand-tight · Stripped holes dowel + glue
- 04 Hang and adjust the doors. — Every hinge has three screws: in and out, up and down, side to side. We turn them until the gaps match from top to bottom. · BAG 04: Door gaps matched · Drawers close straight
- 05 Anchor it to a stud. — Tall dressers get strapped to a stud. Not plaster, not drywall alone: the stud. TVs go up the same way. · BAG 05: Tall pieces strapped · Anchors ours
- 06 Take every box away. — When we leave, the hallway is empty. Foam, bags and cardboard go with us. · BAG 06: Boxes out all · Foam + bags gone
