Jan

The month sets a typical unheated-garage guess. Slide it to what your thermometer says.

At 20°F the fresh-food side is already cold. The thermostat barely asks for cooling, so the freezer side drifts soft. That’s the January problem.

Cold garage, soft freezer.

The fridge thinks it’s done. On many models the thermostat sits in the fresh-food side, so the compressor rests and the freezer warms.

Many manuals list a minimum room temperature around 50–60°F. Typical; check yours.
