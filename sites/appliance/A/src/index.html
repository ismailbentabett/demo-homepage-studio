04 You notice

Fridge

“The fridge isn’t cold, but the freezer is.”

Behind the panel

Usually the evaporator fan has stopped, or the defrost system let the coils ice over.

Safe check Press the door switch and listen at the freezer vent. No fan sound? Vacuum the coils under the front grille and give it a day.

Stop and call if there’s a sheet of ice on the freezer’s back wall.

[[quote Fridge btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“There’s a block of ice on the back wall of the freezer.”

Behind the panel

Usually a failed defrost heater, defrost thermostat or defrost control.

Safe check Nothing worth doing yourself. Move the food that matters to a cooler.

Stop and call now. The fresh-food side keeps warming until the defrost works again.

[[quote Fridge btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“Water on the floor under the fridge.”

Behind the panel

Usually a clogged defrost drain or a cracked drain pan. Sometimes the ice-maker line.

Safe check Pull the kick plate and look at the pan with a flashlight.

Stop and call if the water comes from the line at the back.

[[quote Fridge btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]

Washer

“It won’t drain. It’s full of water.”

Behind the panel

Usually the drain pump is blocked (a sock, a coin) or its motor failed. Sometimes a kinked drain hose.

Safe check Look for a kink in the hose behind the machine. On a front-loader, open the small coin-trap door with towels and a pan ready.

Stop and call if you hear the pump hum but no water moves.

[[quote Washer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“It bangs and walks across the floor.”

Behind the panel

Usually a load out of balance, or worn suspension rods and shock absorbers.

Safe check Spread the load out and level the feet with a level on the lid.

Stop and call if it still bangs with a small, even load.

[[quote Washer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“A loud roar when it spins.”

Behind the panel

Usually the tub bearings.

Safe check None. Stop using it before it gets worse.

Stop and call now, and on an older washer ask us whether it’s worth doing at all.

[[quote Washer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]

Dryer

“It runs, but there’s no heat.”

Behind the panel

Usually a blown thermal fuse or an open heating element. On gas dryers, the igniter or the valve coils.

Safe check On an electric dryer, make sure both halves of the double breaker are on.

Stop and call if the breaker is fine.

[[quote Dryer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“Clothes take two cycles.”

Behind the panel

Usually a restricted vent or lint packed behind the drum.

Safe check With the dryer running, go outside: the vent hood flap should open wide. Then try the vent check below.

Stop and call if the flap barely moves.

[[quote Dryer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“It squeals or thumps when it turns.”

Behind the panel

Usually worn drum rollers, the idler pulley or the belt.

Safe check None worth doing.

Stop and call before the belt lets go.

[[quote Dryer btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]

Dishwasher

“Gray water in the bottom, and it smells.”

Behind the panel

Usually a clogged filter or sump, a kinked drain hose or a blocked air gap.

Safe check Twist out the filter under the bottom rack and rinse it in the sink.

Stop and call if water still stands after a clean filter.

[[quote Dishwasher btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“It leaks at the door.”

Behind the panel

Usually a worn door gasket, or hand-dish soap foaming over.

Safe check Use dishwasher detergent only and wipe the gasket.

Stop and call if the seal is torn or flattened.

[[quote Dishwasher btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]

Range & oven

“The oven takes forever to heat.”

Behind the panel

Usually a weak bake igniter on gas ovens, a failing bake element on electric.

Safe check Watch the igniter through the bottom slots: a dim orange glow for minutes with no flame means a weak igniter.

Stop and call if it never lights. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas company first.

[[quote {{services.4.short}} btn btn--primary btn--sm | Book a visit]]
“The burner won’t stop clicking.”

Behind the panel

Usually moisture in a spark switch after a boil-over, or the spark module.

Safe check Let a wet cooktop dry out for a few hours.

Stop and call if it keeps clicking once it’s dry.

Clothes take two cycles?

Sometimes it isn’t the dryer. It’s the vent behind it.

Every elbow in a dryer vent acts like extra feet of pipe. Long runs choke the airflow, the dryer runs hot and slow, and the thermal fuse is often the part that gives up first.

A common limit is 35 feet, minus 5 feet for each 90° elbow and 2.5 feet for each 45°. Typical numbers: your dryer’s manual has the real one.
