Before you call

What is it doing? Check one thing before you call.

Fridge: warm, frosting over or leaking

FIX

Likely causes

  • Dust packed on the condenser coils
  • A failed evaporator fan behind the freezer wall
  • Defrost parts frozen over
Safe check

One thing to try

Pull the bottom grille and vacuum the coils. Look at the back freezer wall: a sheet of frost points at defrost parts.

Stop

Stop and call when

The compressor clicks on and off every few minutes, or you hear nothing at all and the food is warming.

[[i arrow]]Send this to the tech

Washer: won’t drain, stops mid-cycle

FIX

Likely causes

  • A sock or coin in the drain pump
  • A kinked drain hose behind the machine
  • A worn drain pump or lid switch
Safe check

One thing to try

Check the hose isn’t kinked. Many front-loaders hide a pump filter behind a small door at the bottom front; towels down first.

Stop

Stop and call when

You smell burning, the breaker trips, or water is coming out from under the machine.

[[i arrow]]Send this to the tech

Dryer: runs, but the towels come out cold

FIX

Likely causes

  • A clogged vent run
  • A blown thermal fuse
  • A dead heating element or gas valve coils
Safe check

One thing to try

Go outside with the dryer running. The vent hood flap should open with a strong push of warm air.

Stop

Stop and call when

There’s a scorched smell, or lint has built up inside the cabinet around the drum.

[[i arrow]]Send this to the tech

Dishwasher: gray water, smells, cloudy glasses

FIX

Likely causes

  • A clogged filter under the bottom rack
  • A drain hose without its high loop
  • A tired drain pump
Safe check

One thing to try

Twist out the filter under the bottom rack and rinse it in the sink. It takes two minutes.

Stop

Stop and call when

Water shows up on the floor under the door, or the breaker trips mid-cycle.

[[i arrow]]Send this to the tech

Range: slow oven, clicking burners

FIX

Likely causes

  • A weak oven igniter or a dead bake element
  • Water in a spark switch after cleaning
  • A loose burner cap
Safe check

One thing to try

Clicking right after you mopped the top? Seat the caps, leave the knobs off and let it dry for a day.

Stop

Stop and call when

You smell gas: leave the knobs alone, open a window and call your gas utility first, then us.

Where the tag hides

Read us the model number. We’ll bring the likely part.

Tap a machine to see where the tag usually hides. These are typical spots; some brands move it.

Fridge · fresh-food side wall

Open the fridge door. The tag is usually on the side wall near the top, sometimes behind the crisper drawer.

Washer · door rim or back panel

Front-loaders: around the door opening. Top-loaders: under the lid. Otherwise on the back panel, top corner.

Open the door and look at the frame around the opening, usually on the left. Bring a flashlight; it’s small.
