# Strategy — general-B (Open Ledger Builders)

1. **The one job.** A Madison homeowner planning a basement, kitchen or addition lands here and clicks "Price it" or calls within 10 seconds, confident that every dollar will be shown line by line before work starts.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1: "Madison remodeling budgets, open line by line." Kicker above: "General Contracting · Madison, WI". Primary button: "Price it". Secondary link: "Call (608) 555-0123". Proof line under h1: "Mon–Fri 7:30–5:00 · Middleton, Sun Prairie, Verona · 4.8 ★ (Sample rating)". Right half of hero shows a sample ledger filling in.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The page itself is an open-book cost sheet: every section is a ledger line with a percent-of-budget column that adds to 100% by the footer. No other GC in Madison shows their fee and contingency as honest lines in public.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "It'll cost double" → Hero ledger + Budget split tool + Cost range estimator.
   - "I won't know what's happening" → Weekly report preview tool + Process story.
   - "Permits will stall us" → Permit checker tool + Permits service entry.
   - "They'll take the deposit" → Open-book promise + Sample reviews naming the final-vs-start number.
   - "Living in dust for months" → Live-in-or-move-out guide (tool) + Timeline builder.
   - "Is my basement bedroom legal?" → Egress window service + detail-egress-window photo.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Price it" button → multi-step quote form (#contact) with service prefilled. Secondary: sticky mobile bar with Call + Price it; text/SMS link in header. Tool → form hand-off: every tool ends with a "Price this with us" button that pre-selects the service and ZIP. Phone always visible in header and mobile bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's dark rail-yard track with weekly crosstie stamps (no video scrim, no week-by-week rhythm).
   - Not C's topo-map contour climb with mountain color-led palette (no illustrated map, no elevation labels).
   - Not a luxury gallery site with finished kitchens only; this site leads with numbers, tools and process photos mid-build.
