Tool · Panel check

What panel do you have?

Open the panel door and look at the label and the breakers. Pick the closest match for a straight answer.

No photo upload, nothing sent. It runs in your browser.

Which one looks like yours?

Waiting Pick the panel that looks most like yours.

Replace Stab-Lok breakers have a long record of not tripping under overload. We swap the panel, trace every circuit and label it in plain words.

Replace Zinsco breakers can fuse to the bus bar and stop tripping. Worth replacing before you add anything new.

Upgrade Fuse boxes usually mean a small, older service. Talk to us about 100 or 200 amps before a charger, heat pump or new kitchen.

Look first Split-bus panels are often original and full. We check the load and the bus before you add a big circuit.

Likely fine Keep it. If you are adding a charger or heat pump, run the load check below to see if it has room.
