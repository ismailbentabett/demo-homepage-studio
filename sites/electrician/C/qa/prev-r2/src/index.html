Common questions

Do you pull permits?

Yes. Every panel replacement, service upgrade and new circuit gets a permit and a final inspection. The permit number goes on the panel label so the next electrician knows what was done.

Will you patch the plaster?

We cut small access holes to fish new wire through plaster walls, then patch them flush. The patches are ready for paint when we leave. For larger openings from a panel swap, we recommend a plaster specialist and can refer one.

How long does a whole-house rewire take?

A room-by-room rewire in a 1920s bungalow typically takes 5–10 working days depending on size and access [[sample timeline]]. We work one room at a time so you can keep living in the rest of the house.
