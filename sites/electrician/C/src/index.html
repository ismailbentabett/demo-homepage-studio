05Living room

Two-hole outlets have three honest fixes.

A three-prong outlet on a circuit with no ground only looks safer. Here is what each fix does, and when it fits.

Run a ground wire

A ground is pulled from the panel to the outlet, and a true three-prong outlet goes in. The most complete fix, and a good fit when walls are already open for a rewire.

Fits when: you plug in computers, a fridge or anything with a three-prong cord.

GFCI protection

A GFCI outlet cuts the power in a fraction of a second if current leaks, even with no ground wire. It carries a "No equipment ground" label so the next owner knows.

Fits when: the walls are closed and you want shock protection without opening plaster.

Replace like for like

If the wiring behind it is sound, a worn two-hole outlet is swapped for a new two-hole one. No false promise of a ground that is not there.

Fits when: the outlet only feeds lamps and two-prong cords.
