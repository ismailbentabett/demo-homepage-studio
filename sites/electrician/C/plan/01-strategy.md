# Strategy — electrician-C (Bungalow Current Electric)

## 1. The one job
A Kansas City homeowner in a 1920s bungalow or Tudor lands here worried about old wiring; they must call or price a room-by-room rewire, panel update or EV charger within 10 seconds.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
Above the fold: h1 "Kansas City bungalow rewiring, room by room" inside a Tudor arch; kicker "Electrical for 1920s homes"; primary button "Price my rewire"; secondary link "Call (816) 555-0112"; proof line "31 years · Brookside to Gladstone · 5.0 Sample rating". Next band peeks 80px.

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody else in KC frames their site as a tour through a bungalow with each room as a chapter and a floor-plan marker that tracks where you are. That is the signature.

## 4. Objections → sections
- "Will this start a fire tonight?" → Hero + Living Room section (two-prong outlets explained plainly).
- "They'll cut holes in every wall." → Story section (room-by-room method, small cuts patched).
- "I don't know if my house can handle an EV charger." → Garage section + EV planner tool.
- "They'll upsell a whole panel when I don't need it." → Basement section (panel evaluation, sample ranges).
- "Nobody pulls permits." → Proof strip (permits mentioned, inspection photos).
- "What does this cost?" → Estimator tool + price block with Sample label.
- "Do you serve my street?" → ZIP checker in service area band.

## 5. Conversion paths
Primary: "Price my rewire" / "Get a number" buttons → #contact form with service prefilled. Secondary: tel: link in header, hero, mobile bar. Tool hand-off: wiring-era decoder and EV planner prefill the form's service and notes fields. Phone always visible in sticky mobile bar under 900px.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's process diagram (no one-line symbols, no teal/copper palette, no offset framed hero).
- Not B's panel schedule grid (no numbered circuit columns, no dark charcoal tone, no mosaic hero).
- Not a generic "we do everything" list (services grouped by room, not a 60-item dump; no lightning bolts, no hard-hat stock).
