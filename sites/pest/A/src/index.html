02

Mud Tubes & Crawlspace Moisture

Conducive

Mud tubes are shelter highways termites build from soil to wood. We break one open to see if it is active, then probe the wood behind it for galleries. In the crawlspace we check for standing water, wood touching soil, blocked vents, and musty air.

Checked: foundation face · piers · foundation wood · vapor barrier · standing water · vent flow

04

Foundation Wood & Attic

Seen

The foundation wood is where the house sits on its foundation. It is the first piece of wood termites reach from the soil, and the first place we probe. We also check the attic for roof rats, disturbed insulation, and plumbing penetrations.

Checked: foundation wood · band board · rim joist · roof sheathing · eave vents · pipe collars
