Station 02 of 06

Mud tubes & crawlspace

Conducive

Mud tubes are the pencil-wide tunnels termites build from soil to wood. We break one open to see if it is active, then probe the wood behind it. Under the house we look for standing water, wood touching soil and blocked vents.

Checked · block piers · vapor barrier · standing water · vent flow

Stations 03 & 04 of 06

Inside the wood, then up top

[[frame story-4 duo__photo | data-reveal="clip" data-cap="Fig. 03 · Probe on the foundation wood"]]
03Seen

Foundation wood & band board

The wood sitting on the foundation is the first wood termites reach from the soil. We tap and probe it end to end. Hollow sound or a soft spot gets photographed and written up.

[[frame story-5 duo__photo | data-reveal="clip" data-cap="Fig. 04 · Attic framing and insulation"]]
04Not seen

Attic & under the sinks

Roof leaks and slow drips feed termites too. We check the attic sheathing, pipe collars and the cabinets under every sink for damp wood and droppings.
