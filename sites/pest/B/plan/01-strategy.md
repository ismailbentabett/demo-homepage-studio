# Strategy — pest-B (Trap Count Pest Co., Baton Rouge)

1. **The one job.** A Baton Rouge homeowner lands here worried about termites or pests; they must call or book an inspection within 10 seconds by seeing sample prices, a clear liquid-vs-bait comparison, and the monitoring log that proves what they get.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** Above the fold: h1 "Termite & Pest Control in {{biz.city}}" with kicker "Every station checked, every visit logged"; primary CTA "Book an inspection" button; secondary "Compare liquid vs bait" anchor; one proof line "{{proof.rating}} stars from {{proof.reviewCount}} Sample reviews"; ZIP checker input visible without scroll.

3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The monitoring log is the differentiator: a live-looking station ring with numbered dots (BR-01 to BR-12) around a house footprint, and log rows that show date, station, activity, and action. No competitor in Baton Rouge shows the actual contract deliverable before the call.

4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "Is my house being eaten?" → Hero + termite-or-flying-ant tool.
   - "They'll scare me into a treatment I don't need." → Liquid vs bait compare tool with sample prices.
   - "What are they spraying?" → Service cards name product type and re-entry interval; label-left-with-you promise in proof.
   - "Contracts with fine print." → Retreat guarantee statement + sample log showing what retreat looks like.
   - "Flying ants or termites?" → Identifier tool above the fold.
   - "How much will this cost yearly?" → Plan cost calculator tool.
   - "Do you serve my neighborhood?" → ZIP checker in hero + service area list.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: "Book an inspection" button → #contact form (appears in hero, after tools, sticky mobile bar). Secondary: "Call {{biz.phone}}" tel link in header and mobile bar. Tool → form hand-off: each tool ends with a prefill button ("Get this in writing") that opens #contact with problem type and house type prefilled. Phone is always one thumb-reach away via sticky mobile bar on 390px.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not pest-A's inspection-station walkthrough with finding tags (A = process axis, B = proof/tools axis).
   - Not pest-C's dusk/atmospheric New Orleans story (C = local-story axis, B = numbers-and-logs).
   - Not a fear-driven bug-macro site: no red banners, no cartoon mascot, no "DANGER" copy, no safety claims.
