Layers 02 + 03 · Edge & membrane

Drip edge, then ice shield at every eave

Metal along the eave sends water into the gutter, not behind the fascia. Peel-and-stick membrane goes over it at eaves, valleys and pipes, where ice dams and wind-driven rain get in. Both are new on every job.

Drag the line: edge metal first, membrane on top.

Six nails a shingle. Class 4 if hail keeps finding you.

Layer 07 of 07 · Ridge cap & vent

Ridge vent out, soffit air in

Hot attic air leaves through the ridge; cool air comes in at the soffits. Balanced, it stops summer heat from cooking the shingles from underneath. Then the cap goes on and the photo packet is done.
