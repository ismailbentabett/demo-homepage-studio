# Strategy — roofer-C (Hailward Roofing)

1. **The one job.** A homeowner in the OKC metro who just had hail, wind or ice damage lands here and calls or texts within 8 seconds to book a free inspection; secondary path is the after-the-storm ground check tool that hands off to the quote form.
2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1: "Hail & Storm Roofing in Oklahoma City" (trade + town). Kicker above: "{{biz.city}} · {{biz.stateName}}". Primary CTA: "Call {{biz.phone}}" button. Secondary: "Check your roof from the ground" tool link. Proof line: "{{proof.years}} years serving Edmond, Norman and Moore". All above the fold at 390px.
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** The page is an almanac of central-Oklahoma weather: each section is a month band showing what that season does to a roof and what Hailward does about it. Nobody else in OKC structures their site around the local sky. Signature: the month ring that advances as you scroll.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "Storm chaser from out of state" → Hero kicker + service area map + ZIP checker section.
   - "They'll sell me a whole roof for one leak" → Repair-or-replace sorter tool + leak repair service card.
   - "Will insurance pay?" → Insurance claim timeline explainer (no promises) + Class 4 premium discount note.
   - "Nails in my driveway" → Cleanup bullet in replacement service + magnet-sweeper photo.
   - "They'll cover up rotten wood" → Deck-check bullet in replacement + process photo of bare decking.
   - "How much will this cost?" → Sample price ranges via estimator tool + price block labelled Sample.
   - "Is it an emergency?" → Storm tarping service + open-now indicator + phone always visible.
5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: sticky mobile bar with Call and Text buttons (always thumb-reachable). Secondary: ground-check tool → prefilled quote form. Tertiary: ZIP checker → if in area, show form; if not, show nearest referral message. Every CTA above #contact points to tel: or #contact.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not a layer cross-section (roofer-A): no vertical stack diagram, no build-order narrative.
   - Not an inspection report (roofer-B): no numbered findings, no mono measurement stamps, no document-as-hero.
   - Not storm-panic: no red banners, no countdown timers, no siren icons, no "CALL NOW!!!" shouting.
