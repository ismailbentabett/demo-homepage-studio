- **Typography (8.5): let the type be the report.** Today the report feel comes from mono micro-labels sprinkled everywhere. Replace it with a real document typography:
  - One mono voice only for the report furniture: the F-numbers, the sheet headers and the figures.
  - Everything else in Public Sans sentence case, including the field keys, the eyebrows (make them part of the heading line, e.g. a red F-pin plus the heading) and the timeline steps at a readable 15 px.
  - One display moment per band, set big and tight: "28–31 squares", "32.3 sq", "$12,000–$21,000".

  A text-only screenshot should read like an inspector's printed report, with numbers as the hero and labels quiet.
- **Photo (8.5): make the plates a sequence, not a set.** Every drawn plate already shares one hand. Now make the sequence tell the job, so the whole page reads as one inspection shot in order:

  - F-04 the hail square
  - F-05 the soft decking
  - F-06 the magnet in the yard
