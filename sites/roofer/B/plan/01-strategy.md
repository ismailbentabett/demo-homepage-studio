# Strategy — roofer-B (Squarecount Roofing, Fort Worth TX) · axis PROOF & TOOLS

## 1. The one job
A Fort Worth homeowner holding 2-3 bids with 3 different roof sizes (or a post-hail dented vent) lands on a phone.
In ≤ 8 s they call / text {{biz.phone}} to book a photo-report inspection; secondary in ≤ 60 s: "Count my squares"
(estimator) → sample band → quote form prefilled with squares.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody in DFW shows the *count*. The hero is a live sample report whose roof plan fills facet by facet with its
squares in mono, then totals. The same plan follows the page: each section is a finding pinned on it. Signature.

1. "Storm chaser takes my deposit and vanishes" → F-07 Local sheet (Fort Worth + 7 towns, ZIP check, {{proof.years}} yrs sample, licence sample, Sat hours).
2. "They'll sell a whole roof for one leak" → F-02 Leak findings: fix-or-watch calls; Leak repair line item first in prices.
3. "Will insurance pay / deny after tear-off?" → F-04 Claim timeline (Texas deductible note, ch. 707, no promises).
4. "Three bids, three roof sizes" → F-03 Square count: estimator + the math shown (facet × pitch × waste).
5. "Rotten wood covered up" → F-05 Photo report: every opened area photographed, numbered.
6. "Nails in the driveway" → F-06 Close-out: magnet sweep photo as the last finding before sign-off.
7. "No prices anywhere" → Line items page (proof.prices, Sample pricing, dotted leaders).
8. "Who answers after?" → Signed-off notes (reviews) + guarantee line {{proof.guarantee}} in footer sheet.
