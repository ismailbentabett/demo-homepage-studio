04Finding 4: {{services.1.name}}

How a hail claim goes in {{biz.stateName}}, step by step.

No promises about what your insurer pays. Just the order things happen in, so nobody rushes you into signing.

{{services.1.blurb}}

  1. 1

    Storm

    Hail or wind rolls through. Write down the date and photograph anything on the ground.

  2. 2

    Inspection

    We get on the roof, chalk the hits and photograph each one. You get the report first.

  3. 3

    Claim filed

    You call your insurer with the storm date. The report backs up what you tell them.

  4. 4

    Adjuster visit

    We can meet the adjuster on the roof and walk the same diagram, finding by finding.

  5. 5

    First check

    Usually the claim minus depreciation and minus your deductible. The deductible is yours to pay.

  6. 6

    Build

    Tear-off, decking photos, new roof. Anything new we find gets its own photo and line.

  7. 7

    Final invoice

    The invoice goes to your insurer with the build photos attached.

  8. 8

    Depreciation released

    The money held back is paid once the work is shown done.
