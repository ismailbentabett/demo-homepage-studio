# Strategy — selfstorage-C (Second Season Storage · Clearwater)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Pinellas homeowner or snowbird lands here with a calendar problem (boat season, storm watch, condo reno, northbound April) and must either call during office hours or hold a unit online in under 30 seconds, confident the place is local, dry and gated.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in the masthead: "Second Season · Clearwater".
- A Gulf-coast photo (boats on trailers at golden hour) that reads as Florida, not a national chain.
- Two clocks visible above the fold: office hours and gate hours, live if open.
- One reason to believe: "Family-owned since {{proof.years}} years" next to the Sample rating.
- A way to act: "Hold a space" button anchored to #contact, plus [[tel]] in the sticky mobile bar.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature tide-calendar band running across the hero and repeating through every section makes it obvious this is a locally written season guide, not a template. No competitor in Pinellas frames storage as a calendar story.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section that answers it |
|---|---|
| Mildew / salt air | #climate (t-mid) — explains what climate control holds, shows hallway photo |
| Wrong size / overpaying | #sizer (t-paper) — What Fits Sizer tool + sample price block |
| Price bait-and-switch | #prices (t-light) — sample ranges from `proof.prices`, labelled Sample, with guarantee |
| Can't get in when I need to | #hours (t-deep) — two clocks, live open-now, gate code explained |
| Safety / who else has access | #security (t-paper) — gate keypad + cameras + family-owned note |
| Storm-season timing | #storm (color band t-accent) — Aug-Oct story with shutter/generator checklist |
| HOA won't allow boat/RV | #boats (t-light) — covered + open spaces, fit-check tool, wide boat lane |
| Lease trap | #lease (t-mid) — plain-words month-to-month explainer + refund guarantee |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: multi-step quote form at #contact (size → climate/drive-up → move-in date → contact). Every CTA above points here with `data-service` prefill.
- Secondary: [[tel]] in header, mobile bar, and every section's secondary action; SMS enabled.
- Tool hand-offs: Sizer, Boat Fit Check and Season Planner all end on "Hold this size/space/date" which scrolls to #contact with answers prefilled.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's process walk-through (no numbered roll-up doors, no move-in step aisle).
- Not B's instrument panel (no dark night-blue, no mono readings, no humidity dial).
- Not a national-chain template (no drone roof shot, no "Reserve now" widget, no coupon banner, no beige-and-orange palette).
