# Strategy — selfstorage-B

## Buyer
People in West Palm Beach who need storage and fear two things: mildew in Florida humidity, and a price that jumps after move-in. They want a number before they call, and proof the air stays dry.

## Objection to answer first
"Will my stuff get musty, and will the rent double?"

## Path
Give them the readings before the pitch: size in square and cubic feet, hallway temperature and relative humidity as numbers, lease terms in plain words, monthly range from the sample price list. The page is an instrument panel; trust comes from the gauge, not the adjective.

## Tone
Dark-led. Night-blue panels with dew-cyan for readings and warm white text. One amber for the call action. Feels like a climate-control dashboard at dusk.

## Differentiators vs siblings
- A uses a light process walk-through with roll-up doors as section markers.
- C uses a color-led seasonal story with a tide calendar.
- B is the only one that leads with numeric readings, mono type for data, and a 50/50 split hero with a drawn floor outline crossing the seam. No other site in the library uses this combination.

## Tools selected
1. What-fits sizer (hero side, outputs size + sample monthly range).
2. Climate-or-drive-up chooser (five yes/no questions, outputs recommendation with reason).
3. Lease-in-plain-words explainer.
4. ZIP checker (required).
5. Two clocks display (office vs gate, live open-now).

## Conversion thesis
When the visitor sees their size, their monthly range, and the humidity reading before any form field, the quote request becomes confirmation instead of inquiry.
