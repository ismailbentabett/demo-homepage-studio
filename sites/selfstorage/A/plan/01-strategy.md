# Strategy — selfstorage-A (Wide Aisle Storage)

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner between homes in Port St. Lucie lands here worried about mildew, size and surprise prices; within ten seconds they must see a real monthly range by size, know climate control is explained plainly, and be able to hold a unit from their kitchen table without calling first.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Self storage in Port St. Lucie" in the kicker above the h1.
- Way to act: "Hold your unit" primary button that scrolls to the multi-step form.
- Reason to believe: a proof line under the h1 reading "Mon–Sat 9–6 · Gate open till 10 p.m. · 4.8 ★ (Sample rating)" with the Sample tag visible.
- Visual anchor: a bright 21:9 strip of roll-up doors at midday so they recognize the kind of place it is before reading anything else.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature system: every section is a numbered roll-up door (A-101, A-102…) with the title stencilled above it and the content revealed as the door rises. No other storage site in the Treasure Coast treats the page itself as the aisle.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section that answers it |
|---|---|
| Will my stuff get mildew? | #climate (door A-103) explains what climate control actually holds the air to. |
| I don't know what size I need. | #sizer (door A-102) asks what is going in and draws the floor plan with sample price range. |
| The price online will double later. | #prices (door A-105) shows the sample monthly ranges by size with the lease terms beside them. |
| Can I get in when I need to? | #hours (door A-107) shows two clocks: office hours and gate hours, live. |
| Is it safe / who else can get in? | #gate (door A-106) shows the keypad, camera and fenced perimeter with plain words. |
| What if the unit isn't ready? | #guarantee (inside #contact) states the first-month-free promise from facts. |
| Do I have to sign a year? | #lease (door A-108) says month-to-month in plain words. |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Hold your unit" → #contact (multi-step form: size → climate/drive-up → move-in date → contact).
- Secondary: [[tel]] in header and mobile bar; "Price it" buttons on service cards prefill the service step.
- Tool hand-off: sizer ends on "Hold this size" which scrolls to #contact with size prefilled via `data-service`.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B's instrument panel: no mono readings, no dark night-blue palette, no gauge aesthetic.
- Not C's editorial season guide: no tide calendar, no Gulf-coast color, no magazine masthead.
- Not a national chain template: no drone roof shot, no "Reserve now" third-party widget, no coupon banner.
