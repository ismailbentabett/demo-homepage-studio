## Process (#process)
- H2: How a well gets drilled here
- Step 1 title: Site it
- Step 1 body: We walk the lot, mark the spot, and check setback lines before the rig moves.
- Step 2 title: Drill it
- Step 2 body: The bit goes down through caliche and sand. Every foot is logged: depth, material, water show.
- Step 3 title: Case it
- Step 3 body: Steel casing drops in, grout seals the annulus, and a sanitary cap keeps surface water out.
- Step 4 title: Test it
- Step 4 body: We measure static level, pump for four hours, and record gallons per minute. You get the numbers in writing.
