Three passes to bare wood

Each grit takes out the scratches the last one left. By 100 the board is open and ready to take stain evenly.

  1. 36

    Strip the old finish

    The coarse pass cuts through old polyurethane, wax and the grey in the walking path, down to raw fibre.

  2. 60

    Level the field

    The medium pass erases the 36 swirl marks and evens out boards that sit a hair proud.

  3. 100

    Open the grain

    The fine pass leaves the surface smooth enough that stain soaks in the same on every board.

  4. Stain

    Colour in your own light

    We brush swatches on your floor and leave them overnight, so you choose by your windows, not a showroom.
