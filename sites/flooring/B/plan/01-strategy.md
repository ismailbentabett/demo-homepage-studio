# Strategy — flooring-B (Twelve Mil Flooring)

## 1. The one job
A homeowner in Atlanta lands, sees sample prices per sq ft and a material-compare tool above the fold, and either runs the compare or calls/texts within 10 seconds to book a sample visit.

## 2. Homeowner's first 5 seconds
- H1 kicker: "Flooring · Atlanta, GA"
- H1: "See the spec before you choose"
- Proof line: "Sample prices per sq ft · Wear layer on every quote · Slab moisture tested"
- Two CTAs: "Compare floors" (primary, scrolls to tool) and "Call {{biz.phone}}" (secondary)
- Right half: stacked sample cards showing LVP / engineered / solid / tile with mil + thickness visible

## 3. Contractor's first 5 seconds
Every floor is shown as a physical sample card with its wear-layer mil, thickness, core type, water rating and sample price — the spec sheet nobody else in Atlanta puts on their site. This becomes the signature.
