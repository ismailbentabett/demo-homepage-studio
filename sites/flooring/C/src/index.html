Heart pine restoration

Old heart pine, sanded gently

Painted over, gapped, grey at the edges. We take it back to amber in three passes, without sanding through boards that are a century thin.

  1. 01 · Strip

    Bare wood, found again

    Paint comes off by hand at the edges and with a low-grit drum in the field. We stop at bare wood, not below it.

  2. 02 · Fill

    Open seams take slivers of reclaimed heart pine from the same era, glued edge-on. No putty line shows when the light comes in low.

    Two coats of satin polyurethane. The wood keeps its own color; the finish only protects it.
