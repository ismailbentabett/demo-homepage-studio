2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1: "House Cleaning in Chattanooga, TN" with kicker "Riverfog Housekeeping · The House Almanac"; primary CTA "Book a visit" button; secondary "Sample prices" link; proof line "13 years · 4.9★ · 122 reviews · Sample".
3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** Nobody else in Chattanooga frames cleaning as a seasonal almanac tied to local weather and old-house materials; the signature season dial makes that visible instantly.
4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "A different stranger every time" → Dedicated Team section (same crew note).

   - "Old plaster / heart pine damage" → Old-House Care story with material-specific methods.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: sticky mobile bar with tel:{{biz.phone}} + Book button → #contact. Secondary: Season Planner → prefill form; Guest-Ready Countdown → prefill form. Phone always visible in header and mobile bar.
6. **What this direction is NOT.**
