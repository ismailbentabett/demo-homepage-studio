Line 03 · What gets done

What's on the list

Room by room, line by line. Deep-only items marked (D).

[[frame checklist-kitchen panel__photo]]

Kitchen

  1. Counters cleared and wiped
  2. Sink and faucet scrubbed
  3. Stovetop degreased
  4. Microwave, outside
  5. Cabinet fronts wiped
  6. Floor swept and mopped
  7. (D) Inside the microwave
  8. (D) Cabinet tops dusted
[[frame checklist-bath panel__photo]]

Bathrooms

  1. Toilet, inside and out
  2. Tub and shower scrubbed
  3. Vanity and sink wiped
  4. Mirror cleaned
  5. Floor swept and mopped
  6. (D) Grout scrubbed
  7. (D) Shower door descaled
[[frame checklist-dust panel__photo]]

Bedrooms + living

  1. Beds made
  2. Surfaces and shelves dusted
  3. Mirrors and glass cleaned
  4. Cushions straightened
  5. Trash emptied
  6. Floors vacuumed
  7. (D) Ceiling fan blades
  8. (D) Under the beds
[[frame checklist-floors panel__photo]]

Floors

  1. Hard floors swept and mopped
  2. Carpet vacuumed
  3. Rugs vacuumed both sides
  4. (D) Baseboards wiped throughout
  5. (D) Stair risers and treads
