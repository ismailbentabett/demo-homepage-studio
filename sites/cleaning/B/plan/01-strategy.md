# Strategy — cleaning-B (Every Other Friday Cleaning)

1. **The one job.** Homeowner lands, sees price + checklist before any call, builds a sample quote in <30s, sends the receipt as the form hand-off. Primary: "Price it" (quote builder). Secondary: "Call (256) 555-0153".

2. **Homeowner's first 5 seconds.** H1 "Huntsville house cleaning, priced line by line." Kicker carries trade+city. One loud copper/sienna "Price it" button. Quieter "Call" link. Proof line under h1: "Sample 4.8 · 264 reviews · Same crew every visit." Next band peeks. Receipt strip visible at hero bottom.

3. **Contractor's first 5 seconds.** Nobody in Huntsville shows the price before the visit with a line-item receipt that matches what you actually pay. Signature: the running receipt — thermal-paper strip with torn edge that accumulates lines as you scroll.

4. **Objections mapped.**
   - "Different stranger every time" → Same Crew story band + review tags naming the team.
   - "Skip baseboards and charge anyway" → Checklist explorer tool with deep-only items marked.
   - "Don't know what's included" → Quote builder prints each room/add-on as a receipt line.
   - "Quote one price, another at door" → Sample pricing table + receipt disclaimer "final after walk-through".
   - "Okay with the dog?" → Pet hair visit service card + frequency quiz asks about pets.
   - "How long does it take?" → Visit-time estimator output on receipt.
   - "What if they miss something?" → Re-clean guarantee in proof band.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: Price it → quote builder → receipt → form prefill. Secondary: Call → tel: link in header/sticky bar. Tool → form: receipt total passes beds/baths/frequency/add-ons to multi-step form via data attributes. Phone pinned in sticky mobile bar at 390.

6. **What this is NOT.**
   - NOT A's clipboard timeline (no tick marks, no room-by-room process rail).
   - NOT C's almanac/season dial (no editorial spreads, no brass circular motif).
   - NOT a sparkle/smiling-glove cliché site (no yellow gloves, no bubbles, no "spotless!").
