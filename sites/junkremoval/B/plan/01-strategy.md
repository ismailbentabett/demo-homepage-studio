# Strategy — junkremoval-B

## Buyer
Katy, TX homeowners 35-75 facing a relocation deadline, garage overload, or one big item. They fear price surprises and indiscriminate dumping. They trust visible pricing by truck share and a clear donate/recycle/landfill sort.

## Objection to answer first
"They'll quote one price and charge another, then dump everything." → Show the price range by truck share before the call, and show where each item type goes (donate / recycle / landfill) as a ruled sheet.

## Path
Tool-first: "Will you take it?" answer box in the hero gives an immediate yes/no/note. Load sizer converts items to a truck share and sample range. Where-it-goes sorter shows destinations per item. ZIP checker confirms service area. Multi-step form captures the job.

## Differentiators vs siblings
- A uses process/floor-plan signature and coastal teal palette.
- C uses garage-door panels and SoCal dusk palette.
- B uses **three-column sort sheet** signature and light-led sorting-table white + concrete grey + three destination inks (donate blue, recycle olive, landfill clay). Mono-accent type: monospace for data, grotesk for headlines.

## Tone
Light-led, factual, calm. No cartoon trucks, no starbursts, no "We haul it all!" bubble font. The page reads like a clipboard sheet filled in by a careful crew lead.
