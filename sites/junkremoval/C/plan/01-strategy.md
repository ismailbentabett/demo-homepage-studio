# Strategy — junkremoval-C

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner in Irvine with a garage full of decades or a June lease deadline lands here and calls or texts within ten seconds because they see the price logic, the quiet respect for keepsakes, and that the crew will be gone before the neighbors notice.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town: "Junk removal in Irvine" above the fold.
- A way to act: phone number and "Price it" button visible without scrolling.
- One reason to believe: a sample price range by truck share and a line about what gets donated vs recycled.
- Visual: warm late-afternoon stucco garage with the door half up, not a truck mascot.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature system — sectional garage-door panels that rise as you scroll — makes the site feel like opening a real Irvine garage, not a template. No competitor in Orange County has a page that moves like the work itself.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| Price changes after arrival | #prices (sample ranges by truck share) |
| They'll dump everything | #where-it-goes (donate / recycle / landfill sorter) |
| Walls and floors get marked | #care (guarantee token, padded carry photo) |
| They'll take what I meant to keep | #keep-pile (planner tool, blue-tape rule) |
| They won't show up when promised | #arrival (window picker, text-before-arrival note) |
| Strangers in the house | #reviews (sample reviews from Irvine towns) |
| HOA will cite the pile | #hero + #process (in-and-out-before-lunch promise) |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: call button in header + sticky mobile bar + #contact form.
- Secondary: "Price it" buttons open #contact with load size prefilled; ZIP checker hands off to form.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's process-led floor-plan clearing (no top-down drawings, no step numbers).
- Not B's tool-first sorting sheet (no three-column ledger as the hero, no mono-dominant type).
- Not a franchise look: no cartoon truck, no lime green, no bubble font, no starburst coupon.
