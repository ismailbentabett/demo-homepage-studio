# Concept — junkremoval-C

## Three rejected alternatives
1. **Truck-as-hero with price badge.** Obvious: every national franchise does it. Rejected because it reads as generic and violates brief §4.
2. **Before/after garage grid.** Template cliché: two unrelated stock photos pretending to be one job. Rejected per DEMO-RULES §5.
3. **Eco-leaf iconography with donation claims.** Greenwashing without proof. Rejected because brief §4 lists it as a competitor trap.

## Thesis
The site is the garage door itself: each section is a panel that rises as you scroll, revealing the ordered space behind the clutter, and the village name on the handle tells you this is your street, not a stock suburb.

## Signature system: garage-door panels
- Hero: shape-masked photo inside a sectional door outline; bottom panels show lit interior.
- Each content section is a panel with a horizontal rule and a village label ("Woodbridge · 40 years", "University Park · June lease").
- Panels rise via clip-path on scroll (desktop pinned story; mobile stacked reveals).
- Reviews hang on the inside face of a panel.
- Footer is the door fully up with the driveway clear.
Appears in: hero, services, process, proof, prices, contact, footer.

## Type
- Display: **Cormorant Garamond** (warm serif with good italics for village stories). Used ≤3 times in registry; unique in junkremoval niche.
- Text: **Karla** (quiet sans for labels, forms, body). Pairs well; unique pairing in library.

## Hero family
**13 — Shape-masked photo.** The photo sits inside a sectional garage-door shape, half raised. Unique in junkremoval niche; used ≤3 times in library.

## Motion
- Intro (≤1.2s): hero door rises one panel; headline fades in the lit gap. Skippable via reduced-motion.
- Scroll story 1: six village garages along a drawn street; one door opens per scroll step showing what left.
- Scroll story 2: keep-pile items sort into donate/recycle/landfill columns as you scroll.
- Entrances: text rises (opacity+translateY); images unclip (clip-path inset).
- Hover: hairline grows from left via scaleX on buttons and cards.
- Reduced motion: all panels shown open statically; no animation blocks content.

## Tools
1. **Keep-pile planner**: tickable list → walk-through sheet → estate cleanout booking.
2. **Hot tub check**: size/access/power → whole/cut-up + sample range → call.
3. **Arrival window picker**: day + morning/afternoon → two-hour window → form handoff.
4. **ZIP checker**: yes/no + honest edge-case message → form.
5. **Multi-step quote form**: what's going → where → list/photos → day → contact.

## Voice
- "We go slowly through the drawers before anything leaves."
- "The garage is empty by eleven-thirty; nobody on the street sees a pile."
- "Your grandmother's ring stays yours; we set it aside first."

Banned words: eco-friendly, guaranteed, best, award, free estimate.
