# Strategy — fireplace-C (First Cord Chimney & Stove)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Burlington homeowner who burns wood or just bought a house with a fireplace lands here, sees their season reflected back, and either calls or prices a visit within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in the header: "Chimney Sweep & Wood Stove Care · Burlington, VT".
- Hero headline names the season they are in ("September · stack the first cord") with a woodshed photo beside it.
- Proof line under the h1: hours · service area · sample rating with the Sample tag.
- Two actions visible above the fold: [[call]] and "Price a visit" (anchors to #contact).
- The heating-season calendar strip peeks in at the bottom of the hero, signalling the concept immediately.

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The heating-season calendar as a navigation spine: every section is a month, services are pinned to their best month, reviews are dated by month. Nobody in Burlington sells chimney work as a year-round rhythm; everyone else sells it as a panic.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| "Is there going to be a chimney fire?" | October (first fire) — sweep done in August means you're clear; symptom checker for smoke/smell. |
| "They'll upsell me a liner I don't need." | January (midwinter check) — camera photos from inside the flue, findings written as fine/watch/fix. |
| "They'll get soot everywhere." | September (sweep) — drop cloths, vacuum on the firebox, clean-hearth promise stated plainly. |
| "Is my stove installed right?" | July (install window) — stove/insert installs described with the stainless liner, done while the roof is dry. |
| "I don't know if I need a sweep or an inspection." | Tools section — symptom checker routes to the right visit; estimator shows sample ranges. |
| "Do they come to my town?" | Area section — ZIP checker against {{biz.zips}}, towns listed. |
| "What will it cost?" | Prices section — sample price block via estimator, labelled Sample. |
| "Are they open when I can talk?" | Header proof line + footer — {{hours|today}} open-now indicator. |

## 5. Conversion paths
- **Primary:** [[call]] in header, hero, mobile bar, and after every major section. Form (#contact) is the single quote entry.
- **Secondary:** Symptom checker → prefill form with symptom; estimator → prefill form with service; ZIP checker yes → prefill form with ZIP.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away on mobile via the sticky mobile bar.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not a process climb from hearth to cap (that is direction A).
- Not a numbered-findings inspection report (that is direction B).
- Not a cozy Christmas-stocking fireplace scene; no family sweaters, no Mary Poppins, no scare banners.
