# Strategy — fireplace-B (Draft Note Chimney & Stove)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Bend homeowner who just noticed a chimney smell, a smoke problem or a home-sale inspection request lands here and, within 10 seconds, sees that every visit ends with a numbered written note showing exactly what is OK, what to watch and what to fix; they then call or price a visit before leaving.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in the kicker: "Chimney Inspection & Stove Service · Bend, OR".
- H1 on a paper note: "Your chimney, in writing."
- A photo clipped to the note showing an inspection camera screen of flue tile.
- The phone number as the note's "questions?" line.
- Proof line under the h1: "Mon–Sat 8–5 · {{biz.areas|list}} · {{proof.rating}} (Sample rating)".

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
Nobody else in Central Oregon hands over a numbered finding note with photos from inside the flue. The signature is the **numbered finding**: every section reads like one line on that note, with a status chip (OK / WATCH / FIX), a mono finding number and a plain sentence of what it means.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Objection | Section |
|---|---|
| "Is there going to be a chimney fire?" | hero (the note shows what is safe) + sweep interval planner |
| "They'll upsell me a liner I don't need" | Finding 03 (liner) with OK/WATCH/FIX examples + sample prices |
| "They'll get soot everywhere" | Finding 02 (sweep) describing drop cloths and vacuum |
| "Is my stove installed right?" | Finding 05 (clearances) + wood stove install service |
| "I don't know if I need a sweep or an inspection" | symptom checker tool + Finding 01 explaining the difference |
| "What will it cost?" | sample price block + estimator |
| "Do you come to my town?" | ZIP checker + area list |
| "Are you open when I can talk?" | open-now + hours |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: `[[call]]` in header, hero note, mobile bar, and after every third finding.
- Secondary: "Price it" buttons (`[[quote svc]]`) on services and findings → multi-step form at `#contact`.
- Tool hand-off: symptom checker, sweep planner and firewood dry-check all prefill the form with the chosen visit type.
- Phone is always one thumb-reach away via the sticky mobile bar under 900px.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- NOT A's dark hearth-to-cap climb with the brush marker and ember glow (we are light-led paper, not soot-dark).
- NOT C's seasonal calendar with month tiles and Vermont texture (we are a single visit note, not a year-long story).
- NOT a cozy family-by-the-fire scene, a Victorian sweep cartoon, a wall of safety badges, or a red "chimney fire!" scare banner (all banned by brief §4).
