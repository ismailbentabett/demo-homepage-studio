## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The signature is the **chimney cross-section**: a drawn flue runs up the left margin, each page section is one level (firebox → damper → smoke shelf → liner → crown → cap), and a brush marker climbs as you scroll. No competitor in Missoula shows the work as a vertical journey through the chimney itself.
