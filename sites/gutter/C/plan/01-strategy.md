# gutter-C — Strategy (Firneedle Gutter Co., Portland)

1. **The one job.** A Portland homeowner under firs or a bigleaf maple, phone in hand after a wet week, lands and
   either texts a photo of the gutter or calls, within 10 s. Third path: "price my cleaning" (pre-fills the form).

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 and 1440).** Kicker "Gutter cleaning & guards · {{biz.city}}, {{biz.state}}";
   h1 "Every Portland roof drops something different." (city via token in the kicker; the h1 stays a sentence);
   primary `tel:` Call {{biz.phone}}, secondary "Text a photo" (`sms:`), text link "Sample price: cleaning $150–$425";
   proof line "4.9 · 132 reviews (Sample) · CCB SAMPLE-OR-CCB-237104 · 9 yrs". Hours "open now" chip from kit.

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Portland sells gutters by the tree. Firneedle's page is a field guide:
   each clog is a numbered specimen with its own drop-season bar. A contractor sees a 12-month bar that tells
   a homeowner *when* to book. That bar is the signature (Drop-Season Bars), and the cleaning plan sells itself.

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "They'll sell guards that don't work under my trees" → Specimen plates (each says which guard, or none) + tree sorter tool + Sara W. review (still one cleaning a year).
   - "Someone will fall off a ladder" → Visit steps (how the visit runs, plain process, no claim of insurance beyond facts) + license line.
   - "They'll blow it onto my beds and leave" → Visit step 2 "by hand, bagged, downspouts flushed" (services blurb) + Mike O. review.
   - "New gutters look cheap and sag" → Services: seamless, trim-matched; fascia replaced first; Doug F. review (new hangers).
   - "Not sure the gutters are the problem" → Downspout disconnection check tool (Portland's disconnected downspouts) + Anita S.
   - "What does it cost?" → Sample price ledger right after the plates, and in the hero.
   - "Do you come to my town?" → ZIP checker in the range-map hero + footer.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: call (hero, sticky header ≥ 720px, mobile bar). Secondary: text a photo (`sms:`).
   Tools → form: tree sorter writes the trees + suggested service into the form; disconnection check writes the
   suggested fix; cleaning calendar writes "plan: N visits/yr". On 390: a 64px bottom bar, Call (rust, 60%) + Text
   (40%), visible from first paint, hidden while #contact form is in view.

6. **What this direction is NOT.**
   - Not A's slope line / diagonal split / water drop rolling down the page (process axis, Memphis).
   - Not B's gallon numbers, rain gauge, mono numerals, centered statement + marquee (proof axis, Seattle).
   - Not a video hero, and not a water-themed page at all: C is about *what falls from trees*, drawn in ink,
     not about water. No blue anywhere.
