Plate 1 Douglas fir

Pseudotsuga menziesii

What it does

Needles fall every month and mat into a wet felt that slows the water long before it stops it.

The fix

Fine micro-mesh guards, plus one cleaning a year. Mesh helps; it doesn’t end the ladder.

Plate 2 Bigleaf maple

Acer macrophyllum

What it does

Leaves the size of a dinner plate fill a run in three weeks and plug the drop outlet first.

The fix

A cleaning booked the week after the big drop, not before it. Every downspout flushed.

Plate 3 Moss

Bryophyta

What it does

Grows on shaded north roofs all winter and washes down in clumps with every rain.

The fix

Moss brushed off the lower shingles and the run cleaned on the same visit.

Plate 4 Cottonwood

Populus trichocarpa

What it does

Seed fluff in June sticks to mesh and to wet debris, so guards can clog under it.

The fix

A summer cleaning instead of a guard. We’ll say so before you buy one.

Trees over your roof
JFMAMJJASOND

Pick the trees over your roof.

  • Douglas fir. Needles all year. Fine micro-mesh, still one cleaning a year.
  • Bigleaf maple. Big leaves October to December. Mesh or a screen; clean after the drop.
  • Oak. Leaves in fall, catkins in spring. A screen handles the leaves.
  • Cottonwood. June fluff clogs mesh. Clean instead of guarding.
  • Pine. Long needles in fall. Micro-mesh stops them.
  • North roof. Moss all winter. Brush the roof edge in early spring.

Downspout check

Many older {{biz.city}} downspouts were cut off from the sewer and now empty onto the yard. Three questions tell you which fix suits the spot.

How far from the foundation to the property line?
Which way does the ground slope?
Basement or crawl space on that side?

Answer the three questions.

Ground slopes away and there’s room: a splash block. Basement or flat ground: an extension. Short yard or a slope toward the house: ask about a drain line.

How a visit runs

Ladder up, debris down, nothing left on your beds.

  1. 01

    We look at the trees first

    Before the ladder goes up, we walk the yard and note which trees hang over which run.

  2. 02

    We clean by hand and bag it

    Gloved hands and a scoop, into bags, off the roof. No blower aimed at your garden.

  3. 03

    We flush every downspout

    Water down each outlet until it runs clear at the splash block.

    You get the photos

    A text with photos of each run and any clog we pulled, before we leave.
