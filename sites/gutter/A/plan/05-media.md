# gutter-A · Media plan

The approved library has 2 usable A frames (CURATION.md). The target was 10-16 keys, but no other gutter frame passes DEMO-RULES §5, and B/C keys are off-limits. Each photo is used once. **Every other visual is drawn SVG/CSS** (list below). An empty slot beats a wrong photo.

| Key | Section | Crop | Treatment | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hero-A2` | Hero, low side of the diagonal split | `object-position: 72% 38%` (gutter corner + downspout outlet on the right third; the gutter line runs along the slant edge) | site grade + 8% copper multiply overlay (warms the grey sky); `clip-path` slant `--slant:18%` desktop, bottom slant on mobile; unclip on intro | "A seamless run turning the corner into its outlet." |
| `hero-A1` | `.break` full-bleed band (between services and proof), height `min(72svh, 640px)` | `object-position: 68% 45%`, so the left 25% is cropped off (holiday wire trees on the far left must never show; check the 390 and 1440 screenshots) | site grade; a 3px copper `.run` crosses the band along the eave line at the roof edge (about 38% from top), with a mono mark "fall ¼ in / 10 ft" | "Two stories of brick, white downspouts at both corners, oaks overhead." |

**Problems to know:**
- hero-A1 is a Buffalo, NY house in winter (bare trees). It reads as a generic brick two-story, not a Memphis job. Keep the caption generic: no town in it and no "our job".
- hero-A2 is a white gutter, not copper or bronze, and a lot of the frame is sky. Its strength is the diagonal. Don't crop it tighter than about 60% width at 1440, or the gutter turns into a sliver.
- Only 2 photos are on the page, so the critic will see the Photo category as thin. The drawn system has to carry the visuals (below) at the same finish level.

**Drawn instead of photographed** (inline SVG, the `.run` palette, stroke 2px, no clip-art houses, no blue water drops):
1. Fittings story: 6 fitting illustrations (end cap, hanger, seam, outlet, elbow, splash block), each a technical line drawing at 160px with mono dimension marks.
2. Services: a tiny profile per card (K-style 5-in vs 6-in trough, 3x4 downspout, mesh guard section, fascia board, pop-up emitter).
3. Size check: trough cross-section that swaps 5-in to 6-in, plus the fall over the run.
4. Downspout count: the run, with drops placed along it.
5. Tree sorter: one drawn seed/leaf mark per tree (sweetgum ball, oak tassel, pine needle, maple leaf), in mono-labelled plates. Keep them small so it doesn't drift into C's field guide.
6. Footer: downspout + elbow + splash block, with the landing drop.
