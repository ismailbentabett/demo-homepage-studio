## How (Fittings)
- Kicker: How we hang a run · six fittings
- H2: From the end cap to the splash block.
- Steps: 
  1. End cap · We look first. We walk the roofline in the rain marks and find where the water actually leaves.
  2. Hanger · We measure. Every run is measured end to end and the fall is snapped in chalk on the fascia.
  3. Seam · We form it in your driveway. The machine rolls one piece per run from coil, so there's no seam to leak.
  4. Outlet · We place the drop. The outlet goes where the water collects, not where it's easy to reach.
  5. Elbow · We turn it away. The bottom elbow kicks the water out, away from the wall and the beds.
  6. Splash block · We check where it lands. We run a hose down each run and watch it land before we leave.

## Trees
- Kicker: Tree debris sorter
- H2: Which tree hangs over your roof?
- Lead: Guards are picked for the tree, not sold by the foot. Pick yours.
- Oak · Drops tassels and pollen in April, then leaves in Nov–Dec. Screen guards catch leaves; tassels need micro-mesh. Clean twice a year.
- Sweetgum · Drops spiky seed balls all winter into spring. Micro-mesh, because the balls jam screens. Clean in spring and late fall.
- Pine · Drops needles all year. Micro-mesh only; needles slide through screen. Check once a year, rinse the mesh.
- Maple · Drops seed "helicopters" in spring and leaves in fall. Screen works; mesh if seeds sprout in the gutter. Clean twice a year.
- No trees · Grit from the shingles and the odd leaf. No guard needed. One cleaning a year, or every other.
