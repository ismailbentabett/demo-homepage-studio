How we hang a run · six fittings

From the end cap to the splash block.

  1. 0 ft · fall 0 in

    End cap We look first.

    We walk the roofline, read the rain marks and find where the water actually leaves the roof.

  2. 10 ft · fall ¼ in

    Hanger We measure.

    Each run is measured end to end, and the fall is snapped in chalk on the fascia before a hanger goes in.

  3. 20 ft · fall ½ in

    Seam We form it in your driveway.

    The machine rolls one piece per run from coil, so there is no joint halfway along to leak.

  4. 30 ft · fall ¾ in

    Outlet We place the drop.

    The outlet goes where the water collects, not where the ladder is easiest to set.

  5. 40 ft · fall 1 in

    Elbow We turn it away.

    The bottom elbow kicks the water out, clear of the wall, the slab and the flower beds.

  6. 50 ft · fall 1¼ in

    Splash block We check where it lands.

    Before we leave, a hose runs down every run and we watch where the water lands.

Tree debris sorter

Which tree hangs over your roof?

Guards get picked for the tree, not sold by the foot. Pick yours and we’ll tell you what we’d put up, if anything.

Oak

What drops
Tassels and pollen in April, then leaves through November and December.
Guard
Screen stops the leaves. The tassels mat on screen, so under a big oak we fit micro-mesh.
Cleaning
Twice a year: late spring and after the leaves are down.
[[quote Guards btn--primary | Price guards for an oak]]

Sweetgum

What drops
Spiky seed balls from late fall into spring, plus star-shaped leaves in November.
Guard
Micro-mesh. The balls wedge into screen holes and sit there all winter.
Cleaning
Spring and late fall, or a rinse of the mesh once a year with guards on.
[[quote Guards btn--primary | Price guards for a sweetgum]]

Pine

What drops
Needles all year, heaviest in fall, and pollen in early spring.
Guard
Micro-mesh only. Needles slide straight through a screen and lie flat in the trough.
Cleaning
Once a year with mesh. Without guards, every season.
[[quote Guards btn--primary | Price guards for a pine]]

Maple

What drops
Seed helicopters in spring, broad leaves in fall.
Guard
Screen handles the leaves. If seeds sprout in your gutter each May, go to mesh.
Cleaning
Twice a year: after the seeds and after the leaves.
[[quote Cleaning btn--primary | Book a cleaning]]

No trees

What drops
Shingle grit and the odd leaf blown in from next door.
Guard
None. Save the money and put it into a second downspout if a run overflows.
Cleaning
Once a year, or every other year on a newer roof.
