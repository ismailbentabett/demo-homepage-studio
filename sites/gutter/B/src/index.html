The arithmetic on every estimate

1,000 sq ft × 1 in × 0.623 = 623 gallons.

That is one ordinary inch of rain on a small roof. Your gutters and downspouts have to carry every gallon of it, all winter long.

Where 623 gallons go

Follow one inch of rain to the yard.

    The roof sheds it.

    A thousand square feet of roof, one inch of rain. All of it slides to the edge and into the trough.

  2. 02

    One 2x3 drop takes it all.

    A single small downspout at the far corner. The trough backs up and the water spills over the front, right where you walk.

  3. 03

    A second drop, both 3x4.

    Split the run and widen the pipe. Each drop carries half, and the gutter drains before it can fill.
