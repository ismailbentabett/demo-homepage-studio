   "gutters undersized" on an inspection) lands from "seamless gutters seattle". In ≤ 10 s they see the sample
   per-foot range, confirm their town/ZIP, and tap Call or Text a photo. Secondary: run the gallons math → prefilled quote.

2. **Homeowner's first 5 s (390 and 1440).** Kicker "Seattle gutters · sized by the gallon" (`{{biz.city}}`);
   h1 "Every inch of rain on your roof has to go somewhere."; proof line under h1 in mono:
   "Sample · 1,000 sq ft × 1 in = 623 gal · seamless from $11–$24/ft"; buttons Call `{{biz.phone}}` (primary yellow)
   + "Price my roof" (secondary, jumps to runoff calc); inline ZIP checker under the buttons; the hero filmstrip of sample cylinders (family 9) peeks at the bottom.
   Rating line "4.9 · 247 reviews (Sample)".

3. **Contractor's first 5 s.** Nobody in Seattle prices gutters by the water: every competitor says "free
   inspection". Gallonworks shows the arithmetic (sq ft × in × 0.623) and a graduated cylinder that fills with the
   page. Signature: **Rain Cylinder Ledger** (a measuring cylinder in the margin + a mono gallon column per section).

4. **Objections → section.**
   - "Are the gutters even the problem?" → Runoff calculator + "Where the water goes" story (roof plane → downspout).
   - "Guards that don't work under my firs" → Guard compare (micro-mesh vs screen vs reverse curve), hose-test line.
   - "New gutters will look cheap / sag" → Services: seamless, 5" vs 6" sized from roof math; fascia repair first.
   - "They'll leave a mess / blow debris on beds" → Process step "flush + flow-test every downspout"; review Steve K.
   - "No idea of price" → Sample pricing ledger (facts.proof.prices) in the hero proof line and the price band.
   - "Do you come to my town?" → Hero map + ZIP checker; area list in footer.
   - "Someone falls on my property" → Insured line + licence (Sample) in trust row; no ladder photos anywhere.

5. **Conversion paths.** Primary: tel: (header right, hero, sticky mobile bar left half). Secondary: sms: "Text a
   photo of your gutter" (mobile bar right half, hero text link). Tool → form: runoff calc and size check push
   `roofSqft`, `gallons`, `size` into the #contact form (service + message prefill). On 390 the phone sits in the
   sticky bottom bar (56px, Call | Text) from first paint; header shows logo + Call icon button 48px.

6. **What this is NOT.**
   - Not A's diagonal slope line or copper pitched seam (no diagonal anything, no rolling drop).
