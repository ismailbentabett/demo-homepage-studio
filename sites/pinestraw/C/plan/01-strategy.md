# Strategy — pinestraw-C (Two Seasons Pine Straw)

## 1. The one job of this page
A Marietta homeowner lands here because the beds look tired or an HOA letter arrived; they must see that we do pine straw twice a year on their street, get a sample price range, and call or text within 10 seconds.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in the h1: "Pine straw, twice a year. Marietta."
- A kidney-shaped bed photo under a dogwood (the shape every front yard has).
- Proof line: "Mon–Sat 8–6 · Cobb County · 4.9 ★ (Sample rating)".
- Primary CTA: "Price it" → #contact. Secondary: [[tel]].

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The two-page almanac: the page is split into SPRING and FALL halves with a season ribbon that turns from dogwood white to oak-leaf rust as you scroll. No competitor in Cobb frames the work as a local calendar.

- Primary: "Price it" button → #contact form (service prefill via `data-service`).
