The two weeks that matter

In Cobb County the straw goes down twice. Once for the spring HOA walk-throughs, once before Thanksgiving company arrives.

SPRING

Dogwoods and azaleas open, HOA walk-throughs start. We spread fresh long-leaf while the blooms are out so the beds read clean for the inspection.

FALL

Oaks drop their leaves, family drives in for Thanksgiving. We clear the beds, recut the edge and put down a deep red-brown layer before company arrives.

Rake, edge, pull, spread, blow

  1. [[frame process-1 | process-step__frame]]

    Rake the old out

    Faded straw and oak leaves pulled from the beds and hauled away.

  2. [[frame process-2 | process-step__frame]]

    Cut the bed line

    A spade recuts the curve where the clay has crept into the lawn.

  3. [[frame process-3 | process-step__frame]]

    Pull the weeds

    Hand-pulled before the new straw goes down so they do not come back through.

  4. [[frame process-4 | process-step__frame]]

    Shake and tuck

    Long-leaf flakes shaken loose and tucked under the drip line, off the stems.

  5. Blow the walks

    Driveway, path and porch cleaned up before we leave.
