# Strategy — pinestraw-B

## 1. The one job of this page
A Valdosta homeowner lands here with bare red clay showing or an HOA letter in hand, sees a per-bale price and a way to count bales for their beds in under five seconds, and calls or texts before the doubt returns.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade in plain words: "Pine straw delivered and spread"
- Town: Valdosta, GA
- A sample per-bale price range visible above the fold
- The bale counter tool ready to use (bed size → bales)
- Phone number and text option within thumb reach

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The load ticket signature: every section ends with a torn manila strip carrying hand-tally marks and a running total that matches the tool output the homeowner just saw. No competitor shows arithmetic this openly.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| Thin layer, overcharged bales | Hero (price + counter), Bale counter story, Ticket proof |
| Straw on driveway / gutters | Process strip (blow-off step), Reviews |
| Gray old straw / short needles | Needle guide, Materials close-ups |
| Weeds buried, not pulled | Process strip (cleanup step), Service cards |
| No-show on deadline day | Open-now status, Annual program card, Form date step |
| Slope washout | Slope check tool, Process tuck step |
| Can't trust a number without seeing it | Load ticket signature repeats, Sample pricing label |

## 5. Conversion paths
- **Primary:** Call button in header + mobile bar; form at #contact reached from every CTA.
- **Secondary:** Bale counter → "Book this count" pre-fills form; bed sketcher → "Send this sketch" pre-fills form.
- Phone is never more than one thumb-reach away on mobile (sticky bar).

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not A's dark wet process sequence (no rain-soaked bed-line motif, no condensed verb stack).
- Not C's seasonal almanac (no spring/fall split, no dogwood-kidney mask, no local street lists).
- Not a lawn-care template with pine straw as one tile among twenty; the page is the yard ticket itself.
