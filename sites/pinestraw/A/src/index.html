The order matters

Straw laid flat on an unedged bed is in the driveway by the next storm. We do it in six steps, every time.

RAKE [[frame process-rake-out step__img]]

Old gray straw raked out to bare soil. You can't tuck new over old.

CUT [[frame process-spade-edge step__img]]

A spade-cut edge between lawn and bed so the straw locks against something solid.

PULL [[frame process-weeding step__img]]

Weeds pulled by the root, not buried. Runners dug out so they don't come back through.

[[frame break-wet-bed break__img | | loading="lazy"]]

Wet bed soil after a Gulf rain — the moment when untucked straw slides.

SHAKE [[frame process-shake-bale step__img]]

Bales shaken loose, not dumped in piles. Even coverage at three inches fresh.

TUCK [[frame process-tuck-edge step__img]]

Straw tucked down along the cut edge and under shrub drip lines. This is what holds it.

BLOW [[frame process-blow-off step__img]]

Walks, driveway and gutters blown clean. Nothing left stuck in the boxwoods.
