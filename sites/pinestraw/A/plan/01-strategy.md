# Strategy — pinestraw-A

## 1. The one job of this page
A homeowner in Mobile with gray, washed-out beds lands here and calls or texts within 10 seconds to book a pine-straw refresh that includes edging, weeding and tucking — not just a dump of bales.

## 2. The homeowner's first 5 seconds
- Trade + town in plain words: "Pine straw installation · Mobile, AL"
- A per-bale sample price range so they know it is real
- The phone number and a "Price it" button above the fold
- One reason to believe: the process order named as verbs (RAKE · CUT · PULL · TUCK) over a wet, close photo of a tucked bed edge

## 3. The contractor's first 5 seconds
The **bed line**: a single hand-cut spade edge drawn down the left margin, with each process step notched into it. No other pine-straw site in Mobile sells the *order* of the work as a visible system; every competitor shows a lawn-care grid or a parking-lot island.

## 4. Objections and where each is answered
| Fear | Section |
|---|---|
| "They'll throw down a thin layer and charge me for 80 bales." | Bale counter tool + sample price block |
| "Last guy left straw all over my driveway." | Process story (blow-off step) + review quoting neatness |
| "It'll be gray old straw full of sticks." | Needle guide card + material photo macro |
| "They won't edge or pull weeds first." | Process story steps 1–3 + service blurbs |
| "Nobody shows up when they said." | Open-now badge + form microcopy + review quoting punctuality |
| "The HOA deadline is next week." | HOA deadline planner tool |
| "Straw slides off the slope after rain." | Slope check tool + washout re-tuck service + guarantee |

## 5. Conversion paths
- Primary: "Price it" → multi-step quote form (#contact). Appears in hero, mobile bar, end of process, end of services, end of tools.
- Secondary: `tel:` link in header, mobile bar, and open-now line. Always one thumb-reach away on phones.
- Tool hand-off: bale counter, slope check and HOA planner prefill the form via `data-service`.

## 6. What this direction is NOT
- Not B (Baleyard): no tally marks, no load ticket, no paper-white palette, no slab serif.
- Not C (Two Seasons): no almanac split, no dogwood/season ribbon, no serif-led voice, no color-led green/terracotta field.
- Not a lawn-care template: pine straw is the only trade named; no mowing, no fertilizing, no generic handshake photo.
